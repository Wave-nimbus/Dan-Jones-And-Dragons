## Varu'Il

Varu'Il is a large city on the eastern coast of Djoria, the south island continent. It has a strong desert aesthetic, reflected by low buildings and a warm temperature. The city is in a state of tension due to the Zotrian tribes attacking the city on a regular basis. It is also where the Heart of The Keep appeared and began causing even more problems for the city. ### The Maelstrom 

One of the larger taverns within the town. It has a strong nautical aesthetic and serves as the home base for where Raya, the Runebreakers, and eventually the Flower Crowns organize and operate from.
