## Springoak

Springoak is a relatively large traditional medieval/fantasy town situated within a valley in the Far Reach Mountains. It lies at the crossroads of the Merchant's Pass and Guilded Lane, two of the most prominant trade routes on Orlon. It is also home to Horace Devinshire, the Wizard of the White Tower. Notable locations within the town itself include:

### Fine Rock Inn

Presumably a typical fantasy tavern where adventurers of all types can congregate, find news, and recieve jobs. Little else is known about it other than its name.

### White Tower

The wizard tower of Horace Devenshire situated in the northern part of Springoak. It is made of smooth white stone with ivy climbing up along its outside. It is over 100 ft tall with multiple floors. Most notably is a magical contraption capable of greatly enhancing divination and scrying magic.
