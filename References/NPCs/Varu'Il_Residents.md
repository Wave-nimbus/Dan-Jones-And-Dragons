# Notable Varu'Il Residents

## Raya Aliskov, Contact

A resident of Varu'Il who serves as Adokas's contact within the city. For undisclosed political reasons, Adokas and the Council of E'lythia cannot operate directly in the city. She serves as their eyes and ears and gives updates about the situation.

## Tristo, Guard

A male Half-Elf in charge of attending Varu'Il's Teleporation Circle. The Circle is inside a jail cell for an extra layer of security, so Tristo is in charge of verifying any arrivals are who they say they are.
