# Notable Silverhill Residents

## Hayden, Guard

A male Human guard who takes time off to talk with Nolan. As Silverhill is a mage city, he presumably has magical power, though this is unknown.

## Nolan, Teleportation Circle Attendant

A male Gnome who is in charge of Silverhill's Teleportation Circle. He sends people to and from the city using the Circle and a large book of runes. If he has any magic beyond using the circle it is currently unknown.
