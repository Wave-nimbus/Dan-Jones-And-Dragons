# Notable Springoak Residents

## Betty, Fine Rock Inn Owner

A female Halfling with red curly hair. She is the owner of the Fine Rock Inn. Before the campaign, Valse flirted with her and stole her silverware using Hobson's body. Hobson eventually got to apologize and repay her, settling the score.

### Horace Devonshire, The Wizard of the White Tower 

Originally from Springoak, this stereotypically blue robed wizard was in charge of ensuring the amplified Spire's scrying spells worked perfectly. He also helped erase many of Feyli's memories of the Midnight Lotus using her Music Box. He assists the Party when they search for the Second in Springoak in exchange for the Party destroying a construct he created.

## Zelden, Barkeep

A male Human who serves as the main barkeep of the Fine Rock Inn. Little else is known about them.
