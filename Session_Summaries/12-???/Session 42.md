## Session 42: Personal Puzzles

We open once again within Wellmore Keep's entrance hall after the Party has taken their Long Rest. Everyone takes a WIS save, but only Coil fails. A magical fatigue washes over everyone, as Coil's CON score (not bonus) is reduced by 1. The Party prepares to explore further into the Keep while Miory, Oro, and Otto stay in the Entrance Hall. They head back to the sauna room and use their Blue Key on the East door. The room is simple but well-furnished and blurry windows show an exterior that doesn't exist. There are three tables, each with a lockbox on it. One blue, one white, and one black. A fourth table has a small brass wind-up key and a note. The note says that at least one box always tells the truth, at least one box always lies, and the prize is in exactly one box while the others are empty. The white box says, "The statement on the blue box is true." Blue says "Two of these statements are true." Black says "The prize is in the box in the true statement." The Party mulls over the puzzle, with Feyli and Morenthal thinking it's in the black box. Gelnek agrees and Trilby's head spins. Gelnek opens the black box, revealing a small key made of bone with several dangling bits on it. He concludes that it's his key. Hobson and Trilby search the room for the letter, finding a 'B'. This confirms that the letters on the bottom two rows spell "Ebony" and "Ivory". A door to the north leads out of the room. Coil voices concern that the Keep knows a lot about the Party, and Trilby and Gelnek decide to do a deep clean of the Flower Crowns' house when they get back. The Party heads back to the sauna room to use their white key on the north door.

A small child's room is filled with stuffed animals in a corner, a pair of beds, and several drawings on the walls. The beds are themed for a boy and girl. In the center of the room is a small table with a spectral child girl who is crying and holding a key. Trilby asks what's wrong, and the girl floats over. She explains that she was going to give her mother a present in the form of a drawing, but her brother ripped it up. Her mother might get mad that she doesn't have a gift anymore. Trilby asks if they can help fix it, and the girl agrees but says that they have to hurry before her mom gets home. On cue, a crashing sound comes from the door to the east, as if something is trying to break it down. The Party proceed to fix the drawing as more banging happens. Coil goes over to push against the door and Gelnek casts Warding Bond on him. Trilby asks for the girl's name, and she says that she's $\color{gray}{\textit{Ruby-Sue}}$. Morenthal asks if anyone in her family loves her, and she says that her imaginary friend Scrubbins the clockwork bird does. Trilby stops dead and asks more about it. More of the picture is fixed, but Coil is also hit by a spectral claw from the door. More picture fixing, more claw attacks. Just before a third attack, the picture is fixed, showing several keys on it. The largest one materializes into a blue key and drops to the ground. The girl thanks the Party and dematerializes, and the door looks perfectly fine. Trilby looks around the room and notices that the drawings on the wall form a large 'M'. The only other door is to the east. Gelnek uses the Gelnek key on the door, which sounds off a loud drum beat before dissolving.

Inside is what appears to be $\color{tan}{\textbf{Chief Stompy}}$ meditating in a large tent with exotic fabrics everywhere. Stompy is burning incense and holding a cloudy orb. As the Party enters, Stompy greets them and bids them to enter. He was using his orb for divination, trying to get guidance for the Ustenki Tribe. Hobson uses Detect Magic and compares it to the inn, and he concludes it's about the same. He tells the Party and everyone gets comfortable. Stompy gives the orb to Gelnek and says it may be able to help the Party. A white cloudy texture overcomes his eyes as he touches the orb, and he reaches out to others. Feyli takes the hand first, receiving the same effect. Coil takes her hand, and the rest of the Party become a little concerned. It doesn't seem harmful, but they're still suspicious. Hobson takes Coil's hand and Valse makes a very brief appearance before also joining the vision. Trilby takes Hobson's hand, and Morenthal joins in last. The Party is split across voice chats for one-on-one time with the DM, and they will need something to write with. Gelnek goes first, appearing within a white void, but he feels fine.

Gelnek is presented with a sigil: A star divided into 6 kite sections. The top section has six diagonal blue lines going from the top left edge to the bottom right edge. Going clockwise, the second section has 5 concentric rings, all black except for the 3rd one. The third section has 8 flame motifs, all red except for two, which are blue. The fourth section has a left hand with an eye symbol on the back of it. Rings exist on the middle and pinky fingers. The fifth section has a pair of snakes facing each other, and the right one has a crown. The left snake's tail ends in an empty diamond card symbol while the right snake's tail ends in a filled spade card symbol. The final section has a vertical trident and a horizontal sword over it. The sword's hilt is on the right side. <br>
Gelnek is given 2 minutes to take notes about the sigil as best as he can. Once that is complete, Feyli appears in the white void as well. They can talk to each other, but Gelnek obviously cannot give any visuals. Gelnek must describe the sigil as best he can from his notes while Feyli takes notes. Coil joins the vision, and Feyli continues the telephone chain. They run out of time before Coil can copy down Feyli's notes, but they can take a penalty so that she can repeat her notes to him. They think about that, as Coil processes and compiles his notes into something better. They decide to continue the chain with Hobson without the penalty. Trilby gets information from Hobson next. As the chain goes on, less and less information gets passed, and so Hobson and Trilby spend some time just talking to each other. Finally, Trilby and Morenthal get to talk. However this time, Morenthal has to draw what Trilby describes using Roll 20. Because drawing takes longer, Morenthal and Trilby get 5 minutes to do this. The penalty would have reduced this time. They end with 20 extra seconds, and now other party members can communicate anything that got lost across the chain. Sections two and three got lost, so Feyli successfully gives information on Section 2. Dan J. then goes down a checklist of things that got carried all the way through the telephone chain. The Party gets several things correct, but they only got 8 out of 10 things needed. As the Party comes to, Chief Stompy is gone, and in the smoldering ashes of the campfire is a black key. Hobson and Feyli find an 'A' within the fabric above. Another door to the east exists, and they use their blue key on it. 

Inside is an art gallery with white walls and a few empty podiums. A central podium has a plaque on it, surrounded by 9 paintings. Doors lead to the north and east out of this room. Hobson is able to read the plaque with magic. It is in Celestial and says that only the sound of the correct creature will reveal their prize. Gelnek proclaims that the Ustenki could mimic several creatures as he gives a random cry. Trilby also moo'd in the background. They each take psychic damage from it before Feyli can stop them. Trilby gives another moo for more psychic damage while Hobson says they should look at the paintings. The first painting depicts an oasis in a desert with some camels walking nearby. Next is a mountain cliff and cave that has two trolls in it. Third is a sandy beach with a single small red crab. Fourth has a snowy forest and a pair of wolves walking across the snow. Fifth is a wide open plain with four centaurs stampeding across. Sixth is a dark cave lit by a sliver of light that reveals an owlbear family. Seventh is another desert with the perspective looking at several vultures circling overhead. Eighth depicts a forest of enormous redwood trees with three griffons walking beside each other. The ninth has a dark motif with gnolls eating a fresh corpse of some kind. The Party mulls over ways to mix the information. They focus on the animals and landscape in the paintings. Trilby looks around for additional information and finds the letter 'U' in the tiles on the ground. The Party observes silence, and Morenthal suggests that Trilby act like a crab, receiving psychic damage for saying crab. Trilby checks to see if the paintings can move, but they are fixed to the wall.

Morenthal takes a closer look at the first painting, trying to figure something out and reframing it in his mind. He sees that the painting has a very well-hidden label that says "Camel 1." The numbered order & animals are: Camel, Griffon, Vultures, Gnolls, Trolls, Crab, Wolves, Centaur, and Owlbear. Trilby starts counting animals. There are 3 Camels, 3 Griffons, 7 Vultures, 2 Gnolls, 2 Trolls, 1 Crab, 2 Wolves, 4 Centaurs, and 6 Owlbears. The Party continues to be stumped, and saying the animals out loud only deals damage. Dan J gives the hint of ignoring the plaques for each painting. Trilby goes to investigate the four empty podiums in the corners of the room. Hobson translates the first one, which says "Your journey begins with the Camel." The second one says "Your journey ends with the Vulture." Third says "The Camel walks into the Owlbear's den." The last one says "The Owlbear meets a grisly fate with the Gnoll." The Party thinks that it has something to do with the food chain, but that also doesn't seem correct. With the Party stumped, they call on Miory and explain the information. She thinks that there's some kind of order to it, and that it's probably a 9-letter word. The Party is still stumped and hung on the food chain idea. Miory walks up to the camels and says that she doesn't think they would be first if the solution was food chain related. Trilby takes a very close look at the camel painting one last time just as Morenthal says "Manticore." He figured out that indexing the number of animals into the animal name wasthe key. A key that resembles a dagger with a bloody cloth wrapped around it falls onto the pedestal. Morenthal is slow to pick up his key. Miori goes back to the Kua Toa brothers. The Party uses the Black key on the door to the east.

This room is notably larger, 90 ft square. The walls are made of polished marble while the floor and ceiling are pure black. A 20 ft tall pillar with a lit torch stands in the center of the room. Hobson detects that the torch is magical. Additionally, when the whole Party enters the room, the door closes behind them. Feyli wildshapes into a bird to approach the torch and the pillar, which actually melds into the torch. Initiative is rolled, but combat doesn't start yet. She tries to blow it out as a bird, but nothing happens. She doesn't even get singed by the fire. In the meantime, Morenthal is attacked by something, fails a STR save, and has his STR score reduced by 3. He swings wildly behind him, connecting with something, but no blood or damage appears. Something scrapes against Coil's armor but he doesn't take damage. Morenthal tells Feyli to cover the fire. Other attacks go after the Party members, only hitting Hobson and Trilby. Hobson loses 1 STR and Trilby loses 3 STR. Hitting 0 STR means death, and Trilby is now at 4. As Feyli tries to cover the fire, she feels more movement around her. Morenthal asks Coil to knock over the pillar. Additional attacks hit Coil, Morenthal, and both save on the STR saves with Trilby's help. Trilby notices a ray of light that hits some kind of flesh, which is visible against the marble wall before it disappears. Coil attacks the walls instead of the pillar but only hits the wall. Morenthal once again swipes wildly behind him. He makes contact before trying to knock over the column. The column is part of the floor, so it doesn't move. Feyli uncovers the light to see what happens. Coil and Trilby see that where their shadows should be on the white marble, long armed fleshy creatures are visible instead.

*Combat Begins* for real now. Morenthal, seeing how the walls work with shadows, attacks his shadow again. He feels purchase but doesn't seem to cause meaningful damage. He walks over to a different wall, and a fleshy shadow appears on the marble wall. Coil rages and attacks his flesh shadow, causing blood to spatter against the wall. Coil hits Hobson's shadow but fails to kill it. Feyli is very done with this situation, so she moves up to the wall and casts Blight on her shadow. The result is a nuclear shadow as it explodes. Morenthal agrees with that idea. Fire and Air could mean general heat. Trilby lights a torch and places the fiery part into the basin between red and white. The torch explodes, burning Trilby and Gelnek. However, smoke now swirls in a small bubble in the basin. Air and water is believed to turn into mist or fog. Hobson attempts to use Prestidigitation to make mist in that basin. It also explodes, far enough away to be harmless, but nothing remains in the basin. Thinking a bit harder, Hobson realizes that these are representing slivers between the elemental planes rather than directly being tied to the classical elements themselves.
