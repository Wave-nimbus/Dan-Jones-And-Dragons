## Session 40: Holding Up a Bargain

(*Note, Nichole missed session. Feyli will be controlled by Dan J.*)<br>
We open in the bottom of the Vaenphyres hideout as Graster walks in. Obviously, he didn't expect the Party here, so he asks if the Party is responsible for all the dead people. Trilby asks who he works with, and Graster says that he works with the Black Rose Trackers, another mercenary group that is better than the Vaenphyres, mainly doing security. He saw a corpse in the street and investigated. Since the Party has things handled down here, he'll go back up to the tavern and play defense. He waves goodbye and apologizes for scaring Miori. The black armored lady named $\color{teal}{\textbf{Cyndra}}$ walks up to the ogre, rips a key off of its necklace and begins heading south. The Party asks what it's for, and she replies that there's something of hers that needs to be disposed of. She also tells them to do whatever they want as long as they keep their eyes to themselves. Trilby mentions the Kua Toa, and Cyndra points to the likely jail cells and keeps walking. Trilby, Gelnek, and Morenthal all follow out of curiosity. This room is nicer than the rest of the base, though that's not saying much. In a corner, there's a fishtank with something in it. Plenty of loot, chests, and bookshelves are visible and open. Cyndra opens a lockbox on a shelf and opens it to find several pieces of paper. She quickly checks them all before setting them alight with a flint and steel. Trilby asks Cyndra about the cell keys, but she just shrugs and continues to watch the papers burn. Trilby goes and grabs the ogre key ring and begins opening doors to cells. 

In the north cell, there are multiple iron cages and a handful of corpses in  the room. In one cell, there is a yellow skinned Goblin holding a sparkly stone talking to himself and a human skeleton. He's malnourished, missing a part of his ear, and crooked teeth. Coil approaches, and the goblin says that it's his turn to hold the stone for the week. Coil doesn't care and just rips the door open. It happened to be unlocked. The goblin immediately runs into Hobson and uncovers a broken lockbox hidden in the wall. He tries and fails to open the lockbox. Trilby asks if he needs help, and the goblin holds it out. Trilby opens the box with a key. Inside is an evil-looking red and black dagger. The blade seems to have a hollow part at its tip. The goblin snatches it and begins to whisper how he missed it. Hobson can easily tell the dagger is magical, specifically Necromancy. Coil hesitantly says "you're welcome," and the goblin says thanks. Trilby asks what the goblin is going to do with the dagger. The goblin says that it's his tool for painting colors. He demonstrates by pointing at the wall. Trilby notices that the goblin has scars on him, but there appear to be designs in those cuts and scars and points it out. The goblin says that it's his proof of worship and asks if he can leave. Trilby asks who he worships, and the goblin counters with the same question. Trilby stammers out a non-answer, and the goblin gets super close to say that "The Harvest runs deep," and that Trilby would be welcomed. Trilby thanks him, but also declines the offer. The goblin is free to leave since the doors are open. Coil asks for a name, and he responds that his name is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Chew}}$. Hobson asks why Chew was being held, and he says that he painted colors with the Vaenphyres. He slowly begins to leave and then runs out of the base. Hobson doesn't detect anything else in the room.

The Party heads to the other cell room but takes a detour through the mess hall. There is general basic food and wine, but nothing that is obviously important. The other cell has another set of cages and a handful of bodies. There's a Half-Orc figure in one cage in very rough shape but still breathing. Gelnek notices that they are wearing a tabard of Aether the Breaker. On the far end is an actual cell door built into the wall. Gelnek approaches the half-orc, who is unconscious. He casts Cure Wounds, and the half-orc reawakens. Gelnek greets them and says that the Mountain is not done with him yet. The half-orc smiles and they exchange introductions. His name is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Davos Bloodfist}}$ He remembers breaking up a bar fight started by a Vaenphyre member, who went to get backup. And then he ended up in the cage. Davos assumes that if the Party is here, then their numbers got culled. The Party unlocks him from the cage, and Davos stretches upright. Gelnek introduces the whole Party to him. If the Party needs anything, Davos says they're welcome at his forge. He also doesn't know much other than the ogre's name and that the Kuo Toa are in the cell. He lets the Party do what they're doing. Coil looks inside the cell and sees the Kua Toa. They're both about 2 and a half feet tall. One of them has black and white scales and the other one looks more like a koi fish. They unlock the cell and debate who should enter. Gelnek enters and says that they're saved. The Kua Toa immediately wail that they're here to finish the job and sob even more. Gelnek tries to calm them down. The black and white one is still terrified, but the koi one confirms that freedom is here. The latter calms the former down enough to walk. Gelnek hands them a quarterstaff and a dagger, and the older koi brother takes the staff. Gelneks says that $\color{teal}{\textbf{June}}$ (the Order of Cabal member in the Bleeding Heat) was looking for them, but they don't know who June is, but they don't want to say more. The last time they said more they got thrown into the cell. They ask why June wants to meet them, and Morenthal says that they don't know, but if they tell the Party, they can all figure it out together. They're convinced, so the koi one says that they can say what happened to their brother, and hopefully June and the Party can help. They also ask for food. Hobson heads over to the mess hall and comes back with food.

The Kua Toa eat in the main room. The koi one introduces himself as $\color{teal}{\textbf{Oro}}$ and the black & white one as $\color{teal}{\textbf{Otto}}$ The brother $\color{teal}{\textbf{Ox}}$ needs help. Hobson asks what's wrong with Ox. Oro explains that they found a giant stone chair that gave them wishes that definitely worked. The Kua Toa took turns on the chair asking for food and shiny objects, which always came from the water above. They even asked for a bigger house and received said house. Ox then wished for more friends, which brought a bunch of people who all were dead. Ox wouldn't leave the chair and became very mean before kicking Oro and Otto out. Gelnek whispers to the Party that they found the Throne of Want. Hobson whispers back that they're concerned about bringing them back to June given this information. The problem is that they can't get their stuff back if they don't tell June. The throne won't fit in the Bag of Holding anyway, though. They aren't sure about how to move the Throne, so they'll need to ask the Council first. Regardless, they need to deal with the Throne before their stuff. As for bringing Oro and Otto back to the ocean, they consider stacking them in a suit of armor. The two of them can definitely lead the Party back to their home. Trilby also confirms that they are capable of running and hiding if things get problematic. Coil confirms that the house is deep underwater, which is a problem given how many people need to breathe. Trilby considers that he can prepare Water Breathing after a Long Rest, and they all agree on that idea. Morenthal reminds the Party that anywhere outside is not safe for the brothers, so they need to figure out where they can stash them until the Party is ready. Morenthal suggests fortifying this base and letting Trilby rest. Miori pops in to say that's a pretty bad idea given how long 8 hours is. She also points out that going for the Throne without all of their stuff is a problem.

Miori also says that talking to June is a possibility. However, given what the Kua Toa know, if June figures out what the Throne does, then there's a problem. Gelnek proposes lying to June that the Kua Toa are dead. They mull it over, since it still requires hiding the brothers. They choose to trust Graster to protect the two of them until the Party is ready. Morenthal tells Oro and Otto not to tell anyone else about the Throne. The Party then notices that Cyndra has been listening in. She offers to protect the two of them for a day for 500 gp. Coil pays 500, Trilby pays 300, and Gelnek pays 200, for a total of 2 days of protection. Hobson asks if he can hold onto something to ensure compliance. Cyndra hands over a fancy pocket square. They choose to send them to the Airship with Miori as the rest of the Party prepare for the deep. The four of them head out, planning to meet at the Airship in 2 days. The Party then explores the rest of the base, noticing a torture chamber and some storage closets. Morenthal says that they need to fake some Kua Toa bodies. So they dress up some corpses which will pass basic visual tests but fail any actual checks. For interesting objects, Trilby finds a pair of normal Healing Potions, Gelnek finds 32 gp in the bunk room, the ogre has a magical ring, and a couple of chests in the ogre's room. Inside those chests are a shattered magical longsword, a large jug with runes and multiple corks, a Supreme Healing Potion, a Potion of Greater Restoration, a brass orb covered in runes, and 2344 gp of treasure. Hobson recognizes the large jug as an $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Alchemy Jug}}$ which is capable of creating several liquids up to a certain amount daily. Hobson thinks the brass orb is some kind of puzzle device, like what was on Trilby's Bag of Many Things to start. Trilby uses Identify and only gets back that the orb is some kind of puzzle and arcane lockbox (shall now be called the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Puzzle Orb}}$). Hobson also grabs the ogre's ring, but it needs attunement. Trilby uses Identify again to figure out that it is a $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Ring of Spell Storing}}$.

Before the Party started interviewing the Kua Toa, Davos left and said that his forge is called the $\color{coral}{\textbf{Steel Heat}}$, which is owned by his family. Trilby takes the orb and divvies up the gold among the Party (390 gp each) with 4 left over for Miori. Graster opens the trapdoor to tell the Party that it's probably time to leave. Morenthal makes passable corpses of the Kuo Toa, but they aren't great. The Party leaves and confirms with Graster that everything is good. Also, the less that Graster knows about what is actually going on, the better. They all head off to the Bleeding Heart, with Hobson telling Graster to stay near the door in case the Party does something stupid. As they enter, they can see that Kurva is still at the same table as when the Party left. They also try to figure out who's the best liar, since they want to talk to June immediately. June is still sitting in the same table, and there is a second figure who is also wearing the red robes of the Order of the Cabal. That figure's face is obscured as June is writing in a journal. Morenthal greets June and lies by saying that they found the Kua Toa but that the mercenaries had already killed them. June invites him to sit, Morenthal does, and June pulls out a cylinder, causing anyone close enough to take a WIS save. Gelnek and Morenthal were close enough to be affected, and everyone else is back. The cylinder creates a Zone of Truth, compelling anyone in its range to speak the truth. Morenthal and Gelnek barely save against the Zone. June asks to hear the full story. Morenthal explains how they fought through all the mercenaries and located the bodies of the Kua Toa. June confirms they weren't revivable, and Morenthal says that the bodies were unrecognizable. June tells the other figure to order someone to check it out. The other figure obliges by casting Sending. June believes the story given the Zone. Morenthal reiterates that there wasn't anything they got information wise, and he confirms the hideout was the Last Drop. June says that she'll uphold her end of the bargain and says that her associate $\color{teal}{\textbf{Alara}}$ found the group responsible for taking the Party's stuff. Alara can show the Party to the dead drop where the items were taken. June also says that antagonizing an entire faction in the Shard Coast is a bad idea. However, she'll take their word for it. For now. She's more than a little ticked off, so Alara stands up and begins to walk downstairs. 

During that conversation, Coil would have told Kurva that things are going down. As Alara leaves, the Party follows. Gelnek greets Kurva, who is going to order more food. She also says that they're on Gelnek's tab. Gelnek thanks her for everything and gives 100 gp for everything that's been going on. He also asks where Kurva is going next, and she replies that she wants to go to Ty'Vanera in the spring. Until then, she'll wait in the Shard Coast, and Gelnek looks forward to catching up. The Party follows Alara to an old warehouse called $\color{coral}{\textbf{Goldman Shipping}}$, which Morenthal recognizes as a defunct shipping place that is used as a dead drop spot. She opens a door to the warehouse, stands to the side, and points out a pressure plate to the group. Among the several crates filled with random nautical stuff is a much nicer chest. Alara opens it up with a key and then stands aside to let the Party grab their stuff. She tells them to close it up when they're done. Morenthal quickly grabs everything and passes the gear back. Everything appears to be accounted for. Alara begins to walk away but turns back to talk to Morenthal. She says that many people in the city want him dead, including her. She steps on the pressure plate, causing several iron gates to block the entrances. Alara is outside and begins to run off after a salute. Nobody in the Party had noticed other figures in the room. Yuul Greaterfist steps into the light and just looks disappointed. He says that he thought he had trained Morenthal better than all that has happened in the Shard Coast. He's gonna hurt Morenthal, and he hopes he'll learn this time.

*Combat Begins.* (*Note: JoCat stepped away during this fight. Dan J. controls Gelnek and Feyli*) Morenthal comes in screaming straight for Yuul's chest for a massive blow. Yuul does a quick stab in retaliation before ducking past and attacking Hobson. He knows how to hurt Morenthal now. Coil takes a tiny bit of that damage, but Hobson is instantly downed by that. Yuul then immediately stabs again, giving Hobson two death fails. Gelnek uses Channel Divinity, Preserve Life to pump Hobson with 50 hp and moves up next to Yuul. Trilby summons Scrubbins in protector mode for some much needed shielding. Several Bloodletter mooks shoot at the Party, hitting a couple times. Hobson gets up and hits Yuul with a Booming Blade. Yuul immediately slathers some poison across his blades. A pair of Bloodletter assassins slash into Gelnek, Trilby, and Feyli. A Bloodletter mage casts Silence across the Party, and the other one casts Magic Missile at Morenthal. Coil enters his rage, flanks Yuul and begins to lay into him with his greatsword with Action Surge to boot. Yuul retaliates with his poisoned blade, but Coil is not only a Warforged but also succeeds the CON save against the poison. He takes very little damage. Feyli activates Archer form and swings her scythe into a flanking enemy. At the top of the round, Morenthal gets a flashback to him as a 10 year old, holding a dagger. In front of him is another boy no older than young Morenthal. Yuul stands to the side with his arms crossed and says that if Morenthal won't do it, he will. The flashback ends.

Morenthal screams at Yuul to fight himself, not the Party. He stabs Yuul, and blood gushes out of his mouth. Morenthal screams into the Silence bubble as he stabs over and over again. Morenthal can read Yuul's lips as he says that he was right to take him off that boat. Morenthal is perfect. He has the eyes of a killer. $\color{tan}{\textbf{Daemon}}$ was never making him soft over the years. Morenthal is only like this because Yuul made him like this. Yuul knows that Morenthal will never be free, and blood will always come back. Yuul's grip loosens as he dies. Morenthal is shattered after that interaction. Gelnek reaches out a hand to comfort Morenthal before he bonks an assassin on the head. Trilby gets Scrubbins to chirp more temp HP around, and Trilby stabs the other assassin. Several Bloodletters who seemingly were roped into this operation begin to flee. One manages to open a window and get out. Others who are engaged continue to fight. Hobson gets out of the Silence bubble and shoots Eldritch Blasts at the mage who cast Silence, successfully disrupting concentration. The two assassins disengage and run away as do the mages. Coil wraps an arm around Morenthal and attempts to comfort him. Feyli blasts the last Bloodletter still fighting with a Moonbeam, killing him. Morenthal is still distraught so Gelnek also goes over to comfort Morenthal. Trilby helps the last mook open the locked door, and said mook runs away, ending the fight. The Party does a group hug around Morenthal, trying to comfort him. Morenthal eventually composes himself and says they should leave. Trilby asks if they should bury Yuul, but Morenthal says that he doesn't deserve it. As they all leave, Morenthal looks back and reflects on how Yuul shaped him. The last direct tie to the Bloodletters and the one who would personally hunt him down is dead. And that's it. The Party heads back to The Bleeding Heart.

The Party, exhausted from everything that all happened, goes to their rooms. Coil goes to talk to Morenthal after a few minutes. Coil knows the answer, but he still wants to know: is Morenthal holding up okay? Morenthal has no idea how to even respond, nor does he know how he can hold himself up after everything. He's relieved that it's over. Yuul can't haunt Morenthal anymore. At least not physically. Morenthal says that Daemon and Yuul are the closest things to a father Morenthal ever had. Coil says that's depressing and confirms that Yuul taught Morenthal everything. Morenthal guesses he should be grateful for all the skills he learned, but Coil doesn't think so. Coil thinks that Morenthal should take more credit for himself. The influences of others leave their marks, but people are ultimately made up by their choices, and so Morenthal is what he is. He defeated Yuul, which Coil suspects Yuul didn't actually want. Morenthal reflects that if Yuul had caught him before he knew the Party, he would've traded the Party and the Throne for his life. He's also shocked that Yuul refused to fight him, instead attacking the Party. Coil agrees that it was a low blow, but it is a testament to Morenthal's compassion. Morenthal says Yuul always used it against him, claiming it made him soft and that it would be his downfall. Coil understands and says that he's proud of Morenthal. Tears are shed. Everyone takes an uneventful Long Rest overnight. Other than the WIS save, which causes Trilby, Hobson, Coil, Gelnek, and Morenthal lose another max HP. Coil remarks that he's down a big chunk of max HP due to previous fights. The Party has two whole days in the Shard Coast before Cyndra comes back with Otto and Oro. Trilby swaps spells around to get Skywright and Water Breathing prepared. Feyli also has Water Breathing prepared, just in case.

No major consequences are heard regarding the Vaephyres' base getting slaughtered. However, it is unknown if the Order of Cabal figured out Morenthal's corpse trickery. Coil goes to check into Leaf's store, seeing that it is still locked and there is no security there. The first day he checks, it appears undisturbed. He asks Morenthal for a little break-in help. They break in at sundown. Coil starts looking across shelves for more Voedian artifacts. Most things are not as good as Coil's construction, but there are some pretty intact things. He finds Leaf's journal regarding his research of Voedian. Inside that journal is a map with pins where other Voedian vaults have been discovered. They are found in $\color{tan}{\textbf{Maple}}$ (the one Estevan goes to), $\color{tan}{\textbf{The Desert of Thorns}}$, $\color{brown}{\textbf{Ernaand}}$, and the Crimson Vale. There are also unconfirmed sources elsewhere. Further, some wealthy folks in Brightcoin use the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Vaults of Eversteel}}$ to store their artifacts. Morenthal checks to see if there's anything of interest to him, and chooses to pick up part of a broken sword. Nobody else does anything specific. Graster comes into The Bleeding Heart to catch up with the Party. He reiterates that he's with the Black Rose Trackers trying to make an honest living. Morenthal tries to leave a note behind for Zaris where only she knows, as he can't meet her directly. He says that he's sorry for what happened. A couple of days later, the Party go meet up with the Viradescent Javelin. Miori, Cyndra, Otto, and Oro ride up a couple hours later. Miori says that their time was uneventful, but the Party says they'll catch her up later. Cyndra says that the two Kua Toas are safe and nobody came into contact. Their business is concluded, but Coil asks where they could ask her for more work if needed. Cyndra says that if they can find the $\color{cyan}{\textbf{Union of the Suns}}$, that group can contact Cyndra. Hobson recognizes them as a more elite group of mercenaries who tend to be loners. They don't really answer to anyone, and they're very decentralized. Coil recalls that Blade Goodwill was employed by the Union before he was employed by $\color{tan}{\textbf{Mr. Wick}}$.

Hobson confirms that Oro and Otto are fine. They got to eat plenty of food, but they got told to shut up a bunch of times, especially when they tried to play with a rat. The Party plans to go deal with the Throne now, but it's a long way into the ocean, so they'll need a boat. The Viradescent Javelin can't go into the water, but they can fly out over the sea, so they take it out. Oro and Otto direct the ship to fly over an hour out into the Sanguine Bay before Oro thinks they've gone far enough. Azru brings the ship down as close to the water as possible. Coil gets Miori to cast Greater Restoration to clear most of the max HP debuff on Coil. Trilby then prepares to cast Water Breathing on everyone. It takes the form of a series of medallions with duck heads emblazoned on them. They hang around a person's collars. Miori decides to join for this adventure. Everyone dives in. The descent is a long swim, other than Coil, who just sinks. Eventually, they reach a massive reef about 200 ft underwater. Coil hits the bottom and follows on foot as Oro and Otto continue to direct the Party. In the distance is a blue fiery glow as they approach a massive mansion underwater. Hobson and Morenthal realize that this is the mansion that was called missing by the newspaper: $\color{tan}{\textbf{Wellmore Keep}}$. Oro says that they need to get to the doors quickly, because the mansion doesn't like their presence. As the Kua Toa brothers approach, several stone statues around the outside animate and prepare to attack the brothers. The Party attempts to rush the statues. Hobson and Trilby flop around like fish out of water while Morenthal and Coil easily rush them. Gelnek succeeds, Feyli fails. The total for the group check is a success, however. They manage to push the doors open and slip inside. The interior of the manor is dry, with some kind of magic repelling the ocean behind the doors. The doors close after everyone enters, and the statues outside reset. The entrance hall of the manor has black and white marble on the floor, and a small table with a flower vase stands in the center. Water flows from the ceiling and dissipates along the wall trim along the floor. On the table is a key ring with three keys, one red, one blue, and one white. Three locked doors greet the Party. End of Session.
