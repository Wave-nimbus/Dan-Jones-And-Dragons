## Session 43, A Test of Creativity

*Content Warning: Forced, unconsensual transformation of characters' races. This may cause feelings of dysphoria; Sexual themes.*

Today's adventures in Wellmore Keep begins as they leave the alleyway from Morenthal's room. They use the West door from this room (4th row, 3rd column), opening it with their green key. A ruined temple greets them, having been overgrown by nature. Small animals scatter, and moonline shines down on an empty basin held by a statue of a woman. Trilby examines the statue and basin, and he sees a dark red-brown stain in the basin. The statue is not quite human, as her skin has a wood-like texture and her hair transforms into branches and leaves. He recognizes this as $\color{tan}{\textbf{Vierna, The Evertree}}$, goddess of nature, the harvest, and plants. Feyli, being a Damphir, licks the stain to check if it's blood. It's not. Trilby searches the temple for clues on the puzzle, and he eventually uncovers humanoid bones under a tangle of vines. They guess that they need to smash up some berries and mix them into the basin. Feyli pulls out her mortar and pestle as Gelnek proceeds to mash up berries. He pours it into the basin, and it magically overflows, with small waterfalls pouring over the side. Eventually, the basin drains, leaving behind a second Hobson key. A door to the North is available, and Hobson notices an "L" carved into the statue. They use their white key on the door.

A mage's study greets them, with tomes, spell scrolls, and floating candles galore. Magical brooms and a self-stirring cauldron are visible inside. In the center of the room is an octahedral (8-faced object) hovering and slowly spinning. Each side has a unique & intricate pattern. Hobson determines that each pattern on the octahedron represents a school of magic. Trilby touches the object and takes a mix of magical damage. The Party guesses that they need to use spells from each school of magic, so Hobson uses Prestidigitation on it. The symbol for Transmutation disappears, and a golden key can be seen inside. Feyli goes to pick it up, but the strange magical barrier is still there. In retaliation, she casts Ice Knife, forgetting about the small blast of ice when it hits. Hobson and Trilby take some splash damage as the Conjuration rune disappears. Hobson uses Eldritch Blast for Evocation on it. Trilby casts Blade Ward, but since it only affects himself, it doesn't do anything. Trilby starts a ritual cast of Identify for Divination magic. Gelnek casts Gentle Repose on the octahedron. Feyli and Morenthal examine the spell scrolls on the table. There are a wide variety of spells available, though none can be taken outside this room. Feyli picks up an Animate Dead scroll and casts it on the octahedron, clearing the Necromancy symbol. Identify finishes, removing the Divination face. With no more spells in their arsenal that can help, the Party turns to the spell scrolls once again. Trilby determines which kinds of spells are both easy to cast and among the three schools remaining: Illusion, Enchantment, and Abjuration. Trilby picks up Color Spray to clear Illusion. Coil attempts to cast with Protection from Evil and Good, successfully clearing the Abjuration rune. With enough prodding, Morenthal attempts to use a Hold Person spell, but he fails. The scroll burns up in his hands. Morenthal tries again with a Sleep spell, but fails miserably, backfiring upon the Party to no effect. Gelnek realizes that he has Command for the Enchantment rune, and he casts it. The final sigil disappears and the gold key drops to the floor. Coil sees that the cauldron has a "Z" carved into it. Doors to the East and West are available. Hobson use his key on the West door.

A birch forest goes on from horizon to horizon. A small cottage is visible in the distance, and unnaturally vivid colors reflect the colors of the plants. Hobson doesn't recognizes it, but Valse pipes up and says that this is his getaway home, and Valse walks Hobson forward. Hobson tells the Party that this is probably a Valse room. While not the actual Feywild, this does resemble Valse's getaway spot from the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Unseelie Court}}$. Valse says that they should head in, but they should watch their step. The Party steps into a ring of mushrooms, which sends up a cloud of spores that makes everyone, including Coil, begin to cough. The Party feels different. Hobson feels sluggish, and the tall grass doesn't feel as tall anymore. Morenthal feels slow and lethargic, seeing that his skin is tanned. Feyli shrinks considerably and no longer feels the desire for blood. Trilby barely changes, and he feels a little more elegant. Gelnek has changed dramatically as a tail -- his tail --- wraps around his legs. His skull helmet doesn't fit. Coil feels his armor change into scales, and the sensation of breathing is new to him. The Party looks at each other in disbelief. Coil is a Dragonborn, Gelnek is a Tiefling, Hobson is a Half-Orc, Trilby is a High Elf, Morenthal is a Dwarf, and Feyli is a Halfling. Valse explains that the spores can be washed off, and the effect will dissipate in an hour. Some of the Party takes it in stride and others are devastated. Trilby gives Feyli uppies, and she's very mad about it. Hobson asks if there are any more traps between them and the house. Valse says there shouldn't be, but they shouldn't wander in the forest. Inside the house, a quaint wooden cottage greets them. A basic living area blends into a kitchen area. Off of this room is a bathroom and a bedroom. The bathroom has a bathtub, but it is empty. In the corner of the living area is a large mirror with a cloth over it. Six statues sit on the table in the kitchen, resembling the Party as they originally appeared. Trilby and Hobson ask Valse about the statues, but Valse says he never had them before. Hobson's Detect Magic says there's a large illusion over the area, and the cloth of some kind of enchantment. Hobson uncovers the mirror and sees himself in his normal Halfling state. Trilby sees his human self. Valse is surprised, saying that it's broken to his eyes. Trilby picks up and examines the statue of himself. The statues have small indents and slots. He brings the statue up to the mirror, and he sees human Trilby holding the statue, which seems to have mildly pointed ears. Coil is processing the feeling of having skin, and Feyli realizes that Coil can feel hunger. She gives him some bread, as he tears off his muzzle, enjoying the feeling of opening and closing his mouth. He takes the bread and eats it, getting used to the sensation of swallowing.

Morenthal, meanwhile, is tearing the bathroom apart trying to find a source of water. Valse says that there should be a tap, but there isn't. Gelnek asks Valse about everything that's changed about the house. Valse mentions the statues and the lack of a tap in the bathroom come to mind. Trilby notices that the statues look unchanged in his hand but look different in the mirror. Coil's difference is more pronounced, as going between a Warforged and Dragonborn is notable. Similarly, Hobson is dramatically different. They think about what the puzzle could be, and Gelnek proposes putting them in order of height. Coil tastes his statue as Trilby gives Coil his Ring Pop instead. From shortest to tallest in their original form, the order is Hobson, Gelnek, Morenthal, Feyli, Trilby, and Coil. However, they realize that this was the order they were originally in. Morenthal continues to lament how wrong everything is. They now line up to figure out their height in their current forms. The shortest to tallest order is now Feyli, Morenthal, Gelnek, Trilby, Hobson, and Coil. The cabinet with the slots and statues opens and reveals a red key. The bath also now has a faucet. Everyone taking turns will take about an hour. During this, Gelnek realizes he can play his drum with his new Tiefling tail. Everyone is happy to take a bath, though Trilby is having a good time being a High Elf. Hobson asks if Valse wants to see anything else, and Valse proceeds to lead him into the bedroom and reminisces of all the times he had in there. Hobson tells him to keep those memories to himself. Valse is looking forward to coming back one day, and Hobson says that he did well for himself making this house. They also find the letter "A" in the room on one of the cabinets. Doors to the North and South are visible outside of Valse's house. As everyone transforms back, Gelnek gives them a pep talk, using the Inspiring Leader feat to give everyone temporary HP. Hobson wants to see the Feywild but is worried about getting instantly cursed or arrested due to the association with Valse. When Coil transforms back, the leather muzzle is not attached, but the locking mechanism returns and keeps his jaws locked tight. Gelnek and Hobson are gonna miss being tall. Trilby declares they should head South, and they use a Gold Key.

Inside is a large indoor pool with sharp spikes on the walls, but there is room to walk around the perimeter of the pool. There are four platforms hovering just above the water level in the pool. The only door in or out is the one they entered. The pool is about 30 ft deep, but Hobson doesn't detect anything magical about the water. As they walk around, Hobson sees that there's a glint of a key at the bottom of the pool. Trilby attempts to use Mage Hand to bring the key up, but Mage Hand isn't strong enough to lift it, though it could be the weight of the key or if it's stuck. There aren't stairs or a ladder into the pool except for on the platforms' pillars. They have several magical options plus Coil's lack of breathing, but Feyli just Wildshapes into a crocodile and dives in. The rest of the Party sees a dark shadow dart towards her. *Combat begins.* Morenthal whips out his shortbow and shoots an arrow, which misses. Hobson hits it once with an Eldritch Blast and calls to Feyli to look out. She's able to notice the blur, and Gelnek delays his spot in initiative to be after her. The strange creature summons tendrils of pure water to attack the Party. Hobson and Trilby are hit by the tentacles, and one manages to pull Trilby into the water and grapples him. The creature then attacks crocodile Feyli, quickly knocking her out of Wildshape. Trilby fails to escape the grapple, but Coil manages to slash the tendril and dissipate it. Feyli swims to the surface and misses a Guiding Bolt at one of the tendrils, and she enters Archer Form to hit it instead. Gelnek creates a Guardian of Faith in the shape of a Crocodile in the pool, which easily kills one of the tendrils when it spawns. Morenthal kills the last tendril easily as Hobson fails to hit the creature with Eldritch Blasts. The creature spawns a tendril to attack Morenthal before attacking Feyli several times over. Trilby gets uppies from Coil to get out of the pool before casting Vortex Warp on the creature. It fails and is teleported out of the pool next to the entire Party to start flopping around. Trilby also uses his Trilbee on Feyli. Coil somehow has trouble hitting the flopping creature with most of his attacks, and Feyli proceeds to shoot several bolts of radiant energy into it. She also decides to keep swimming. The final tentacle is destroyed by the Guardian, and Gelnek smacks the creature with his warhammer twice. Morenthal gets a massive hit on the creature, killing it, and ending the fight. The water in the pool drains, and the key can be retrieved. It appears to be another Gelnek key. A letter "O" can be found on the ceiling.

The Party uses their red key on the East door from the room with a "Z" in it. Trilby further heals Feyli with a bonbon. The door opens into a dark forest with a path that leads to a hunter's lodge. A torch next to a door in the East of the room is visible. This door is the door to the lodge. A metal wire cage near the lodge has another Trilby key in it. Ten chains lead from the cage to set bear traps around the forest. Trilby examines one of the bear traps, and he notices that a word is carved into the pressure plate: "Dare." A different trap says "Haire" Inspecting them all shows: "Haire", "Pair", "Swear", "Chair", "Dare", "Square", "Were", "Bare", "Prayer", and "Staire". Trilby cuts off a lock of hair and drops it on to the "Haire" trap. It snaps closed, and the chain disintegrates into golden particles. They figure out that they need homonyms to activate the traps. Feyli stares at the one that says "Staire," but nothing happens. She brings Pocket back out of her bag and tells her to stare at the trap. Trilby takes out two of the fake Potions of Greater Restoration and uses Mage Hand to place the pair of potions on the "Pair" trap. The potions are unharmed and the trap is gone, so he takes them back. Several Party members try to cuss out the "Swear" trap, but nothing happens. Coil, however, writes a swear word on a piece of paper from Trilby and solves the "Swear" trap. Feyli dares Morenthal to touch the "Dare" trap. Morenthal goes over and activates the trap, and it thankfully dissipates before it would deal damage. Hobson drops his tinder box onto the "Square" one, solving that one. Pocket blinks and Feyli says that Pocket can stop staring now. She goes back into the bag with a treat. Trilby gathers his courage and goes to sit on the "Chair" trap, but Coil decides to protect the boy. Coil sits on the trap, solving it and taking zero damage for his trouble. Feyli motions to the Party to turn around and sends Pocket to distract them. While distracted, she strips and activates the "Bare" trap. She gets dressed again right afterwards. Morenthal muses on how to activate the "Were" trap, but Hobson realizes what he can do. He uses Summon Valse in Werewolf form and gets Valse to step on the "Were" trap, solving that one. Valse then goes to climb a tree. Feyli asks if Gelnek has a prayer for the final "Prayer" trap. He prays to Aether the Breaker, and while he feels warmth, nothing happens. Hobson writes the prayer that Gelnek said and completes the "Prayer" trap. The last one is "Staire," and just staring at it doesn't seem to do anything. Something has to make contact on it. Trilby draws a pair of eyeballs and drops it onto the trap while staring at it. The final trap dissolves, and they can grab the Trilby key as the cage dissolves. The hunter's lodge has a "U" in a different color.

They use the gold key on the hunter's lodge door, which the Party assumes will lead them to another fight. A large ballroom greets the Party as they enter. Small tables are along the edges of the room, but the focal point is an enormous grand piano that is playing music. Trilby notices wisps of dancers in the hall, but they never stay around for long. A door to the North is visible. The Party enters the room fully, as Trilby reaches halfway across the room towards the Piano. The music stops, and the spectral hands that were playing the piano disappear. They reappear and are now the size of Trilby as they approach him. Gelnek is about to enter when Miori comes running up behind him and enters the room first. The door shuts behind her, leaving Gelnek out of the fight, but Mirori is in. *Combat Begins.* Morenthal once again misses his opening bow shot, and Coil follows up exactly the same way. Feyli casts Insect Plague on the hands, hitting both of them easily, and she enters Arche form again. A Legendary Action goes off from an unknown enemy. Coil hears beautiful music and fails a WIS save. He becomes charmed and is given the order to "kill those who would prevent the music," starting with Trilby standing next to him. It is fortunately not his turn, so Hobson blasts the hands (and insects) with Synaptic Static. Only one hand fails that one. Werewolf Valse is also here, so Hobson tells him to enter the Insect Plague. Valse says no and ends his turn. On the hands' turn, one doesn't move while the other one goes to smack into Morenthal and Trilby, knocking them both prone. Trilby, however, uses his Armor of Magical Strength to remain upright. Miori runs up, transforms her hand into a pink & purple crystal, and stabs into the hand that just attacked. Trilby summons Scrubbins in Protector mode to keep everyone up and going. Morenthal stabs into the hand in front of him for a big hit. The hand shakes as if it was hurt, just like the one still in the Insect Plague. Coil is controlled by the music and is thus compelled to kill Trilby. He gets two swings off, and the Party knows something's up. Feyli yells at him to stop and casts Mass Cure Wounds on the Party. She also moves to be closer to Coil. The invisible creature goes now, causing everyone except for Coil to take a CON save as a blast of music hits them. Only Hobson and Morenthal save, and the others take large amounts of Force damage and become Frightened. Hobson hits the hand in the Insect Plague with his Eldritch Blasts and fails to get a frightened Valse to attack the second hand.

That first hand charges through Trilby, Scrubbins, and Feyli, smacking the former two and knocking Scrubbins prone. Miori walks over and uses a high level Cure Wounds on Trilby. However, this does trap Trilby, so he can't move. He uses Scrubbins to give more temp HP and stabs the hand. Morenthal goes for the stab and gets it easily. Coil, still charmed, smashes his sword into Scrubbins once. Feyli shoots another Guiding Bolt and Archer shot at the same hand Morenthal hit. She manages to kill that hand. The invisible entity crushes Morenthal with a powerful force, but he's still standing. Hobson gathers his strength and shoves Werewolf Valse closer to the second hand so that he can attack it. Valse fails to land a hit, and the hand crashes through Feyli, Scrubbins, Trilby, and Miori again. Miori runs away from the hand, taking an Opportunity Attack, but she's able to cast Greater Restoration on Coil, snapping him out of the charm effect. Trilby has Scrubbins give out more Temp HP and uses his last few charges of the Trilbee. Morenthal whirls around and stabs the thing behind him. It is very large, but he can't really see it. Coil, having now gotten out of charm, asks Trilby for a status update, and Trilby says that there's one hand left and an invisible creature near Morenthal. Coil proceeds to smack the heck out of the hand, forcing it to shake. Feyli casts Blight on the hand, dispersing it. The giant invisible creature is revealed as a giant eye connected to an emaciated body hanging from the ceiling. Feyli hits it with an Archer blast, and it responds by squishing Morenthal again. Hobsoon shoots the eye with Eldritch Blasts, and Valse continues to be useless, but at least he breaks out of Frightened. Miori transforms her arm again and stabs the eye, taunting Morenthal by not healing him. Trilby has Scrubbins fire more temp HP out, hits his Ray of Frost, and breaks out of Frightened. Morenthal keeps up with the stabbing. Coil spawns Goo Coil to get flanking on the eye, laying into the giant target with ease. Feyli blasts it with another Blight and another Archer hit, and she breaks out of Frightened. The eye decides that it's time for Morenthal to die, so it lands a critical hit and knocks him to the ground. Hobson's Eldritch blasts & Werewolf Valse eviscerates the eyeball, finally ending the fight. Miori gets Morenthal back up. She leaves the room to continue searching for the boys. Gelnek walks in and asks what he missed. The eye dissolves into an amethyst, which breaks open to reveal a Morenthal key. The Party also finds an "R" in the room. End of Session.
