## Session 44: Master of the House

We open after the most recent fight in the giant ballroom, and the Party is reuinited. The amythyst that contained the key is still intact enough to be collected by Feyli. Gelnek uses a Channel Divinity: Preserve Life to heal the Party right before they all take a Short Rest. There are doors in the ballroom, Valse's cottage, and an earlier room. The Party heads back to the earlier room to finish the row, and they use Trilby's key on it. Inside is a reading nook filled with tacky flower patterned blankets and plastic covering the furnature. $\color{tan}{\textbf{Miriam Gray}}$ is sipping lavender tea and greets the Party. She asks the Party to bring a book from each bookshelf, gesturing to a large bookshelf behind her. On each of the 6 shelves there are 5 colorful books that stand out, each of which has an image of a creature on it. Shelf 1 has a Rhino, Mastiff, Hyena, Owlbear, and Camel. Shelf 2 has a Gargoyle, Minotaur, Griffon, Chimera, and Cockatrice. Shelf 3 has a Vrock, Etin, Succubus, Rakshasa, and Erinies. Shelf 4 has a Banshee, Mummy, Ghoul, Skeleton, and Pegasus. Shelf 5 has a Beholder, Mindflayer, Aboleth, Harpy, and Nothic. Shelf 6 has a Troll, Fire Giant, Cyclops, Centaur, and an Ogre. The Party begin to think about what the puzzle's solution could be. They believe it has something to do with finding the odd one out. Trilby realizes that Shelf 1 has all Beast type creatures except the Owlbear, which is a Monstrosity. They believe shelf 2 is Minotaur, since it doesn't have wings. Shelf 3 seems to be "find the not-Fiend," which they aren't sure of. Gelnek doesn't have any idea. Coil recalls a story from his past that an infernal assassin looked like a Rakshasa. Feyli remembers that Etins are not infernal, instead being mountain monsters. Shelf 4 is Pegasus, since it isn't Undead. Shelf 5 is likely Harpy, since it isn't an alien monster. Finally, shelf 6 is Centaur, since that isn't a Giant type. They take those books and bring them to Mirriam. She hands back the Minotaur book, saying she's already read it. Applying the classification logic to Shelf 2 results in the Gargoyle being chosen as not a Monstrosity. Mirriam takes the book and shakes out a crystal key. They locate a letter 'E' inside. They backtrack to Valse's cottage room and uses the Gelnek key.

This room is the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Brass Squid}}$, which is a familiar sight to everyone in the Party. The illusions of patrons flit by while three stone figures sit in a corner table. First is $\color{tan}{\textbf{Gavel}}$, the Orc tavenkeeper sitting in front of a slice of apple pie. Next is $\color{tan}{\textbf{Mera}}$, the Half-Elf server with a piece of toast and blueberry jam. The third is $\color{tan}{\textbf{Derrick Chaterly}}$, the Human server with a bowl of potato soup. A door to the North sits at the top of the stairs where the inn rooms would be. Gelnek goes to the bar, but all the service fades before it reaches him. Including hashbrowns, which makes Trilby distraught. Trilby notices that nobody at the table has silverware, but asking for some does nothing. Gelnek uses Mending and notices that the statues are clearly carved rather than them being petrified. He talks to Gavel, but only the fading voices respond. Trilby goes behind the bar and grabs some silverware for them. He puts a fork for the pie, a knife for the toast and jam, and a spoon next to the bowl. The statues animate and eat the food. A gold key is created as the food merges and morphs together. One of the signs with the Brass Squid's name on it has the 'O' of the capital Q highlighted. They decide to finish the column using the Morenthal key.

Inside is a dim chamber with candles on wooden tables, clearly a Bloodletter barracks. Hushed conversations between Bloodletters members continue as the Party is invited to a central table by a hooded figure. Morenthal sits at the table, and the figure pulls back his hood to reveal himself as $\color{tan}{\textbf{Malaki Irion}}$, who says that they're doing 1 on 1 games, so Morenthal didn't need to bring the whole crew. The game is Liar's Dice: each person gets 5d6, and rolls them secretly. Malloke starts bidding for the count of a number between all 10 dice. For example, saying there are two 3s. Morenthal then gets to either raise the count or the value, but not lower it. Alternatively, they can call "spot on" or a lie. Spot On requires the exact amount, and calling a bluff requires at least as many. Calling correctly means the caller wins, else the other does. (Due to the nature of the game, the actual dice results are hidden from everyone.) Malloke starts and calls two 3s. The end result is Morenthal calling the bluff at Malloke's five 4s. Malloke wins, as there were seven 4s. He laughs and says that Morenthal might need the whole crew. If at least 1 person wins, they can leave. Feyli immediately jumps to go next. Malloke starts with two 2s. The end result is Feyli calling the bluff at two 6s. Malloke wins again with exactly two 6s. Trilby goes next. Malloke starts at two 1s. The end result is Malloke calling the bluff at three 3s. Trilby wins with exactly three 3s on the board. A Coil key falls out of the cup used to play the game. A door to the East is easily found, and a wanted poster with the letter 'C' is hung on the wall. They head back to the ballroom and use the gold key on the north door there.

The door leads to a staircase and attic space above. Boxes have been pushed aside, and a large amount of webbing is visible within. The wooden roof goes almost 50 ft high. Coil leads the way and swipes away cobwebs. Trilby looks up and causes the fight to start. *Combat Begins.* He sees a massive spider with an eyeball on its back hanging on the ceiling. It drops four eggs which immediatley burst into miniature versions of the spider. The giant spider also drops to the floor. Morenthal rushes the giant spider and hits with a crit. It reacts by spins and spawns more mini spiders. His off-hand almost kills a mini spider. Coil spawns Goo-Coil to rush the big spider before easily killing two mini spiders. Morenthal gets chomped by the big spider and fails the CON save to avoid being paralized. Feyli casts Blight on the big spider, but it saves, taking half damage. Gelnek nimbly runs up to Morenthal to cast Lesser Restoration, removing the paralysis. Trilby summons Scubbins in Flamethrower mode and immediately blasts the mini spider in front of him. The mini spiders swarm around, killing Goo Coil and getting a couple bites. Hobson uses Eldritch Blast to keep blasting small spiders, and Valse kills one too befor running up to be next to the big spider. Morenthal misses both the big spider and the little one next to him. He proceeds to pout. Coil rages and misses the big spider twice. Morenthal gets bitten once again but saves the paralysis. It then climbs over to a pillar and climbs 10 ft off the ground. Feyli uses Call Lightning and Archer Form to blast the big spider. As a reaction, the big spider spawns a ton more mini spiders. Gelnek has this, though. He casts Spirit Guardians, creating a ton of fly swatters to annihilate a ton of spiders just by running around. Trilby moves Scrubbins to burn away the mini spiders nearby, and he manages to avoid the fire himself. He the casts Fireball on the big spider, which kills it. Several tiny spiders remain, but many are blown up by Spirit Guardians. They're mostly harmles though, and the Party makes quick work of them. However, the fight isn't over, because the eye of the big spider is still skuttling around. Coil kills it, and it explodes into spider guts. A fist-sized pearl sits among the vicera as well as a gold key. Coil brings it to Feyli. Among the random boxes, the Party finds an 'E'. A door to the North and East are visible. Feyli and Gelnek heal up the Party. Feyli uses the crystal key on the East door.

An elegant master bedroom greets them. Blue fabrics decorate the room. Windows show the ocean floor outside, and streams of water come through the seams int he walls, similar to the Entrance Hall. Hobson notices that the illusary magic that is felt through the rest of the manor is not present here. A large painting of a nobleman does ping as magical. The figure has a celestial hood over him, stars mixing with the background. Hobson doesn't recognize the figure themselves, but the clasp of the hood has a symbol of $\color{tan}{\textbf{Zimera}}$. It is a star with a banded ring around it. She is one of the Divine Nine and is a goddess of knowledge. He notices that the eyes of the portrait follow Hobson. He waves and the figure straightens and begins to speak. He offers congrats to the Party for getting this far. He is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Sylas Windfield}}$, creator of the Wellmore Keep. Trilby introduces himself and asks if the estate is normally like it. Sylas says that this room is normal, but the rest of the estate is likely never before seen by the Party. He created it for a family that he unfortunately never got with his wife $\color{gray}{\textit{Elizabeth}}$. He decided to change the manor into a force that would seek out worthy people to explore its halls and eventually claim it for themselves. When those people's time was up, the process would repeat. The manor is currently occupied but he has no qualms about stealing it from the curretn owner, as it is all quite exciting. Sylas has an idea of why the Party is here, and Trilby asks about the talking painting thing. The painting contains a piece of Sylas's soul, and it was created before his death. Trilby asks for the letter in the room, and Sylas says that it's the letter 'R'. Sylas continues, saying he has no challenge or riddle, but he does have information. As the manor is blessed by Zimera, he says that each Party member may ask a question about anything. Little is outside the sight of the Divine Nine, and Sylas has a small amount of that knowledge. This offer only stands as long as they remain in the room.

Trilby starts with by asking for information about the upcoming trials in the manor. Sylas says that the trials within the manor should not be concerning. The occupant of the final room, however, is very dangerous. The Throne is not simply an artifact, but it is a creature that feeds on the desires of the person who sits on it. He does not know if the brother can be safely removed. <br>
Hobson goes next and asks about getting Valse out of the bracer without killing him. Sylas says that removing Valse from the bracer currently would lose the soul to Etherial Sea. The body is gone, except for the pieces on the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Isle of Shariza}}$ which have been corrupted. By purifying that body, it would become safe to bind the spirit of Valse to a new vessel. Valse is unsettled by the news and actually begins to think long and hard about his situation for the first time in a while. <br>
Gelnek is third and asks if the Breaker has abandoned Gelnek. Sylas seems to look into the distance for a while before asking what he feels during combat when he calls to Aether. Is he provided for? Gelnek raises his eyebrows and nods. Sylas doesn't know the Breaker as well as Gelnek, but Gelnek would know if he was on the Breaker's bad side. Gelnek is happy and releived at that answer. <br>
Morenthal slinks to the painting and asks where his parents are. Sylas says that $\color{teal}{\textbf{Tarlia}}$ and $\color{teal}{\textbf{Balin Billium}}$ live in $\color{brown}{\textbf{Deepwheat}}$, across the sea. They were robbed of their only child as they emigrated from the Underdark, stolen on the ship they traveled on. They did not give Morenthal up. Deepwheat is across the sea and is a safe haven for people from the Underdark, including Drow. <br>
Coil steps up and asks what he used to be in the past. Sylas knows some things. Coil was first made during a time of divine turmoil. His form was something else other than his current state. But his day-to-day life is still a big unknown. Coil asks how he came to be in his current state. Sylas says that the black ichor powering Coil may have once been part of a god. How the Voedian society figured it out is unknown, but they discovered a method of immortality. Losing one's original form is a part of that. Coil asks which god the ichor is made from, and Sylas whispers the name $\color{tan}{\textbf{Ul'brius}}$, aka the Seeping Rot. <br>
Finally Feyli steps forward but asks the Party to leave the room before she asks her question. Morenthal resists but is eventually led out. She asks about Aryk and her parents, and if they're better now. Sylas explains the afterlife. Souls are given a quiet eternal existence with the Nightmother. There is no suffering, and they are treated with respect and love. Feyli asks if they remember her. Sylas asks if Feyli remembers them. She does. And that's the answer. She leaves the room, and the painting stops animating. The Party goes North from the previous spider room and uses their gold key on it.

Inside is a beautiful garden that has since been overtaken by nature. A massive growth with a vilebloom (corpse flower) bulb on top rests in the center of the room. Trilby preemptively summons Scrubbins to give everyone temp HP. Coil approaches the vilebloom, stepping on one vine that comes from the flower. The air crackles with electricity, cooking off some smaller plants, as a form rises out of the flower. Sparking vines break out of nut shells next to the flower, and *Combat Starts.* Coil runs up and kills an electric vine with two strong swings of his sword. Morenthal once again rushes the main flower boss for a critical hit and is shocked by the surrounding vines. He also kills a surrounding vine and nut. The giant flower moves between the Party, attacks, and hits Morenthal, Coil, Trilby, and Scrubbins. A nut and vine is restored too. Feyli casts Wall of Fire on two nuts and the main flower, killing two nuts and seemingly dealing major damage. She also enters her Archer form, but misses. The nuts hit Coil once while Scrubbins regenerates the temp HP. Trilby misses a Ray of Frost aimed at a nut. Hobson uses Synaptic Static to hit the main flower and a nut, killing the latter. The flower saves against the spell. They also notice that the nuts and vines seem to either be absorbing damage or otherwise shielding the main flower. Valse moves into attack position but doesn't swing yet. Gelnek tries to cast Command: Grovel on the main flower, but the flower doesn't seem to understand so nothing happens. Coil kills the final nut, and Morenthal goes in for the heavy hits. The plant is still coursing with electiricty, so Morenthal gets shocked again. The flower moves again, provoking several opportunity attacks, but only Valse makes contact. Valse is also the only one to be shocked as a result. The main flower leans down and spins before shooting Lighting Bolt at Coil, Valse, Scrubbins, and Feyli. Everyone who fails the save is also forced to take a CON save. Scrubbins is paralyzed for 2 rounds and Valse is killed. Roots draw in more energy, creating 2 more nuts, and some wounds are healed. Feyli drops Wall of Fire and uses Insect Plague on it and one nut, which seems to only be useful on the nut as vines deflect the insects away from the main flower. Her archer shot kills the other nut. Trilby picks up the paralyzed Scrubbins and shoots Scorching Ray at the flower. One gets swatted away before Trilby remembers to shoot the nut, and he kills it. With all the nuts down, Hobson is now able to blast the flower with Synaptic Static, and the flower fails this time. Gelnek misses a Guiding Bolt, and Coil lays into it, receiving shocks in retaliation. Morenthal matches the energy and attacks. The flower moves to cast Lightning Bolt again on almost everyone, and Coil gets an opportunity attack, but he doesn't get the kill. Hobson and Feyli fail the DEX save, but only Feyli becomes paralized and loses her turn. The flower creates 1 nut and heals some damage. Trilby kills the other nut with some extra Scorching Rays, leaving the flower open. Hobson blasts the flower with Eldritch Blasts and Gelnek hits with a Guiding Bolt. Coil takes the final trade of damage by killing it with a big stab into its heart, endign the fight. The flower dissolves into a massive emerald and a Feyli key. Coil brings the emerald to Feyli again, and the Party heals up again with many spells and abilities. They locate the letter 'O' carved into a tree nearby. A door to the East leads out of the room. They use Coil's key on it.

The heat of a loud forge hits the Party all at once. A massive Warforged is crouched over an anvil, hammering away at a glowing piece of metal. Coil recognizes this figure who turns to look at the arriving Party. His tiny head turns to greet them. He asks if Coil is here with a blade or just lookng to get away. Coil says the latter, so the smith gestures to a broken barrel which Coil sits in. The smith asks what Coil is getting away from, and Coil says that it's a strange house. The walls are covered in weaponry and armor. Under one array of weapons is a set of 6 murals, each one has a sunken indent of a sword. The first mural has a young man practicing with a sword. The second has a king who is knighting someone. The third has a knight in full armor standing at attention. The fourth has a smith forging a blade. The fifth has a knight delivering a killing blow. The final one has a simple tombstone. There don't appear to be obvious weapons that would fit those murals. The Warforged smith has a bag that contains his most recent creation, and Coil opens it. Inside are 6 blades, each made with different materials and has a different look. The first is a finely crafted golden sword. The second has red rust running through the blade. The third has been completely rusted over and nicked to heck. The fourth has three large breaks in it where it has been misaligned by use. The fifth appears to be made of wood but is acutally metal. The sixth appears to be super a basic metal sword. Coil places the most obvious sword first: gold sword for the king. The rust red blade is placed with the knight dealing a killing blow. The simple sword goes to the knight at attention. The wood sword goes to the practice scene. The mostly destroyed sword goes to the forge. The three large breaks goes to the grave site. The Warforged smith looks over and shakes his head, saying that $\color{gray}{\textit{Augusta}}$ wouldn't have wanted that. Coil swaps the large breaks and the most destroyed sword. The Warforged approves and pulls out a golden key from his sack. The Party searches for a letter, and they find an 'R' behind a shield. Coil think long and hard and recalls that the Warforged's name is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Fork}}$. They leave the room. End of Session.
