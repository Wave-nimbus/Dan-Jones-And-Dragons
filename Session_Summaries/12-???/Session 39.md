## Session 39: Choose Your Barfight Carefully

(*Note: Jocat was late to session.*)<br>
We open as Coil leaves the Voedian vault. Miori and Feyli are covered in the ichor and resemble creatures from an oil spill commercial. The vault seals behind Coil as he heads back to town. We switch to the rest of the Party plus Kurva as they figure out what to do now. As Hobson tries to look around for the Cabal members, the entire Party -- including Coil -- takes WIS saves. Everyone other than Feyli loses 1 Max HP. The marketplace is full of a mix of makeshift stalls and more professional stalls, distinguished by whether the stalls have guards or not. Ships on the docks block much of the view of the bay as crews unload cargo and catches. Arguments by noblefolk are common, and bulletin boards are filled with flyers for all sorts of things. They muse the idea of heading to the inn, and Kurva reminds the Party of the Bleeding Heart inn and tavern. Since it is a pretty obvious safe haven, they head on over. The inn is enormous with 3 floors of service, and the food & drink flow quickly and plentifully. A large albino Tiefling woman paces behind the bar and in the kitchen, ensuring everything works smoothly. She recognizes them as the Flower Crowns and asks what she can do for them. They choose to buy three rooms for 10 gp each per night, and Hobson pays. She introduces herself as $\color{teal}{\textbf{Mita Flasbae}}$, and Trilby continues to be flustered in front of her. He says that they're looking for secrets, and Mita wonders if it's about the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Council}}$. She trades a lot in information, which is powerful in a city like the Shard Coast. She knows about the Viradescent Javelin being owned by the Council, and she knows about the death of a pretty well-known person in the Trade District in $\color{tan}{\textbf{Brightcoin}}$.

Regardless, if the Party needs anything from her, then they can ask. Trilby immediately orders different prepared potatoes and drops off his stuff in his room. They're pretty good but a higher class than what Trilby typically wants. While sitting in here, the Party notices that some staff are Tabaxis who are acrobatic enough to climb rope ladders and netting while carrying drinks and stuff. Hobson is deeply impressed and is absorbing as much as he can about the operations. Kurva asks if the Party wants to get their stuff back or if they're taking a break. Morenthal says that searching for their stuff is too dangerous to do without Coil and Feyli. He wants to wait in the tavern for as long as possible, though Trilby points out that Coil and Feyli don't know where they are right now. Hobson remembers that Coil could be in danger, so he uses Scrying on Coil. Hobson is successful, and he sees Coil, Leaf, and company walking through a mineshaft on the way to the vault, but they haven't arrived yet. His scry lasts until Leaf and his bodyguards got eviscerated. Hobson relays that to the others and immediately uses his other spell slot to scry on Coil again, seeing that nobody else is in any danger. He takes the potato plate from Trilby and takes a Short Rest to recover his spell slots. Morenthal looks at everyone to see if anyone would know him or if he knows anyone.

Morenthal doesn't notice someone that's been watching him for a while, but he does notice a figure on the 3rd floor with a red cloak who is not watching Morenthal. The figure has feet on the table, enjoying a drink. Morenthal points them out to the others and says they should have a chat. (*Jocat returned here*) Kurva stays at the table, but Gelnek, Hobson, Trilby, and Morenthal head up to talk with the figure. As they approach, the figure is a woman with strawberry blonde hair, an arcane tattoo across her face, and is smoking a cigar. She makes eye contact and asks the Party what they want. Morenthal explains that his companions lost some things and asks if the woman would know anything. She doesn't know anything about the Party, so she doesn't have to say anything to them. Morenthal stumbles for a while about what he wants and the Party doesn't help, and the woman is a little impatient. Finally, Morenthal mentions the red cloak, and the woman realizes that some of her boys were successful in taking their stuff. Not that she can really do anything about it, since she doesn't have anything. Gelnek jumps in and says that they might trade a favor for a favor. Morenthal hates that idea and offers gold instead. The woman asks what they're here for, but the Party doesn't answer that directly. She does have an idea that could be traded favor for a favor. If the Party does the favor, she'll see if she can arrange for the Party to get their stuff back. She says that the $\color{aqua}{\textbf{Vaenphyres}}$, a mercenary group in the city, has captured some fish people that know stuff about magical artifacts. A few weeks ago, hundreds of people drowned in a single week, people from every faction. Soon after, much of the fishing in the $\color{coral}{\textbf{Sanguine Bay}}$ became thin, and a bunch of cargo was thrown overboard without crew members realizing what's going on. Leads have been very thin until the two Kuo Toa got captured. If the Party can tell the Cabal what the fish people know, then the items might be returned.

The woman can be found here in the inn most of the time for when the Party gets information. The Vaenphyres don't have much of a hold in the city, and they're less ruthless than the Bloodletters, but not as above the table as others. Trilby pays attention finally, and he figures out that the rune on the woman's face powers a True Seeing spell. With nothing else to discuss, the woman focuses back on her drink while the Party goes back sit at the table. Hobson does another Scrying on Coil, seeing him at the northern cliffs of the Shard Coast. Miori and Feyli get out of Coil and end their Wild Shapes. They wash off the ichor in the sea. Miori says that it's mission accomplished since Leaf and his crew are dead. Coil agrees but is processing all that happened. He says that the three of them should go find the rest of the Party, though none of them have ways to communicate with them. They head off back into town, and Hobson's scry ends just as they do so. The rest of the Party plus Kurva mosey over to go find the other three. As they walk over, Morenthal passes an alley and hears someone call out to him. Morenthal brings everyone along to the alley on high alert. A horned figure is hiding in the shadows and tells Morenthal to get closer. Morenthal tells the Party to stay back as he approaches alone. A Tiefling woman Morenthal recognizes named $\color{teal}{\textbf{Zaris}}$ embraces him and calls him an idiot for returning. If Yuul finds him, Morenthal's a dead man. Morenthal explains the important business about hunting artifacts for the Council, which Zaris doesn't buy at all. She emphasizes that Morenthal should leave soon because there's a ton of stuff going down. Morenthal lets it slip that they got pickpocketed, which Zaris wants to tell others if she could. She also explains that the city's trade is screwed up, so there's a power vacuum that is trying to be filled by various underground factions. The Bloodletters brought a bunch of folks in, including Yuul and someone called $\color{teal}{\textbf{Carth}}$. Apparently, the leader of a major merchant caravan died, and a bunch of pirates are revolting, so it's all a big mess. Bloodletters were brought in from all over the country to keep the peace. The Party can tell that she's not hostile, but they gossip about what's going on.

Zaris concludes that she never saw him, and that she'll see if she can cover for him if anyone does see him, but Morenthal needs to leave asap. Morenthal at least has friends now, and Zaris is happy that he's still alive and got away the first time. When the trade stuff calms down, she'll try to contact Morenthal again after she gets back to Brightcoin. She hugs him one more time and tells him to stay safe. They separate and she leaves. Morenthal returns somehow even more distressed than normal. The Party asks about who Morenthal met, and he lies, saying that he met someone named Johnson. Unfortunately, the Party probably won't meet Johnson anytime soon. About this time, Coil, Miori, & Feyli spot the rest of the Party, and everyone reconvenes. Hobson apologizes for scrying on Coil, which Coil accepts and reports that they got the key back. Hobson says that they got work and a way to get their stuff back. Morenthal says that there's a problem with fish going missing, which might be happening in the Shard Coast. He explains the task of rescuing the Kuo Toa from the Vaenphyres. Coil wonders if Morenthal has contacts in the city that could help. Morenthal laughs it off and says that they should just try the inn again. They head off to the Bleeding Heart again, and Morenthal is covering up even more than he was before. Hobson and Trilby take note of how nervous Morenthal is. Gelnek asks Kurva about the Kuo Toa, but she doesn't know much other than they probably eat fish. She does point out some Kuo Toa haggling with a merchant, so the Party knows what they look like. Coil asks Kurva if she knows about the Vaenphyres, but she doesn't. Trilby goes over to ask Mita about them.

They're a mercenary group that are easy to hire, and are fine with doing dirty work. She won't sell the group out, but she can point to where some members tend to hang out. In exchange for an extra order or two, she tells Trilby to try the $\color{coral}{\textbf{Last Drop}}$, which is frequented by members of the Vaenphyres. Coil immediately pays 10 gp, which Mita immediately takes and thanks Coil. Morenthal reminds the Party that they shouldn't use his name too much in the city. The Party discusses what they're approach should be. Morenthal says they could tail them, but Coil says that they could pretend to hire them, since they're mercenaries. The Last Drop is in the southern part of the city as the Party heads over around 9 pm. Various shops are closing up while other taverns become more lively. They also keep their eye on their belongings. The Last Drop is a smaller tavern pushed up against a cliffwall surrounded by housing and a handful of other shops. Trilby wonders what fake job Coil will offer, but Morenthal says it's not a good idea to be honest. Gelnek rambles that not wanting to be honest makes sense for why Morenthal doesn't want to use his name. Getting back on topic, Morenthal says the job should be protecting a caravan. They just need to get in contact with members. They push inside, where several mercenary looking folk are seated at the tables. It's clearly pretty rowdy inside, and everyone looks ready to jump into a barfight if needed. A short portly Dwarf male is the main bartender, happily filling drinks. As the Party enters, there's a record-scratch moment and waits to see what they do. Coil walks up to the bar, drawing a lot of attention based on what he does.

The bartender asks what they can do for them. Coil drops 3 gp on the counter and asks about the Vaenphyres, since he's got a job. Some members at the bar perk up at that, and the Dwarf asks what the job is, including length of time and number of folks. Coil asks how many are on hand, and the Dwarf says about 15-20 on hand for work, though there are less in the tavern itself. He wants to know more about the job, but Coil doesn't answer, so he steps away to talk to the Party. They discuss strategy further regarding how to find their hideout, as well as further specifics of what the job should be. Coil says that Morenthal could scope the place out as Coil goes back to talk to the bartender about the job specifics. He describes life on the Viradescent Javelin as details, and despite being loose with details, he keeps the bartender's attention. Morenthal tries to stealthily get into the back of the tavern, easily able to open a locked door. Inside is a series of barrels and kegs of drinks. Morenthal inspects the barrels, and they all seem to be legitimate drinking barrels. He leaves, concluding the Last Drop is clear. The bartender does the math on the trip based on his details: 6 days on an airship with 15 guys will run the Party 650 gp, but they can get fancier troops for more. Morenthal asks how they know that the troops are the best the Vaenphyres got. The barkeep says that they can do an inspection for 50 gp at a place the Party wants. Morenthal wants the barkeep to name a place, but the barkeep says that this bar in 2 days will be the meeting. It'll be 350 when they do the inspection, and Coil shakes the barman's hand and asks for a name. Coil tells the barkeep his name, while the barkeep says that his name is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Steve}}$.

Trilby stays behind as the rest of the Party heads out, and he says he wants to get into the back room. Steve makes it clear that he's not allowed there. Trilby leans in close and says he wants information. Steve says that he isn't gonna give anything for free. Trilby says that he wants to know about the two Kuo Toa. Several people in the bar stand up and make their threats clear. Trilby says he's talking to Steve, and Steve replies that Trilby had better choose his words carefully. Trilby says that he could arm wrestle for the information. If he wins, he gets all the information he wants. If Steve wins, Trilby gets beat up by everyone in the bar. Trilby does get advantage thanks to his Ring of Steve, and Gelnek gave him guidance. He uses everything; his Ring of Steve, Ring of Good Luck, Armor of Magical Strength, Flash of Genius, and the Guidance to win. Steve's hand slams into the bar, and he asks what Trilby wants to know. Trilby wants the Kuo Toa, but Steve refuses, since they're the Vaenphyres' property now. Trilby wants to work it out, but Steve refuses. Those in the bar begin to advance, and *Combat Starts.* Morenthal immediately shanks one patron dead. Coil enters his rage and hops a table to cleave into one member and misses another. One mercenary stabs Miori and misses on Feyli, while another misses on Morenthal. Coil uses his Ring of Shared Pain to take some of Miori's damage. Steve jumps on the bar to play wack-a-mole on Trilby. Two other mercs go after Coil and Feyli, but barely deal damage. Hobson says that the Vaenphyres should just give the Party what they want as he sends some Eldritch Blasts at Steve. Feyli blasts another merc with Guiding Bolt, who returns the favor. Others miss pitifully, but one hits Coil twice. Gelnek revels in the barfight and casts Spirit Guardians at 5th level to just start obliterating folks. He uses his Nimble Escape Goblin feature to Disengage and hit everyone in the bar.

Trilby goes to summon Scrubbins, but it fails to be summoned due to the modifications, so he just meekly looks at Steve. Morenthal gets a chunky stab in a merc as Coil summons Goo Coil for the fight. Only Goo Coil lands a hit, though. The merc that Morenthal stabbed tries to flee, but Morenthal catches him on the run. Steve keeps up the hits on Trilby, but Trilby remembers he has Shield, so only hits once. Coil absorbs some more pain off of the damage. One merc eats more Spirit Guardians damage before disengaging and running out of the bar. Hobson hits one merc with Booming Blade and tells him not to move or he'll die. The merc is confused by that. Feyli heals Trilby with Healing Word and enters Chalice form for extra healing. Mercs continue to swing and miss at the Party, though several succumb to Spirit Guardians anyway. Gelnek misses with Marac-Star and runs back to the other half of the bar with the Spirit Guardians. Trilby blasts Steve with a Thunderwave, who slams against the wall, dead. Trilby then runs over to mourn the man whose name was Steve. Morenthal shoots the runner with a single arrow, but they keep running. He then stabs another guy to death. Coil chooses to grab a guy right next to him for interrogation. The last merc runs away after disengaging. Miori asks if they're stopping the runners. Morenthal says it's up to her. So she kills the most recent runner, though the other ran away. Combat ends with the Party holding the last guy while the other runner got away. Trilby apologizes for killing Steve and blames the Ring of Steve's power.

So the last guy here asks what the Party wants. They once again reply that they want the Kuo Toa, which the guy doesn't understand. He does say that there's an entrance in the back room behind the bar. The Party easily finds a cellar trapdoor and everyone enters. Coil slings the merc over his shoulder while Miori stands guard at the door. The trapdoor leads to an underground base of sorts. They can hear the sounds of training going on in the room beyond, and there are doors to either side as they enter. Morenthal stalks ahead and sees about five more well-trained mercenaries just having casual conversation and whatnot. Morenthal gestures to the Party to stay put, and then Morenthal introduces himself, saying Steve said these guys would be down here. The mercs greet him, as Morenthal asks about any jobs here. One merc walks up to him and asks what he can do. Morenthal says he's a good shot with a bow and a good dagger hand. The merc tells $\color{gray}{\textit{Frankie}}$ to set up another target, and Morenthal moves to draw attention and shoot his arrows. He also signals to the Party to move up, which they do. Loudly. As Coil enters the room, one turns and wonders who the heck these others are. Morenthal switches targets to one close to him for a quick kill. The Party gets a surprise round on these guys as *Combat Starts*. Coil walks in and throws the merc he's carrying onto another guy before closing the distance. The one who was thrown ragdolls and dies, and Coil enters his rage. Morenthal slaughters another merc before running up to another for a small nick. Trilby successfully summons Scrubbins to shoot at a merc. Hobson enters with some Eldritch Blasts, and Gelnek enters the ring with Spirit Guardians at 4th level. The surprise round ends as Coil keeps slashing into mercs, and he also summons Goo Coil again. 

A door to the south smashes open as an ogre busts into the room. He scoops up some tiles and throws it at Coil, Feyli, Scrubbins, and Trilby, who all take DEX saves. Feyli uses Cure Wounds on herself and moves elsewhere in the room. A figure in full-plate armor and a bow steps in from the south, and she fires a shot at Gelnek with a frightening maneuver. She also uses Action Surge to fire more shots, which causes Gelnek to lose concentration on Spirit Guardians. Morenthal stabs the guy next to him as more mercs run from elsewhere in the base and attack Gelnek. Trilby casts a Fireball towards the south, but the ogre uses one of their many rings to cast Counterspell. Hobson takes the opportunity to cast Synaptic Static in the southern area. The ogre somehow passes the save. Gelnek uses a Channel Divinity of Preserve Life to keep the Party up before running back into the corner. Coil gets some swings at the merc he's fighting and sends Goo Coil to flank with Morenthal. $\color{gray}{\textit{Norn}}$ the ogre walks up to Morenthal for some big warhammer slams, and Morenthal and Coil use some damage reduction on the attacks. Feyli summons an Insect Plague on the ogre and two mercs. The armored knight fires at Goo Coil and then Morenthal with a distraction maneuver. Morenthal stabs the guy in front of him, finally killing him. The normal mercs continue to swarm the Party with attacks. Trilby uses Shatter to hit several mercs, the archer knight, and the ogre, and then Trilby shoots a merc dead with Force Balista. Hobson's Eldritch Blasts plink off the ogre's armor. Gelnek keeps folks alive and awake with a Mass Healing Word before hitting a nearby merc. Miori runs into the room saying that there's a big guy (but not ogre big) coming from up top. Coil kills another merc and jumps into the fray for Morenthal's sneak attack. The ogre smashes a distracted Morenthal, and Feyli summons a Moonbeam on them and enters Archer form to hit again.

The Knight walks up to Coil and tells him, "nice sword," before attacking 3 times, including a frightening maneuver that fails to affect Coil. Morenthal kills yet another merc, and the mercs retaliate on Gelnek and Coil. Trilby holds action to blast whoever comes down the trapdoor. Hobson fires more Eldritch Blasts at the ogre. Gelnek finally kills the merc next to him as Coil goes after the knight and gets Goo Coil back in the fight. He also asks what the knight gets paid, since it probably isn't enough. Miori walks into the room and also prepares to cast a spell at anyone who walks down the stairs. The ogre slams into Morenthal and Hobson after eating Moonbeam damage. Feyli hits the ogre with Blight and ends him with and an Archer form shot. The knight sees that happen and sheathes her sword. Combat is paused until the person walks down from upstairs. The knight says they used to just be a merc, but since the ogre is dead, she's out. The guy that walks down is $\color{tan}{\textbf{Graster Bane}}$, and he asks what the heck is going on down here. End of Session.
