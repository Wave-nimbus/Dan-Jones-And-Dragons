## Session 30: Grand Theft Wagon

We open as the Party enters the Maelstrom within Varu'Il looking for their contact Raya. The Party is mostly beat from the previous fight, but Gelnek calls for a feast in the Ustenki tradition. Hobson ensures that the Party all have rooms for the night. The food is noot particularly fancy, but it is plentiful in the form of dinosaur-like meat and haunches. Other than the Runebreakers, various other town guards come in to relax from fending off the tribes & demon armies spewing from the Keep. Coil goes over to ask the Runebreakers to ask about Raya. The Tiefling woman greets him, and Coil asks about Raya. The male Half-Elf stands and offers to bring him and the Flower Crowns to Raya when they're all ready. Coil tells the Party that they can meet Raya when they're ready. Gelnek talks to various soldiers about the situation in the city. The Keep is moostly held at bay, but the opportunistic Zotrians have captured portions of the city. While they hold power politically, they allow trade & the merchants to work normally since money is king, even moreso than other places. Hobson recalls that Varu'Il was colonized centuries ago with a peace treaty being signed between them and the Zotrians. The Zotrians, however, have been ignoring the peace treaties and trying to eke out territory when they can. Further, a **Merchant Collective** is in change of governing the Varu'Il city. Trilby is able to remember a few other things, such as the volcanoes that generate land. Said new land is given to one side or the other depending on the result of a congress/discussion. The Party takes a Short Rest as they finish the meal and the sun goes down. 

The Half-Elf Runebreaker leads the Party downstairs to Raya. She is an Elven woman with a low-cut top with a heavy furred collar. She stares at a map of the island of Djoria. She greets the Flower Crowns and introducers herself as Raya (???), and she explains how screwed over the city is. The Party is here to destroy The Heart of the Keep. Entering is a problem. At midnight, a bridge lowers from the Keep and anyone who touches it spawns waves of the hellspawn. The bridge is a half mile (kilometer) long, and massive swarms appear to destroy anyone on the bridge. The current plan is to cross with an armored wagon, but it's a one-way trip to get inside. Unfortuantely, the wagon, and the lab it was in, is currently in Zotrian territory. Raya & Runebreakers were coordinating to break through the line and get the wagon back. From there, they can head inside the Keep using said wagon. Assuming the guards know the knight the hordes show up, they can be prepared. Attempting to talk with the Zotrians is likely futile since their presence is already considered an act of war. Once the wagon is retrieved, the Party can easily ride it back to Varu'Il territory. Raya calls the Half-Elf **Rodrick** and asks him to tell her when the Runebreakers are ready to act in the morning. Once everyone's ready, they can go for the wagon. With nothing else to do, the Party heads on off to take their Long Rest. Trilby resumes converting Scrubbins into a holy water gun, working through the night to complete the job. Mechanically, when Scrubbins is in Force Balista mode, Trilby can use a Bonus Action to load the holy water cartridge for Scrubbins to fire. It is equivalent to throwing holy water.

Morning greets the Party after an uneventful night. Raya goes over the plan, reminding the Runebreakers that they're the distraction, not an invading force. She then confirms that the Party is ready to go, as they all head off towards the north of the city. The Flower Crowns were given a map directing them to the Northeast of the city where the lab and wagon are. An obvious signal will let them know when to run for it. Soon enough, a gunshot is heard, and Zotrian forces head off towards it. This is the best chance for the Party to sneak inside enemy lines. They get very close to the lab and see a handful of guards that have hung back. A pair of riders and a small group of melees patrol the front entrance. The Party hatches a plan to catch all the guards by surprise. Trilby uses Vortex Warp to send Morenthal off into some bushes for flanking. Anyone with ranged attacks are going to focus fire on one of the riders while Coil rushes into the thick of things. Gelnek is going to use Hold Person on both riders. Since Morenthal needs to wait for the Party, they get to engage first. Gelnek starts with Hold Person, Hobson uses Synaptic Static, Feyli uses Guiding Bolt, Coil gets a pair of slashs off, Morenthal gets an sneaked arrow off, and Trilby uses his Scorching Ray. With all of these attacks. Both riders and a melee die instantly, while one melee and a mount are severely injured. *Combat Begins* for real as everyone rolls initiative. Two melees attack Morenthal & Coil while the third one blows a horn as an alarm. Trilby summons Scrubbins as Gelnek dashes over to the melee next to Morenthal. Coil finishes of his melee guy and begins to slash into one mount as Hobson uses Eldritch blast on the other mount. Feyli activates her Stary Form and kills the damaged mount with a Guiding Bolt. The other mount fails to bite Coil as Morenthal mounts up on top of the dinosaur, trying to gain control. 

Due to the horn that blew earlier, reinforcements cross a bridge near the lab as 3 more melee guys show up. Heavy thuds can be heard from a street in the oppostie direction. Trilby creates a Wind Wall on the bridge to harass the Zotrian guards as they cross. Scrubbins approaches in flamethrower mode but doesn't act. Gelnek misses his attacks as Coil makes up for it by killing the melee guard. Hobson decides to summon Valse as a Treant to block the bridge and attack the guards. Feyli takes a look to see what the heavy thumps are and sees an enormous dinosaur being ridden by a Zotrian with an elaborate headdress. It is about 100 ft away. She makes a Wall of Fire to hold it off for the meantime. Morenthal rides his new mount to a central position and snipes one more melee. The last melee on the bridge attacks the Valse Treant as the Zotrian chieftan rides in on a T-Rex, overrunning Feyli's Wall of Fire, jumping off the T-Rex, and clubbing Gelnek upside the head so hard that he loses a hit die. Trilby uses a Fireball to blast the chieftan and the T-Rex. Gelnek uses Command to force the cheiftan to run away on its turn. The T-Rex decides it wants Drow for lunch, biting Morenthal and then nearly swallowing him. Thanks to a Bardic Inspiration and Flash of Genius, Morenthal is able to hang onto the mount's reigns and not get eaten. Coil decides Shadow Coil is worth summoning as he begins to slash at the ankles of the T-Rex. Feyli hits the T-Rex with a pitiful Guiding Bolt as Hobson hits it with a double Eldritch Blast. Tree Valse kills the last reinforcing guard and then walks towards the T-Rex. Morenthal, by some miracle, is able to direct the dino mount to distract the T-Rex. The chieftan runs away due to the command as Gelnek gets an attack of opportunity to connect. The T-Rex is burned by Scrubbins, and Trilby sends the Trilbee to heal Morenthal before shooting even more fire at it. Gelnek fires a Guiding Bolt at the chieftan as the T-Rex tail swipes the dino mount, killing it and knocking it into Valse. Morenthal follows gets yanked after. It then turns to bite & grapple Coil, but Coil is able to immediately wriggle out. Feyli misses her Guiding Bolt while Hobson is able to get the killing blow on the T-Rex. Tree Valse puts down Morenthal from where he landed in the branch arms. Morenthal gets one last potshot off on the chieftan as he begins to run away. Gelnek catches him in a Grovel Command, catches up to him, and ties him up after using Hold Person. He carries the chieftan back as disrespectfully as possible.

The combat sounds of the distraction are still audible, so the Party quickly patch themselves up thanks to Trilby and Feyli. They leave the chieftan bound outside as they all head into the research lab. All except Gelnek, who decides to stay behind and stand guard. The entrance is very simple compared to the grand double doors. Behind a front desk is a large service elevator, and a pair of side rooms are trashed. The 5 head into the lift, noting that there are 3 sublevels below the ground floor. They first go to B1 in the loud & slow lift, taking 2 mins to go 1 level. The doors open to a large ruined alchemy lab that has also been mostly destroyed. Ruined equipment and dead bodies are strewn about. Trilby calls for survivors, but there are none. They head off to B2 in the super slow lift. This one is filled with metal drums filled with various alchemical chemicals, and the floor is wet with said chemicals. A closed door in the back of the room is visible. The 5 take a quick peek into the door, passing by 4 more bodies. As Morenthal listens for sounds and concentrates, he feels a pain directly behind his left eye. He tells this to the Party, who is surprised since he never told them about the vision. He switches to simply saying they should be careful inside. The office behind this door appears mostly trashed but there are no people. Trilby and Morenthal head back to the lift as Hobson takes an additional look. He finds a paper that describes an altered strain of Dragon's Bane that is intended to be used against the Zotrians if/when the Merchant Collective agree on it. Until then, experimentation is approved on "Subject Zero." They head back to the elevator and go down to the third and final basement. The smell of chemicals dissipate, but Morenthal's eye pain gets stronger. There are several desks and various tanks filled with green bubbling liquid. About 10 of them have various Lizardfolk inside, suspended and unconscious. At the far end of the room is yet another lift. As he steps out, Morenthal uses his Eye of the Nightmother ability, not picking up anything. They all head over to the other lift.

Hobson's connection to Valse allows him to detect various magical signatures, and he detects an Abjuration spell as his friends walk into the spell. As that happens, 5 of the tubes along the walls shatter, releasing the Lizardfolk, who look angrily at the Party. However, only 4 are visible. They include one with a frilled neck lizard, a scaled iguana, a hela monster, and gecko. *Combat Begins.* The hela walks up to Feyli and bites her with a very venomous bite. Coil sends his Echo to attack the hela monster while he goes after the gecko. The hela monster sprays out poison due to being hit, killing Echo Coil and hitting Feyli. The frilled neck lizard unfurls its frills, revealing a hypnotic pattern that frightens Coil & Trilby. Hobson fires one Eldritch Blast at the iguana, who seems to absorb the force damage. As Hobson notices, he redirects his second shot at the frilled neck lizard. Feyli fires a Guiding Bolt at the iguana, who absorbs the radiant energy. The gecko in front of Coil heals off the last attack before slashing back at him. Morenthal hits it big with his sneak attack and purging dagger. He gets stabbed in the back by an invisible lizardfolk as the iguana attacks Hobson and Trilby by using reflected magic effects. Trilby fails to stab the frilled lizard and is still frightened by it, but Feyli is no longer frightened. The hela monster whiffs against Feyli. Coil slashes away at the gecko but he notices that it would be better to use magic, so he trades targets for the iguana. Coil breaks free of being frightened thanks to Trilby's flash of genius. The frilled lizard shoots a glob of acid at Hobson but misses. Gelnek up top hear the sounds of combat fade from the West and many troops walking towards the lab. Gelnek carries the chieftan inside, leaves him in the front entrance, and begins to climb down a ladder to the 3rd basement. Hobson uses an Eldritch Blast on the gecko, who doesn't seem nearly as affected as he should. Feyli blasts the gecko with a beautiful guiding bolt, but it also doesn't seem as effective as normal. She tries to back off and is hit by the frilled lizard. The gecko heals off a lot of the damage he got as he walks up to slash Hobson. Morenthal gets the kill on the frilled lizard, who oozes acid blood onto the floor, and he drinks a healing potion. Yet again, an invisible blade stabs Morenthal, nearly knocking him out. The iguana misses terribly on Coil, and Trilby heals Morenthal an okay amount. The hela monster bites Morenthal, who uses his uncanny dodge to survive.

Gelnek is 1 turn away at this point as Coil enters his rage to lay into the iguana. Hobson fails to hit the gecko with his shortsword. Feyli enters Stary Form and then casts Blight on the gecko, who both saves and isn't as affected as normal. The gecko flanks Hobson and hits him twice. Morenthal slashes the hela monster, who releases a small cloud of poison that hurts Trilby. Morenthal drinks his second potion as the invisible creature misses. Trilby uses thunderwave to hopefully hit the invisible lizard as well as the hela monster, who lets out another poison cloud. Coil finally is able to cleave the iguana in twain, and he notices his blood is a dark ichor in color. The hela monster switches it up, biting Trilby for quite a bit. Hobson stabs the gecko with Booming Blade, and the gecko shrugs off the thunder damage too. Feyli throws an Ice Knife at the gecko, who's standing right next to her and Hobson, so all three of them take damage, though the gecko shrugs off the cold damage. Morenthal finally takes out the hela monster, who releases a small poison cloud. He then attempts to stab the air behind him, expertly slashing nothing. The gecko slashes some more at Hobson and Feyli. Gelnek has finally arrived, and smacks the gecko and smites it, neither of which appear to do much. Trilby is grateful that Gelnek has arrived and gets stabbed in the back for his troubles. He does, however, finally look around the room and finds some notes about the gecko. Apparently the tail is the weakspot, and armed with this knowledge and his sword, Trilby gets a massive hit on the gecko. Coil follows up with a couple slashes of his own. Everyone prepares to wale on the Gecko as Feyli unleashes a Mass Cure Wounds and enters Chalice Form. Morenthal kills the gecko with his offhand dagger, and now there is only the invisible lizard left. Gelnek finds another note that discusses Subject Zero's regeneration & recovery against the Dragon's Bane strains. He also finds the paper on the invisible lizard. It is invisible without magic or using fire. With the secret out, Gelnek gets stabbed by said invisible creature. The Party prepares their fire attacks: Scrubbins is summoned, Coil wildly swings around, Hobson throws a length of rope, which makes contact with something, but the exact placement is unknown. Feyli casts Wall of Fire to reveal an invisible chameleon with a sword for a tongue. Morenthal gets the final blow with an arrow, cutting off the tongue.

The Party takes a Short Rest and are able to find the other research notes about the 5 lizards that escaped the glass tubes. The full 6-person Party head down the final elevator a few more floors to a darkened chamber. A dark writhing form is struggling against chains. A Zotrian shaman is talking sweet nothings to this shadow, calling it (???), who will be the destruction of the Varu'Il. But for now, they have the Party to deal with. The shadow unfurls massive wings and reveals itself to be a Green Dragon. End of Session.
