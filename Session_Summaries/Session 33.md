## Session 33: Finding the Fractured Pieces

The Flower Crowns and Miory awake within the safe room after the fight with the fiend. The Party all agree to leave this safe room behind and instead go to a different room for proper resting. As they walk over to the Southeast safe room, they do some healing to each other. They all take a Long Rest, spells get newly prepared, and magic items may get attuned to. They discuss what happened in the nightmare. Trilby mentions that he had a vision, Hobson & Miory both say they didn't get a vision. The rest of the Flower Crowns don't say anything. They all walk over to the boat south of the crypt room, and Miory uses Wild Shape to turn into an owl to explore. She reports that it dead ends into a rock wall, and its water the whole way. She doesn't think it's a way out. Trilby wonders if the water goes under the rock wall, but they don't go that way. They check on the body parts collected: a head, a hand, and an arm & hand. They then head north of the central body room, crossing a hallway into a new room. This room is made of clockwork, ticking and shifting constantly. Another door is in the Northwest corner. Trilby takes a long look and realizes that most of the room is nonsensical clockwork, serving no purpose. However, a large mass or gear in a corner isn't ticking in rhythm with the rest of the room. There's also a key-like shape shoved inside of the mass. Nothing happens as Feyli enters the room, and Hobson's Detect Magic doesn't ping the gears at all. Morenthal walks over to the key-like shape and sees that he'll need to be quick to avoid getting his hand crushed by various axles. Trilby somewhat helps by counting time, and Morenthal is able to pull the key out. The inside is highly intricate and detailed, as the entire clockwork mass begins to transform into a humanoid figure. Its arms are dangling chains attatched to the rest of the room, and it stands hunched over but is still 10 ft tall. 

*Combat Starts* against the mechanical being. Trilby summons Scrubbins in Protector mode and jabs his sword into one of the various rotating cogs within the room. Feyli activates her Archer form and fires her guiding bolt at the creature. Morenthal gets a massive hit with his purging dagger and whiffs with his other dagger. The humanoid uses its strange wire arms to animate several gears within the room to act as additional enemies. The gears immediately begin to attack the Party, grappling several members with an extra gear appearing behind them. As Coil starts his turn, the extra gear grinds into his armor, dealing damage. Fortunately, they don't have much health as Coil kills the one grappling him. Miory Wild Shapes into a tiger for attacking and survivability, and Gelnek's Marac-Star kills the gear holding him. Hobson fails to attack the gear grappling him, and Scrubbins takes a little damage to give everyone more temp HP. Trilby shoots a weak Thunderwave, but Feyli casts a Moonbeam directly on top of it. She also uses her Archer form to kill the mechanical humanoid, ending the fight. The humanoid was keeping the gears on the ceiling, which fall on everyone's head. Nothing that a Mass Cure Wounds from Feyli can't heal off, though. Unfortunately, the gears snap Trilby's shortsword in half because it isn't magical.

Morenthal opens the door to the Northwest using the key from the mechancial being, revealing an octagonal dining table. There are 8 chairs made of different materials, as are the objects on top of the table itself. Each Party member notices food & drink that speaks to their own personal tastes. The walls are covered in mirrors. Trilby doesn't immediately notice anything wrong between the mirrors and the table. Hobson, however, can tell the South mirror has illusory magic and can tell something shifts between the room and that mirror. As the Party considers if they should destroy the mirror, Miory casts Dispel Magic on the South mirror, revealing that it shows a different layout to the room. None of the Party members are reflected within the mirror. Hobson now can sense that the food is magical, though the place settings are nonmagical. Gelnek is skeptical of the Ustenki-made food. The Party begins to move items around the room to match the items within the south mirror. Thinking about it, Hobson realizes that everything within the room is pulling from the Party's memories. It takes a while to match everything up correctly, but they eventually finish. Nothing seems to happen, so the Party takes a longer look at the mirror and the room. With few other things to do, they all begin to take a seat around this family feast. Feyli refuses to sit due to what happened last time. Feyli sees that the other two mirrors still have people in the, so the Party vacates the room to make all three mirrors match. A strange wooshing sound is heard, and when they open the door again, there is a rotted human torso with its ribcage burst open. Coil returns it to the central chamber, where the head and full arm stich themselves to the torso. 

The Party continues along the north hallways, seeing another safe room before heading further north and seeing a much nicer set of double doors. They open into a small chapel or church, and it feels welcoming. Pews are in the South part of the room, and a stained glass window to the North depicts a woman with a horse in a field of wildflowers. A series of 5 basins are arraingaed around a central stone pedesstal. Various pieces of art adorn the walls. A carpet lies between the pews, which Morenthal checks immediately to find that it's a normal rug. Trilby sees that the stained glass is doing the same afterimage effect like the mirror previously. Miory uses another Dispel Magic -- she only has 1 more -- on the stained glass. Burning away the illusion reveals a skeletal figure reaping a bloody field of bodies and destruction. A raised section on the glass holds a ring within an exposed ribcage in the mural. Morenthal concludes that the basins are blood basins of some kind, and declares he isn't sacrificing his blood this time. Coil, however, takes a closer look and sees there are words in Sylvan written on each basin. Hobson is the only one who can read Sylvan, so he begins to translate them. The words appear to be mostly random sets of three words. Trilby, Gelnek, and Morenthal examine some of the art, realizing there are suspicious gaps on the walls. One such gap leads to the Northeast safe room. Doors from the safe room and the church lead back to the Library room. Miory is asked about the puzzle, but she thinks that the puzzle involves putting items within the basins. As Morenthal goes to grab the ring, Gelnek casts Protection from Evil and Good, which should protect against many creature types. Morenthal scales the stained glass and retrieves the ring. It's warm to the touch, and that spell seems to muffle Morenthal from screams from within the ring. The muffled screams from the ring are of death and despair. Hobson inspects the ring in Morenthal's palm with Detect Magic, and he can tell various spikes of magic energy are emminating off of it. He doesn't know what it is, nor how he could learn more about it.

The Party leaves the room, heading back to the central chamber, where nothing happens regarding the body. Just as quickly, they head back to the church room. Morenthal places it on the central pedestal, which also stops the screaming. Taking a more thorough look at the basins, the Party notices that there are some small rock fragments in the fifth basin. In the third basin, there's a darker stain within it. The fourth has strange splinters of some material that isn't stone. Finally, the basin puzzle is understood, as the solution is to find a word that combines with the three words to make valid compound words. The words are all objects that can be placed in the basins. The solutions are: Fire, Water, Blood, Bone, & Stone. Feyli cuts herself to fill the blood basin, Trilby pours water from his waterskin for the water basin. As they are filled, the words underneath glow blue. Gelnek & Coil go off to find bones from the bloody closets in the south. Hobson breaks off a part of a marble pew for stone using his adamantine sword. Trilby uses his tinderbox to easily light a fire that fills the basin. The ring is pushed off of the central pedestal as a capsule rises and reveals a leg. Morenthal immediately grabs the leg, hits the ring like a golf ball, and heads off to submit the leg. Coil uses a piece of cloth to pick up the ring without directlly touching it. He wraps it in plenty of cloth and puts it away. As the Party catches up with Morenthal, a group of 12 skeleton guards appear. Using an experimental system for group combat, Dan explains Group Combat: The Party starts and may use an Action or Bonus Action. DC checks are used to insta-kill the weak creatures. When the creatures attack, damage is determined by the remaining creatures and may be split across the Party. *Group Combat Begins* with Gelnek opening with a Thunderwave that destroys eight of the skeletons. Trilby takes out two more with a second Thunderwave. Feyli uses a third Thunderwave, and Hobson finishes the last one with Eldritch Blast, ending the fight as soon as it started. 

The Party goes back to go find other unexplored areas. To the west of the Northwest safe room is a large room filled with nature. The sky is overcast as sun pours through. Waves lap against a shore as the sea seems to continue indefinitely. Rock piles are around the shoreline. A severed leg sits on top of one such rock pile. Hobson vibe checks the room and detects something with Necromancy underneath the rock pile. Morenthal uses his Eye of the Nightmother to try to find something, but it doesn't detect anything. Trilby and Hobson combine their Mage Hand forces to try to grab the leg. The hands bring the leg over towards them before the ground begins to tremor. An iron door begins to shut next to Feyli as a mass of rock & debris rises out of the floor. A strange crystal in the heart of the not-Stone Talus seems to house a humanoid figure. *Combat Begins* The Talus launches one of its rock arms directly at Coil, hitting extremely hard, before going to crush Gelnek, knocking him prone. Feyli creates a Wall of Fire on top of the Talus, though it doesn't seem very effective, and she reactivates her Archer form. Coil begins to cleave into the Talus as Morenthal takes some pot shots at the crystal. Hobson tries to walk behind the Talus and takes a boulder to the back of his head for the trouble. He casts Synaptic Static, which hurts the Talus quite a bit. Trilby tries to use Vortex Warp on the humanoid within the crystal, successfully warping them to be right next to Coil. The figure is shining golden and white, and it appears femminine. She slumps to the ground, unconscious and exhausted, but the Talus is unaffected. It is, however, affected by Gelnek's Command word to "Fall." Miory heals Coil as the Talus falls to the ground, prone. Feyli misses her ranged attacks as Coil hits once. Morenthal misses his arrow shots, but Hobson hits once with an Eldritch Blast. Trilby uses Shatter against the inorganic Talus, which appears to do more damage than expected. Gelnek follows suit and also casts Shatter, and the Talus appears to finally be falling apart. Miory heals Hobson and fails to deal damage to the Talus with her staff. The Talus grabs two new boulder arms, forcing Gelnek, Coil, and Miory to make DEX saves. Feyli decides to end the fight by casting Blight on the Talus, which obliterates it. *Gelnek steps away from session here.*

Morenthal goes to inspect the strange amorphous woman as the leg is retrieved by others. The woman's hair is whispy and smoke-like, ending where the hair would end, and their body is still spectral. She is warm to the touch, and Morenthal's guess is some kind of imprisonment or suspended animation. He takes some of Trilby's water to splash the woman in the face. After a moment or two, she seems to wake up, and her eyes are a burning white and orange. She begins to stand up and lift off the ground. Hobson guesses that the woman is some kind of divine entity or spirit that was imprisoned here. Her feet also seem to fade into nothing. She walks over to the crystal and asks who freed her. Trilby introduces himself and the Party as the Flower Crowns. She proceeds to explain that she was sent by her maiden to this place of suffering. She reaches out towards the sea and pulls back some kind of vision or portal as if it were a curtain. An endless battle of blood and carnage is visible within the view. She says that it was the duty of her and her goddess to aleviate suffering, but she's doesn't have power in another deity's domain. Said wartorn domain is apparently that of the Scarlet Harvest, where suffering and bloodshed is done solely for the sake of bood and violence. Trilby asks for her name, and she says her name is **Aluria**, a servent of the Dawnmaiden. Now that she's free, she can probably return to her maiden's side. She can't do a full blessing, but she can give a parting gift to the Party. She can give a boon to a single Party member or an item to be used in dire need. In a weakned state, she can't give it to everyone. Morenthal wants to take it, and Aluria says the Party must be in agreement. She'll be fine to give the boon and also return to the Dawnmaiden. The Party agrees that Morenthal can take the boon. She floats over and places a hand on his cheek. While she can't give him light, she can still give some kind of focus. Morenthal's Sneak Attack dice are increased to d8s. Now weakened, she prepares to go back to the Dawnmaiden's side. Coil asks about the Heart of the Keep's location, and Aluria say she believes it will be at the peak of the Keep. She also implores the Party not to give into the Heart's false promises. She also says that Ophena knows the Party well. She wishes them luck, condenses into a mote of light, and fades beyond the lake.

Miory walks up to Feyli and mentions that she's surprised the Party let Morenthal have the boon. Morenthal hears that and trips Miory, who storms out of the room. Trilby deposits the leg in the central chamber as the Party heads over to the boat. Miory reminds the Party that the water ended in a wall, so swimming might need to be an option. Coil, who doesn't need to breathe, can just walk along the floor. Miory, Hobson, Gelnek, and Feyli take the boat while Morenthal and Trilby hang back. Coil sees various ruins underneath the water, which isn't surprising. He sees some dust be disturbed underwater, so he knows he's not alone. At the end of the water tunnel, there is a shrine with a waterlogged arm with some motes of light surrounding it. Those in the boat don't see anything except the light deep in the water, about 30 ft down. Coil is attacked by something, but nothing fully connects, and he can't see the attacker. Coil continues towards the arm as more things attack him but fail to hit. He grabs the arm and makes haste to head back to the starting point. The boat follows him in the backtracking. Coil is finally restrained by some kind of water force and is dragged back towards the shrine. Miory thinks something is happening underwater, but she isn't sure. So, she jumps into the water and Wild Shapes into a shark. Black ink spews from the creature as it retreats back into the water. Coil is no longer restrained, and Miory begins to escort Coil back to shore. He's unfortunately still wrapped again, but he gets a claw off on the creature. Hobson is unable to see well enough into the water to try to shoot Eldritch Blasts, so he holds his shots. Coil once again breaks free of the creature's grasp as he and Miory continue to make their way out of the water tunnel with the boat crew not far behind. Without too much additional effort, everyone makes it back to shore with the arm. Trilby gives Coil some healing bon bons as the Party make their way to the Southeast safe room to rest before placing the final limb.

The Party takes a full Long Rest, Coil & Miory dry out, and they prepare for the final push towards the Heart of the Keep. Trilby begins to start casting Identify on all of the magic items as Feyli pulls yet another card from her Deck. She pulls *The Temperance*, which results in a weird taste in her mouth, but nothing else happens. The Bowl & Spoon are revealed to be **Bowl and Spoon of Soup**, which converts normal water into any type of soup over the course of a minute. Any spilled soup becomes water once again. The Red Shortsword is **The Shortsword of Paranoia**, which has 3 charges. When attacking, a charge can be used and a d8 can be added to the attack or damage roll (???) The Gold Amulet is a **???**, which is a +1 Spellcasting focus and gives an extra use of Channel Divinity. Gelnek takes it immediately. The Purple Void Crystal Necklace has 4 charges, (???) A d4 is rolled. 1 hits normally, 2 takes half damage from an attack roll or lets the wearer reroll a saving roll, 3 is unaffected by the spell, and 4 causes the spell to fizzle out as if counterspelled. **???** The Lotus Skull allows for a Necrotic version of Guiding Bolt with a +5 to hit if a lotus petal is plucked. It has 7 uses. Coil pulls out the ring for identification, which Trilby proceeds to Identify. Gelnek does give Protection from Good and Evil to prevent anything bad happening. The ring is called the ???, which allows the wearer to see invisible creatures, but has disadvantage on CON saves. Morenthal takes the ring in the cloth, considering putting it on, or at least touching it without any protection. Trilby and Hobson think that's a dumb idea, but Morenthal touches the ring anyway. A rapid series of images of suffering flash through his mind before it ends. The ring no longer has any sort of evil pressure around it. Coil goes over to take the ring back, but Morenthal thinks the ring might be fine now. Trilby and Coil remain very unconvinced about this. Feyli casts Entangle on Morenthal, who is suddenly restrained by this. He closes his fist around the ring. Trilby is annoyed that Morenthal is being like this. Morenthal tosses the ring at Trilby, but it falls to the ground. Feyli cuts Morenthal out, intentionally dealing damage as she frees him. Miory repeats that she doesn't understand why the Party keeps Morenthal around. Coil wraps up the ring. While most others take their Long Rest, Morenthal heads into the Library to avoid the Party.

Hobson tries to figure out if there's something that has come over Morenthal, but all he can tell is that Morenthal is extremely upset for some reason. Coil sees that Morenthal is not with the Party, so he tries to talk with Morenthal. Unfortunately, Morenthal is extremely good at stealth and can't be found by Coil. He pulls out the ring with the cloth, stares at it for a long time, and leaves it on the pedestal in the Library for Morenthal if he wants to find it, which he does. Morenthal finds and picks up the ring with the cloth. He thinks about returning it back to Coil, but not everyone is sleeping, so he instead pockets it. End of Session.
