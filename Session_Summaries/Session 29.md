## Session 29: A New Development

We start just after Finch the blood mage summons the gorilla-esque fiend and Feyli walks in the door. Coil is still holding the assassin by her throat, and his Echo is behind Finch. Once the scene is set, *Combat Resumes*. The assassin pulls a knife out of her boot and slashes at Coil, who deflects the blows with his tail. Coil 1-hands his greatsword to slash the demon as his Echo hits Finch. The blood demon recklessly slams its arms into Coil and bites him once. Finch doesn't ask how big the room is, he casts a bloody fireball! Feyli activates her chalice form and heals herself to stay in the fight. The assassin girl breaks out of Coil's grasp and grabs one of her daggers from off the floor. Coil and his Echo continue to slice into the demon and Finch, but Finch casts Shield to defend himself. Finch summons bloody tentacles from his forearms to lash at Coil & Feyli. They absorb a spell slot from Feyli and heal Finch in turn. Feyli blasts the demon with a Guiding Bolt and kills it, but the assassin stabs her with a poisoned dagger. She then runs into a side room and Coil locks her in from the outside, and then he flanks Finch using his Echo. Finch gets defeated, ending the fight. The assassin is still locked in the closet and is pounding on the door. Feyli asks WTF is going on and what the Echo Coil is. Echo Coil is mildly confused as normal Coil attempts to explain. He doesn't want to kill the assassin because of the similarity he sees with Morenthal. He wants to talk her down, but Feyli is skeptical and wants to know what's going on. As Coil opens the door, she immediately attacks, so Coil grabs her again. She asks if they'll just let her go, and Coil says it depends. Since Finch paid her to kill Coil, and Finch is dead, she can't get paid anymore. Coil warns her that if she attacks him and Feyli, they won't hold back before letting her down. The assassin grabs her other dagger and says that Feyli just had to show up and ruin the fun. She leaves, and Feyli tells Coil she wants to kill her so badly. The assassin calls after that she lost her chance before running down the street.

Echo Coil asks if they're fighting anyone, and Feyli answers that they aren't because normal Coil doesn't want to. Echo Coil questions why Feyli doesn't order Coil around, and she responds that Coil is not her killing machine. They trade some snide comments before normal Coil is able to absorb Echo Coil back into himself. Feyli wants an explaination, but Coil doesn't have one for Echo Coil since it hasn't happen before. He does explain how the assassin and Finch lured him into the house, though. Coil tries to explain Echo Coil by saying that it came out of his armor and seems to be made out of the void fluid. Feyli then asks if Coil controls Echo Coil and more importantly if Echo Coil says things Coil doesn't want to say. Coil believes he has control over Echo Coil's actions, less so its thoughts. Feyli wonders if there's anyone they could ask about this and jokes that Karshala the Hag might know something. Coil says that Estavan and Moeb might know something. Feyli doesn't know how/if they should inform the Party about this. Pocket pokes her head out of Feyli's bag to break the tension. Feyli finds and gives a necklace to Pocket, who remarks that its a great distraction and is promptly distracted. Coil inspects Finch, who doesn't seem to have any possessions on him, so his spellbook was probably the only thing he owned. He asks if Feyli remembers the bounty hunter who was looking for Finch. They recall that she's at the Shattered Shield. They wonder if they could take anything as proof, but since there's nothing better, they take his head and begin to walk over to the inn.

The rest of the Party is strolling home after running some errands, and they see Coil & Feyli carrying Finch's head in the open. Hobson is at the Flower Crowns' home and sees everyone too. He runs out to ask if Feyli & Coil are okay. They say that they're fine, so everyone heads inside to deal with Finch's head. Coil mentions that they found and killed the blood mage, and Trilby gives some healing bon bons. Coil mentions that he should take the head to the inn, and everyone wants to tag along. Either for the entertainment or the need to make sure everyone's fine. Trilby says hi to Arjuun, and they find Naomi and Pierce in a booth. Coil walks over and puts the raw Finch head on the table. Naomi is relieved the Party wasn't working with Finch and says that she'll have to return the body to Westmount. If they want to head there to collect the bounty. She'll be ready in a day, and she asks if they found a book or not. Naomi doesn't care if she gets the book, but it should be destroyed. Coil opens his chest cavity to show the book, and Naomi says if anyone catches them with it, they'll probably be arrested. Coil's hand hovers over the fire. Naomi, in utter frustration, gives them 250 gp and tells them not to come with her to Westmount. She takes the head, tells Coil to burn the book, and leaves. Coil gives the reward to Gelnek who divides it evenly among everyone, leaving himself out. Coil burns the spellbook, which burns normally. After the excitement of the day, the Party heads on home and sleeps soundly. 

The morning greets them crisp and cold as snow hits the magic dome above Northcliff. Adokas knocks on the door and Trilby tells him to enter. He says that he has an announcement for the Party. Progress is being made on some of the artifacts, but the contact in Varo'El to deal with the Heart of the Keep has requested additional help. A civil war with the city and the desert folk **???** has been going on for a while. A peace treaty is about to break due to the chaos. The contact's name is **???**, and due to political reasons, only hired help can really be sent. In other unfortunate news, the Viradescent Javelin is getting repairs from the roc fight and being used. One alternative is to go by horse/cart and ship to Varo'El, which will take a month. The alternative is to go to Silverhill and take a Teleportation Circle to Varo'El, and they can use a Recall Orb to come back as needed. Northcliff doesn't have a Teleporation Circle due to the Spire, so normal teleporation is fairly touchy. Trilby asks if they have any additional info on the Heart of the Keep. Adokas takes out a scroll and explains the whole story. Back in the Champions' time, they killed a vampire lord in **Ravenmire** who had the Heart of the Keep. Whoever has the Heart now will also probably take on the aspects of a vampire lord, and the castle will be fairly random. He goes on to give advice on fighting vampires: holy water, silvered or magical weapons, holy magic, and anything good against undead. When the Heart reverts back to its form, it should not be touched with bare skin. Using the Bag of Holding they used for the Defiler's Staff is good. For resistances, vampires can use a variety of elemental magic as well as powerful necrotic damage. Finally, natural sunlight is pretty strong too. The Party accept the quest, and both they and Adokas will prepare for the trip to Silverhill. Trilby is adamant that he wants a pony as a mount, and the rest of the Party will probably get horses, but Gelnek will get an Axe Beak. The Party say they can prepare and be ready that night. 

The Party heads out into Northcliff to do preparations. Trilby intends to modify Scrubbins to shoot holy water like a water gun which will take multiple tinkering checks. Gelnek wants to get Counterspell spell scrolls to give to the Party. Scrolls will be: 1st level is 75 gp, 2nd is 150, 3rd is 300, 4th is 600, and 5th is 1000, but since Counterspell is 3rd, they start at 300 each. Trilby buys one, and Gelnek buys two. Gelnek gives them to Feyli and Morenthal. Trilby gets his shortsword silvered for 100 gp, and Morenthal gets his second dagger silvered. Holy water can be purchased at the Temple of the Dawnmaiden at 25 gp per vial. The Party buys all 10 the cleric can make in a day. Trilby takes one for himself and one for Scrubbins and Morenthal takes two. Coil holds onto the rest. Morenthal grabs three normal potions of healing for 50 gp each. Coil buys two greater potions of healing for 100 gp each. Trilby pulls from his Bag of Many Things, receiving a **Wand of Scowls**, which can make someone scowl for a few minutes. He uses it immediately 3 times and rolls a natural 1, turning instead into a Wand of Smiles. Morenthal buys another bundle of 20 arrows for 1 gp. If the Party wants to improve their magic or armor, they'll need magic items which are prohibitively expensive. However, the Void Absorption Hide Armor is transfered from Morenthal to Feyli since he's not using it. Trilby and Coil buy some silvered crossbow bolts at 10 for 100 gp, and Morenthal does the same for his bow, getting 20 arrows. Gelnek gives the Forge & Forestry his drum shield and the Obliteration Ampitheater's voucher. He can either get an elemental resistance or +2 to AC. Riona will get working on it immediately. However, this will be the first time Gelnek is without his drum in many years. With preparations complete, they tell Adokas and he tells the Party of a stable on the Northwest part of the crater (casually known as the **Crust**). A stagecoach is ready for the Party, with the Axe Beak for Gelnek and a pony for Trilby. The pony is named *Ruby*, and it should be a straight shot from Northcliff to Silverhill.

Most of the trip is uneventful as the route is well travelled. Common camping spots and fire pits are set up at regular intervals. The terrain fade into a large pine forest as the Far Reach Mountains's peaks are snowcapped. As night approaches on the 2nd day of travel, the Party sees a massive wagon circle surrounding a large bonfire. A man and a kid invite the Party to the party and they accept. Morenthal is suspcious of friendly people, though they have nothing to hide. Various stories and food are being exchanged as some simple games are played by the bonfire's light. Everyone is very inviting, and one person asks what the Party is up to, and Trilby replies that they're heading over to Sillverhill. The town is relatively closed off to commoners as a magic focused town. **Dane** says most of them are heading off to Blackfane, though some are heading back towards Northcliff. He gives advice that sticking in numbers is an important lesson on the trail. As the night wears on, Feyli, Trilby, and Coil notice that the food that was available was only eaten by the Party. The travellers didn't partake. Feyli also notices that there's a human skull in the fire. Everyone except Morenthal and Coil ate food or had drinks. Feyli asks if Coil noticed anything off with the food. He didn't notice anything, but she also mentions that there's human remains in the fire. Trilby and Gelnek respond and surprise Feyli. They talk amongst themselves about the situation. Nobody believes her about the human remains, so she wildshapes into a cat and tries to stealthily investigate. She finds that other wagons have food that the travellers are eating. She sneaks a drumstick for Trilby, who begins to eat it immediately. Hobson turns on his Detect Magic and sees that Dane pings as having magic items. He has a duster and boots that are magical. Cat Feyli walks around to eavesdrop on some people. In a more secluded wagon is an array of weapons and bloodied garments. She also notices some travellers lift their sleeves up to reveal severe burn marks. She returns to the Party and exits wildshape. She tells them that they need to go.

Exasperated, she lays it out in the open. Bloodied clothing, burn marks under the clothing, different food, and the skull in the fire. She wants to leave, but Trilby wants to keep watch overnight. Gelnek says if there are suspicions about the situation, they could leave. Feyli is fed up with the Party, so she just walks off into the woods. Everyone follows after as Gelnek & Morenthal get their mounts and reins. If the people are bad, they'll stop the Party. If not, they won't follow. Feyli walks a few hundred feet into the woods and finds a log in a clearing. Despite the cold, she doesn't notice her breath, but then she sees Coil, Hobson, and Trilby walking toward her. They agree with Feyli that they should probably go. The four of them begin to walk back. Gelnek and Morenthal are approached by Dane who asks if they're heading out. Gelnek says that a member was feeling homesick and wanted a clearing of their own. Dane then begins to help untie some of the mounts. Gelnek tells him that they noticed the different food. Dane says that they were hunting and they kept the better cuts for themselves. Gelnek insights that Dane knows that the Party knows something they shouldn't, and now there is a tension. They wish each other good travels, and the mounts are untied. The rest of the Party walk out of the woods and saddles up. They head off back on the road with Gelnek and Trilby on their respective mounts and Feyli driving the stagecoach. As they drive along during the night, Hobson, Coil, and Morenthal notice that Gelnek, Trilby, and Feyli all fell asleep. Hobson feels unnaturally drowsy but is at least still awake. Coil helps bring the sleeping party members inside the stagecoach and Hobson ties the Axe Beak and Ruby to the stagecoach. Morenthal asks if anyone knows how to drive the coach, but as he asks that, Coil notices fire light behind them that is approaching Morenthal tells everyone to get inside and he attempts to get the stagecoach moving. Coil tries, but they both fail. Hobson is able to take the reins, and has an invocation that lets him see in the dark. The fire eventually fades as the coach continues. 

Morning breaks at last as the drugged Party members wake up. Morenthal asks if the Party had learned their lesson yet. The Party confirms that they didn't. Feyli is annoyed because she noticed it first but nobody believed her. Half asleep Trilby pulls out of his Bag of Many Things, and a **Tuning Fork** falls out of the bag. As they go over the previous night's events, Coil and Feyli realize that one of the Burning Spears' rites is to burn themselves and sacrifice people to fire. In the distance, Hobson sees the familiar sight of Silverhill. A large tower sits in the middle of a lake and other bodies of water exist around. A massive cliff goes from the road to the lake. An enormous 60 ft wide bridge goes from the top of the cliff to the top of the tower. A lot of white marble and classical architecture makes up the design. Along the bridge are large golems with shield arms and arcane lights. Silverhill is known to be a mage's refuge, and the vast majority of the town's citizens are mages. The door at the end of the bridge opens as an Elf with a clipboard walks out to greet them. Trilby introduces themselves as the Flower Crowns on business from Adokas, which the Elf accepts. Silverhill will take care of the mounts and the stagecoach while they're in Varo'El. The Elf explains that they'll be brought to the Teleporation Circles and tells them not to make small talk. The Party is brought to a massive lift with glass floors. The Elf makes the elevator move down with a magical crystal. Most of the town is filled with every magical need and contraption one could think of, and libraries take up most of the space. Near the bottom is more structurally sound where more workers live and work. A windowless chamber is their stop, and the Elf leads them to a Gnome and a Human at a desk. She thanks them for coming and leaves immediately. The Gnome confirms the Party is headed off to Varo'El and asks if they've teleported before. Morenthal says no, but the Gnome says it'll be fine, just like jumping into a pool of water. Changes in the medium and temperature is weird, but it should be fine.

The Gnome pulls out a large book of glyphs, walks over to a Teleporation Circle, and inputs the glyph sequence. He tells the Party to have a nice time, though the Human reminds them about the demons in the tower. Trilby introduces himself and the Human calls himself **Hayden**. The Gnome is **Nolan**. Nolan says goodbye, snaps his fingers, and the Party is teleported to Varu'Il. A similar windowless stone room with iron bars greets them on the other side. A tanned Half-Elf arrives seconds later and opens the door, explaining that unsavory people are more likely to teleport there. The Party mentions Raya, and the Half-Elf begins to lead them out into the port city of Varu'Il. The city is near an oasis or a bay in a more tropical climate. A tall mountain has a thin trail of smoke. The buildings are mainly made of red and orange clay, contrasting with the blue sea. The city is bustling with people carrying goods all over. The Half-Elf explains that the people may look business as usual, but there is a tension in the air. In the center of the bay is a stange, alien tower: The Heart of the Keep. It has no connection to any coast, and no ship dares to approach it. The Half-Elf tells the Party to head towards the **Maelstrom**, one of the more famous taverns in town. Trilby introduces himself yet again, and the Half-Elf calls himself **Tristo**. As if on cue, the Party hears shouts and yells as a crows run past them. Many people on the street disperse or hide immediatelly. A group of Lizardfolk run in the street who were cutting down civilians. Three other Lizardfolk are mounted on dinosaurs and follow after the Lizardfolk on foot. The raiders circle around this large street as the Party prepare themselves.

*Combat Begins*. Feyli creates a Wall of Fire to cut off a third of the Lizardfolk and enters chalice form. The mounted Lizardfolk run up towards the Party, their dinos biting into Gelnek, and cast Entangle & Scorching Ray on the Party. The third one casts a sapping spell on the Party. Trilby takes out a new stuffed buddy to cast Wind Wall to buffer the Lizardfolk and ends the Entangle spell. The walking Lizardfolk run towards the Party, and one of them climbs a roof. They stab and throw javelins at the Party. Gelnek upcasts Command to hit two dinos and three Lizardfolk, telling them to "grovel" and gives Coil bardic inspiration. Morenthal stabs one Lizardfolk to death and then chugs a potion of healing. Coil summons Echo Coil behind the dinos and cleaves into it for the kill. The rest of the Party are wondering what the heck Echo Coil is, and Echo Coil winks by dimming his eye light. Hobson stabs a Lizardfolk with a Booming Blade on his shortsword. Feyli hits a dino rider with Guiding Bolt, and the other dino falls prone which throws its rider off. The third dino is behind the Wall of Flame, but it is able to climb the roof and jump off next to Trilby. The rider who was knocked off uses another sapping spell on the Party as the rider on the dead dino spews flames. The third rider also spews flames at Trilby and Morenthal. Trillby summons Scrubbins in Force Ballista mode to knock the final rider off his dino. Some Lizardfolk try to hit Echo Coil and miss, but others throw javelins at Gelnek. Gelnek uses Marockstar to kill a rider and walks towards a second rider. Morenthal slays another dino and hits the last rider for some chip damage. Coil kills another Lizardfolk and begins to beat up a second one. Hobson attacks the Lizardfolk that is laying prone in front of him, nearly killing him. Feyli finishes it off as the last dino stands up and misses Gelnek. One rider clocks Gelnek over the head and the other shoots fire at Trilby, Morenthal, and Scrubbins. Scrubbins retaliates by yanking one Lizardfolk into the river and nearly succeeds. Trilby then kills a Lizardfolk and a rider with Scorching Ray. With those kills, the remaining Lizardfolk begin to retreat, ending the fight. None of them get very far as the town guard show up. The final Lizardfolk rider surrenders and its dino follows along. 

One guard steps up and asks if they are the reinforcements that Raya told them to expect. The Party affirms and the guard tells them to hurry to the Maelstrom. Unsurprisingly it is has a major nautical theme having been built in the underside of a ship's hull. Plenty of people are inside trying to relax and enjoy the afternoon. Nobody immediately stands out as Raya, but a group of five people do stand out. They are feasting on a Monster Hunter-esque meal, but they appear exhausted and battle scarred. One is a Human woman with blond hair with a cigar and a greataxe. Another is a Half-Elf male in a robe listening. A male Water Genasi is also listening to the story. The boisterous one is an orange skinned Tiefling woman telling the story of a large monster. The final one is a female Human archer listening and joining in on the conversation. The Party realizes that this is probably the first party Adokas sent to Varo'El. Their reputation is known as **The Runebreakers**. End of Session.
