## Session 41: How's Your Notetaking?

We open in the entrance hall of Wellmore Keep, which has doors to the North, East, and West, as the South door leads back into the ocean. Their keys are on a nice keyring and appear to just be normal keys. There appear to be small runes carved into them but don't ping as magical. On the vase on the table in the center of the hall, there are little images of the Party on the outside. Feyli also notices that the flowers include the Flower Crowns' flowers. Coil checks to see how strong the doors are, and they are notably stronger than normal doors. Miori says that she'll stay in the entrance hall to watch over Oro and Otto, so the Party will explore on their own. Trilby walks forward and opens the North door with the red key. This first room is a trophy room filled with heads of creatures across the planes. A fireplace crackles warmly, and an alchemical rack with several vials sits on the mantle. Across from the fireplace are 8 dragon heads arranged in a semicircle. Trilby investigates the vials and sees a note. It requests using the correct polish on the correct dragon head and warns of injuries if instructions are not followed. The vials have various elemental types and are arranged in the following order: Fire, Ice, Lightning, Thunder, Acid, Poison, Radiant, and Necrotic. The dragon heads are of actual dragon types in the following order; Red, Blue, Black, White, Green, Brass, Copper, and Gold. Gelnek has the idea that the breath elements may be associated with the polish types, however the metallic ones are too similar for him. He definitely knows red is Fire. Hobson thinks about historic tales about dragons. A guest at his family inn mentioned a friendly Brass dragon and the Fire it breathed. Morenthal decides to ignore the puzzle and uses the white key on the one East door.

It is a smaller room, about 15 ft square and dimly lit by an arcane light in the center. The walls are simple and stone. Under the light is a small pedestal and basin with a slowly rotating dagger floating above it. One door leads out of the chamber to the East. Recalling what happened last time, the Party shuns the room in favor of the dragon puzzle. Trilby doesn't remember the colors of dragons, he just recalls that they're smart. Coil manages to recall that the Party recently fought a Green dragon with Poison breath. Feyli recognizes most of the basic dragons. White breathes Cold, Blue dragons breathe Lightning. The others she's not confident enough to say. Feyli takes the Lightning polish and pours its contents over the Blue dragon. The dragon head melts and shriveles into a dragon skull instead of the full head, and the vial refills to full. Trilby pours the frozen Cold vial on the White dragon head. Gelnek does it a third time with Fire to Red. Coil uses the swampy Poison one onto the Green for a fourth success. Hobson puts on himself to try Fire on the Brass dragon, which is correct. Copper, Gold, and Black dragons remain to be "polished." Morenthal and Trilby both don't know anything that hasn't been established. Hobson decides to ask Valse, who is impressively helpful. Valse recalls a Gold Dragon friend who breathes Fire. Valse also confidently says that he hasn't heard of any Radiant breathing dragons before. Hobson proceeds to pour Fire onto the Gold, and that works great. Without any more knowledge amongst them, they ask Miori, but she only remembers the Green dragons breathing Poison. Coil and Morenthal guess that of the remaining answers, Acid would go with the Black dragon, but they don't want to guess it. Gelnek is brave enough to take that chance, and Black dragons do indeed breathe Acid. The Party discusses what the Copper one could be, and Morenthal finally decides to follow the trend of putting Fire on the metallic dragons. He fails a DEX save and is sprayed by Acid. He proceeds to move into the East room and pout. Coil takes the Acid vial and pours on the Copper dragon. 

The skull's mouth opens and drops a key. It is a simple iron key with glitter in its tip. Rather than a ring on its end, it has a red ruby that is similar to the gauntlet on Hobson's arm. That hand actually can't pick up the key, but his non-gauntlet hand can pick it up fine. Morenthal, now in the dagger & basin room, notices carved numbers 1 to 30 on the basin's rim. He asks Coil if the vials in the dragon room are still there and full, which they are. Morenthal attempts to bring the Radiant vial into the basin room, but it empties just as it crosses the door threshold. Morenthal cries out that it's not fair, as Gelnek takes a closer look at the pedestal and basin, which is fairly simple in construction. He takes the dagger, the door seals and traps Hobson, Coil, Morenthal, and Gelnek in the room. The numbers on the basin glow and count down from 30, and the walls shake. Morenthal pours some holy water which immediately evaporates and resets the countdown. Morenthal proceeds to take the dagger and makes small cuts on himself to get blood into the basin. The blood evaporates and resets the counter. Gelnek makes the same effect happen with water. Morenthal says that they still need blood, so at 10 seconds, he keeps putting a bit more blood into the basin. Coil and Hobson are free to look around the room, and Coil notices that the walls aren't actually moving. Morenthal realizes what that could mean and lets the countdown fall to zero. The room unseals, and the basin splits in half to reveal a new golden key. Coil also notice that there's a carved 'N' in the room. Trilby heads back to the dragon room and sees that one of the dragon pedestals has an 'O' on it. He then goes back to the starting room and finds another 'O' under the nice table. Miori watches the Kua Toa boys playing jacks.

Hobson goes to use the ruby key on the East door of the basin room. This room appears to be an inn with figures going about their business and grabbing food and drinks. This is $\color{brown}{\textbf{Bavender's}}$, run by Hobson's mother $\color{teal}{\textbf{Dorotny}}$. A group of adventurers head to a corner table and a centaur couple walk to a larger table. Hobson is utterly dumbstruck and heads to the bar. Morenthal can't figure out what's up with the room, but Coil manages to reach out and pass his hand through a visitor, revealing it to be an illusion. Hobson greets his mom, who asks about the Party. The Party is a little confused, but Trilby says hi, and Morenthal asks where the bar is. Dorotny says it's right outside of Maple. Hobson keeps giving introductions and slowly is registering that something is wacky. Dorotny can talk later, but she hands the Party a menu and goes to talk to other patrons. Hobson recommends the Clam Bisque but says everything is correct in terms of items, but the costs are different. He also focuses to notice the patrons and room ping with Illusion magic. He's sad that it's an illusion, but he's proud of the inn that his family runs. Trilby tries to order some Clam Bisque, as Hobson looks around to catch any details that might be off. In fact, he recognizes everyone in the inn is someone he's served. Thinking harder, the menu is also wrong in both items and prices. Morenthal wonders if they'll fight whatever they order, so they better not get the Tarrasque Skirt Steak. Hobson suggests the corn if that is indeed the case. Feyli waves down a waiter, and the Party orders the six "safe" items on the menu. It takes seconds for the illusion to manifest the food, which the server places in front of the Party. As expected, the food is completely fake, though it smells incredible. Gelnek orders an appetizer version of the Hydra Tenderloin. Morenthal asks the server if they have a secret menu, and they do, but the Party has to say it specifically. Hobson's thought is to take the price of the dish and index that into the menu. The server looks over and mentions that the menu is all out of order. Feyli figures it out: "Scarecrow." The server takes a green key from her apron and places it on the table. They also look for a letter in the room. Dorotny's blouse, which normally has "DB" instead has a 'Y'. There is also a room to the South of Bavender's. The Party uses the green key on that door.

Feyli opens the door to a small crypt that has been overgrown. The center of the room has four graves in a row, and a corpse in the corner has a shovel. One grave has a tombstone while three others are off to the side and laying down. The upright tombstone has a glittering ruby embedded in it. The tombstones appear to have writing that has long since faded. Morenthal goes to take the shovel, but the corpse animates. His name is $\color{Gray}{\textit{Dampe}}$, and he has forgotten where the other three tombstones go. Said tombstones have a Jade, Topaz, and Sapphire on them. If the Party helps, then Dampe will give a shiny reward. He needs to recall which tombstone goes with which corpse. He recalls that $\color{Gray}{\textit{Franklin}}$ was in the 4th grave. $\color{Gray}{\textit{Gregory}}$'s grave is left of the Jade tombstone. $\color{Gray}{\textit{Hubert}}$ has a Topaz on his gravestone. $\color{Gray}{\textit{Timothy}}$ is not on the end and is to the right of the Sapphire. The 1st grave doesn't have the Topaz. The Ruby tombstone is correct and in the 3rd slot. Feyli says the exact answer as she was thinking allowed: Sapphire, Topaz, Ruby and Jade; Gregory, Hubert, Timothy, and Franklin. A crow cries and drops a key directly in front of her. It appears to be made of cogwork pieces with a small green glow on its end. Neither Feyli nor Coil can pick up the key, but Trilby is able to. There is no doorway out of the graveyard. They say goodbye to a now inanimate Dampe, and Feyli sees another 'Y' carved into the shovel. The Party returns to the starting room as they are otherwise dead ended. Trilby uses his key on the West door from the entrance hall.

The room is filled with materials and books, including one full wall bookshelf. A table with tinkering tools sits under an arcane light. Trilby recognizes this as his workshop at home. However, there is an unknown device resting on the workshop table. It is a metal bowl supported by 4 beams that may move in some way. It has a lever with 9 cylinders in its handle that may light up. On top of the bowl is a 3x3 grid of circular slots, about the size of 9 cylinders resting beside it. Below the bowl is a small spindle. Trilby confirms that the library has a book on dragons. Trilby picks up two cylinders and realizes they have different weights, though they are the same size. Trilby goes through the cylinders to figure out the lightest one and puts it into the top left corner of the grid. One light on the lever glows. Trilby moves it to the top center and the 2nd light on the lever glows. Morenthal tries to move the lever, but it doesn't budge. Trilby proceeds to try to sort the cylinders by weight. The lightest and heaviest are easy, but after that, it becomes hard. Trilby goes and grabs a scale that glows with runes. He labels them in weight from 1 to 9 and inserts them all. The lever lights up, and Morenthal pulls it. The bowl is lowered onto the small spindle and tips over, spilling the cylinders. The bowl resets back onto the four legs. Coil surmises that the outer edges have to balance out. Trilby asks to roll for a hint, and Dan Jones obliges but he gets to declare Disadvantage on one future Trilby roll. Trilby accepts and is told that the center is 5. Morenthal figures out that since 5 is in the middle, number pairs add up to 10, so each side has to have 15. The solution Trilby gets, helped by Morenthal by a lot, is 2-9-4; 7-5-3; 6-1-8. The bowl rests on the pedestal with a click and the bowl opens to reveal another blue key. There is a door to the West, and the Party uses the blue key on it.

Inside this one is a classroom with 6 desks and basic wooden furniture. A piece of paper is on each desk, and there is a blackboard with "Final Exam" written on it. A spectral professor stands next to the blackboard and says that once everyone takes a seat, they can proceed. The Party takes their seats. Nobody is allowed to talk or share answers, and a certain grade must be made lest there be consequences. There are 20 questions, and the players DM Dan Jones their answers. The Party needed 16/20 to pass. Only Coil, Trilby, and Morenthal succeeded. The lowest score happened to be 5/20. As the test ends, the papers burn into nothingness, and on the blackboard's tray is a white key. The teacher dissolves. There is a door to the North, and the letter in the room is a 'I' near a flag of the Empire of Orlon. They use the gold key on the door.

A wave of heat blasts the Party, and the room is an enormous sauna. In the center is a large pit of embers and coals with a boulder on top of it. Along the sides of the room is an 'E'. As everyone enters, an iron lattice gate slams down and the boulder begins to animate as a giant reptilian creature wakes up and prepares to attack the Party. *Combat begins.* Coil enters his rage and steps onto the fiery coals, taking a little Fire damage. It does feel like it'll get hotter towards the center, so he steps back. He shoots a crossbow at it, though the lack of magic means it does less damage. Trilby summons Scrubbins in Protector Mode, before he holds Scrubbins, ready to chuck it into the monster's mouth when it roars. The monster sees the Party is bunched up, so it curls itself up and rolls into the Party, who take DEX saves. Feyli, Hobson, and Gelnek fail, taking massive damage. Morenthal hits with his offhand silvered dagger. Gelnek buffs the entire Party with Bless, and runs away from the monster. Hobson moves away, provoking the attack of opportunity, and uses Synaptic Static on it. The monster, impressively, saves. Feyli enters archer form and casts Blight on the monster and hits with the follow up. Coil can't slash through the tough hide, as Trilby awaits to chuck Scrubbins into the monster's mouth as Scrubbins gives out more temp HP. The monster attacks Coil and Morenthal with his claws and bites Feyli. The bite allows Trilby to attempt to throw Scrubbins, and he misses. Coil absorbs some of Feyli's damage. Morenthal once again hits with just his offhand dagger. Gelnek steps a bit closer to heal the Party minus Scrubbins with Mass Cure Wounds. Hobson moves further out of the way and uses Synaptic Static, which the monster fails. Feyli casts Insect Plague on the monster, who is a big chunky guy, so it's not super effective. She also hits with the follow up archer shot. Coil cleaves into the monster, getting the killing blow, ending the combat. It explodes into fire. A large fist-sized ruby falls to the ground, and a green key falls behind Morenthal. Trilby heals up Feyli and Hobson with the Trilbee.

Coil picks up the ruby, which has been cut into a near-perfect sphere. It does not appear to be magical, but it is likely quite valuable. The Party heads back to the starting room to open the door East of the room. Miori watches over the Kua Toa twins who are sleeping. They use the green key. The room has a hill with a tall berry tree and four wells. There are a pair of corpses. One has a wooden mug and the other has a vial. There is a wooden bucket near the tree, and the tree has a carving of a woman cupping her hands as if to drink. Glancing into the wells, they all have water but the fourth has red berries floating in it. Feyli confirms that the berries are safe, but she wants to test Trilby. He also confirms that they're safe. Morenthal finds the letter 'R' in a heart carved into the tree. Feyli does a quick smell test for poison. The North well has the berries, but the South well is definitely poisoned. The other ones could be poisoned without a smell or they might be safe. Feyli goes to test and drink the North well. She fails the CON save and takes a ton of Poison damage and 3 levels of Exhaustion. Coil helps her rest next to the tree as Gelnek casts Greater Restoration on her, which clears the effects. And that's good, because Feyli could feel the effects getting worse. Hobson decides to drink from the West well, but before that, the Party considers taking a rest so that Gelnek can recover Greater Restoration. They don't, and Trilby investigates the corpses. There appears to be a faint powdery film on the interior of the vial. Trilby takes a sample of the West well water and pours it out to see if there's any residue remaining. It will require the water to evaporate, so it will take some time. Morenthal takes the wooden mug from the East well, and he notices something move within the water. Trilby says that if something is alive in the water, then it's probably not poisoned, however nobody is brave enough to take a swig. Coil sticks his hand into the water and wiggles his fingers around. After a minute, a small fish nips his finger and swims away. He's not sure if they're normal fish. Hobson can tell they're common freshwater fish, and with that assurance in hand, he takes a drink from the East well. It's fine water.

Feyli pours some water from the Eastern well into the cupped hands of the tree carving. A section of the tree splits open and reveals a small obsidian key with a red cord wrapped around it. Coil picks up his key. Before choosing the next key and door, the Party decides to take a Long Rest in the entrance hall. End of Session.
