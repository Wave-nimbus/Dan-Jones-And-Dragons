## Session 25: The Gala Massacre

We open back in the Gala at the Wick Estate with Preston & Coil on the main floor of the estate. They do not see the Party members, but Coil sees that nearly every noble at the Gala has the scribbles from the Magic Key over their face. There's a Drow Woman near one of the staircases, a large Male Human stepping out of the smoking room with a pipe, Theodore, and his teen guest without the scribbles. We cut back the the remaining Flower Crowns in the vault as they see Chuck walking towards them. Trilby & Hobson greet him, as Chuck accuses them of attempting to steal something from rich people. Trilby & Hobson attempt to deny it, but Chuck says he also was there to steal things too, so they can all remain chill. Trilby points out the trapped four-armed woman to Chuck, but Chuck has zero idea what that is. After a few quips (including Feyli pretending she never met him), Chuck begins to leave, and nobody in the Party stop him. Meanwhile, Coil and Preston have descended into the wine cellar and see Chuck leave the open vault. He explains to Coil & Preston how to get through the vault, but Coil drags Chuck along by his neck. The three of them make their way over to the rest of the Party. They all agree that the Party needs to leave immediately, which could be difficult due to Morenthal being trapped. Hobson's Detect Magic sees that Chuck has an illusion over him. Coil notices that Morenthal has scribbles over his face, and Feyli has a different pattern of scribbles over her face. Trilby considers using a food cart to get Morenthal out, but he's not sure that would work. Gelnek is asked if he could dispel the magic cage around Morenthal. He casts Dispel Magic at fourth level, and Morenthal is freed. However, he has no memory of what happened while he was frozen. He's told the Party needs to leave, and he asks if they should free the lady. As they debate, Coil is asked if Morenthal had any strange experiences. He says no, and Preston says that they killed Mr. Wick, so they should go. Hobson retrieves the Pause plaque from his Bag of Holding just in case they need to refreeze the lady. Gelnek then uses Dispel Magic at third level to clear the prison around the lady.

As she is freed, she is heard saying "It's not here", before noticing the Party. (???) She is a divine being from the city of **Mechanis**, and the Magic Key was created by her master, so it was not supposed to end up in mortal hands. She wants to take the Key back to her master. The Party want to discuss it, and she gives them a moment to do so. Hobson notices that Chuck has been silent and fiddling with a ring on his finger. They all ask about Chuck, and Hobson asks him to turn off the illusion. Chuck says that he can't since his master cast it. Hobson casts Dispel Magic, and desite it not being automatic, he succeeds. Chuck's disguise fades away, revealing him to be Josh from the Crystal United Mercenaries. The Party go back to discuss what to do with the Key. They quickly agree that she could petition the Council in Northcliff to see if they can get the Key back. Coil asks the lady if the Magic Key has a way to undo its abilities. She replies that it indeed does have those abilities, since anyone can see how what has been tampered in a person. She also points out that Morenthal & Feyli have scribbles, and Coil concurrs. However, she also confirms that Feyli's memory was affected by something other than the Key. Coil thinks he should undo the memory tampering. Morenthal refuses, but Feyli accepts. Coil touches the key to Feyli's arm and time freezes for him. The world seems to blur into a datascape or timeline of every memory she ever had. Along that timeline, Coil sees a sort of "corruption," where a period of time is marked such that Feyli would not remember the events. It goes from when Feyli was still in the Lotus until soon after meeting Coil. Coil recalls his conversation with Horace Devinshire aboout tampering with Feyli's memories. Coil doesn't see memories of Horace outside of the corrupted memories. He debates with himself about whether undoing this is her actual will, as she is ignorant of it. The Black Hand is still around, but since the Midnight Lotus is gone, it may be safe for him to undo it. He aattempts to undo the corruption without looking at the memories. However, he will require the occasional glance to undo it correctly. He proceeds with reversing the corrupted memory.

He sees a conversation with **The Second** (???) It ends with Horace giving Feyli the **Music Box** and tells her to keep checking it for a few months. He does remember that Horace mentioned that the Music Box is done. And as he thinks that he's done, Coil is zoomed out of Feyli's memories. Feyli remembers everything. Specifically, she remembers that she did, indeed, take **The First's Heart**. The Second had found the Flower of Rebirth and had attempted a ritual to resurrect the First. And it worked. He was reborn into an almost pupal form. Feyli then stole the Heart and fled across Orlon. She threw the Heart away near Maple(??) and fled over to Springoak. She eventually learned about Horace's powers and he helped her forget about everything. Of note, the vision seen when Feyli was reincarnated showed Karashala the Hag has now retrieved the Heart. Feyli immediately goes quiet but confirms that the memory alteration has fixed. It is now Morenthal's turn, but he continues to refuse. Coil says that he'll have to do it the hard way, and he lets go of Josh. Josh immediately grabs the key, but he fails to yank it out of Coil's grasp. Feyli notices it and fires a Guiding Bolt at Josh, hitting him and staggering him away from the Party. He backs off from the group. Trilby takes off his hat where Scrubbins has pooped. Scrubbins hops over and keeps an eye on Josh. The four-armed lady has only been focused on the Key. Coil then turns back to Morenthal to use the Key on him, as Gelnek somehow is able to grapple Morenthal and hold him down. Coil gives a quick nod of appreciation before using the Key on Morenthal. Instead of memories marked for "deletion", there are instead several that are marked for "insertion." Coil attempts to clear them out without snooping at memories. Since he is more comfortable with the process, he can clear memories of Mr. Wick "saving" Morenthal through his life. He doesn't see any specific memories though.
