## Session 37: Don't Forget to Double Tap

We open during combat as the Flower Crowns fend off the Steel Tempest from the Viradecent Javelin. *Combat Resumes.* Feyli races onto the top deck and gets Ardwill back up before entering her Archer starry form. A drake rakes into Gelnek & Hobson, and another goes after Geo & Coil. The last one flies to starboard to shoot a line of lightning at the back line of fighters, knocking Karva to the deck. Hobson summons Valse in werewolf form to start tearing things up. Trilby gets Karva back up and has Scrubbins shoot one leader off the port side. A different leader jumps behind Feyli but misses his attacks, and the other goes for Morenthal & Miori. Morenthal uncannily dodges that attack to half the critical damage. The crew makes little headway against the raiders, but Coil cleaves into a drake and summons Goo Coil. Gelnek uses his second Channel Divinity to heal the other Flower Crowns for 10 each, and uses Mass Healing Word on Miori, Morenthal, Pricilla, Feyli, and Gelnek. Miori manages to get an Earthbind on the overboard drake, taking it out of the fight. The raiders miss all their attacks, but Feyli heavily damages another drake. Coil, Trilby, and Feyli combine attacks to kill it on its retreat. The other drake moves away to blast a line of lightning along the starboard edge. Hobson and Valse fail to hit any of the remaining raiders. Trilby and Scrubbins clear out one more captain. The last captain knocks Gelnek down to the deck only to be healed immediately by Miori. The two Coils cleave into the raiders, who hang on by a thread. Gelnek summons Spirit Guardians around himself, appearing as mini goblin versions of the Flower Crowns, killing two raiders and the final drake. Morenthal tears into the final captain while the last two henchmen make decisions to fight or retreat. One takes a swing at Coil while the other takes a wand from the captain, presumably retreating. Feyli nearly kills the captain with another Guiding Bolt plus archer combo. Hobson shoots the retreating henchman as Valse kills the captain and nearly kills that henchman. Trilby knocks the henchman off with a Thunderwave, but that raider did grab the wand, and he is still alive. The last raider doesn't last long in the face of the crew and the Party. Morenthal decides to kill the guy who was falling away with an arrow shot. Feyli obliterates the raider on deck with Blight, ending the fight.

The Javelin crew makes sure everyone is fine, and the Party is able to get a Long Rest before they arrive in Springoak. A valley after the Far Reach Mountains sees a large city with the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Merchant's Pass}}$ and $\color{brown}{\textbf{Guilded Lane}}$ trade routes passing through it. Snow has fallen on the city and surrounding forest. The main castle houses the Royal Family of Eldwoods and is situated on the Eastern side. As the Viradecent Javelin lands on the outskirts, children and teens take peeks from behind walls. Azru tells Ardwill, Vespie, Geo, and Pricilla, and to stay on the ship while the others head off to town. Miori tells the Party that she'll go with Karva to visit her family and to reach out if the Party needs her. Azru says that it'll be about a day before they're ready to go on a normal schedule. He offers any location services, and he suggests the $\color{coral}{\textbf{Fine Rock Inn}}$ for news. The $\color{coral}{\textbf{White Tower}}$ in the North of town has $\color{tan}{\textbf{Horus}}$ if they have magical needs. The Flower Crowns decide to head to the Tower first. As they walk along, they notice suspended arcane lights that light the town at night. The Tower itself is about 100 ft tall with vines climbing from the ground, and there are a handful of windows. The door has a large silver lion door knocker on the front. Hobson recalls that there were probably messages sent between Springoak and Silverhill between older wizards. Coil knocks on the door, and the silver lion animates, saying Horace isn't expecting any visitors, so what's the visit? Coil says the Flower Crowns want to talk. The lion's eyes roll 180 degrees momentarily before returning and welcoming the Flower Crowns inside. Horace will be down shortly, and the door opens. Inside is a large library with scrolls & tomes of all kinds. Floating stones hover between the floor and next 30 ft above. An enormous tome 6 ft tall sits on a lectern surrounded by candles for light. A big cauldron of soup is being stirred by an animated ladle as other animated objects keep the Tower tidy. Horace Devinshire hurries down the spiral staircase and welcomes the Party, asking what he can do for them.

Coil explains that a powerful necromancer is still alive and asks if there's any news on that person. Horace says he hasn't heard anything in the city itself, but he has a dedicated scrying & location room upstairs. Hobson has the spell Scrying, and Horace asks for a favor trade. Trilby says that they want to know the favor now, and Horace says that there's a magic golem that has gone a little haywire. It was built to resist magic, and since Horace wants the workshop space back, he wants the Party to help disable it. He's willing to wait until after the Party's business is concluded. That is fine with the Party, so everyone heads upstairs to the scrying room. They head past Horace's bedroom, kitchen, and some storage on the way to the top of the Tower. A skylight and walls have a kind of colorful texture, and a special chair sits in the center. Horace explains that the spell will be notably more powerful and may cause a headache due to information overload. He also explains how the focus chamber works to Feyli, who casts Scrying. The similar zoom out and zoom in animation happens again as the spell focuses on a cave in the southern district of Springoak. Inside the cave is a small altar where the Second is leaned over it. Bones and bodies lie around him. A small shimmering door stands nearby and a small vial hanging from his neck glows. The Second senses something in his paranoia and hides the vial. There doesn't appear to be anyone else. Feyli ends the spell, and receives a little cloudiness in her mind for a couple minutes. She stays silent for a while.

Morenthal eventually asks if she knows where the Second is. Feyli says she thinks she does. Hobson asks if they should hurry, but Feyli doesn't know, but the Second is definitely paranoid. Coil thinks they should go take him down now, and the Party agrees. Horace sees them out and reminds them of the favor. The Party considers asking for more specific directions but instead decides to go to the forest and look for the cave. Feyli uses Wildshape to become a hummingbird to assist in the search. After a couple hours, Feyli and Hobson locate the cave entrance that is slightly obscured by nature. The Party convenes outside and debates whether to be sneaky or not. Trilby and Morenthal want to go quiet, and as Hobson thinks over it, Feyli wildshapes into a bear. Gelnek thus declares that the Party will go in loud. Feyli goes in first as a bear, and Coil follows immediately afterward. The first chamber of the cave used to be some creature's den, but there are plenty of battle marks. A number of skeletal creatures are sitting on the ground. One is holding a chestplate. The skeletons all see Feyli, and the one with the chestplate shakes it, creating a very loud rattling sound. 

*Combat Starts.* The rest of the Party is about 10 ft outside the cave, so Morenthal runs inside to find the skeletons. He obliterates one with his Purging Dagger, but it grabs Morenthal to explode into bone shards. Trilby runs in next and gets a flamethrowing Scrubbins onto the field before casting Shatter on the remaining skeletons. Three begin to explode, rushing towards Feyli and Morenthal, but they don't cause damage. Coil heads in and runs up to be next to the two remaining skeletons. Gelnek uses Sacred Flame on the skeleton without the breastplate, leaving only the alarm maker left. It attacks Coil for chip damage before bear Feyli kills it. It gets a dying bite on the bear before it dies. *Combat Ends* as a shimmering door leading deeper into the cave dissipates. The room beyond will have the Second. Coil is next to the entrance and can see more skeletons trained on their position. Morenthal uses the Eye of the Nightmother to detect the number of undead. He counts 9 undead and a surge of energy around who is presumably the Second. He relays this to the Party, and Gelnek is very ready to die on this mission. Right before Coil runs in, Gelnek casts Bless on the entire Party. Trilby also switches Scrubbins into Protector form, which buffs the Party further. With all that prep, the Party rushes into the room. The Second is hunched over a stone altar, one hand up, seemingly casting a spell. His figure is even more emaciated than they previously saw him. He recognizes the Party but not Feyli in wildshape. He asks where their friend Feyli is. The Party does not respond, but the Second says she'll meet them soon enough. Angering a god of death within a tomb is a foolish idea. The Second raises his spellbook and *Combat begins* again.

Morenthal rushes the Second for a big ol' critical hit, but he's not considered undead. The Second causes Morenthal, Feyli, Coil, and Gelnek to make WIS saves, managing to get Hold Person on Morenthal and Gelnek. Hobson attempts to summon Treant Valse, but the Second uses Counterspell to fizzle it out. Coil summons Goo Coil behind the Second and kills two skeletons by himself. Half the skeletons head over to the paralyzed folks, tearing into Morenthal and Gelnek. Bear Feyli starts killing the skeleton attacking Gelnek just as Gelnek breaks out of Hold Person. The remaining skeletons attack Morenthal, Coil, and Feyli. Trilby uses Scorching Ray on the skeletons next to Feyli and directs Scrubbins to move closer to Morenthal. The Second casts Invisibility on himself, dropping concentration on Hold Person, and a bunch of new skeletal creatures rise up. Hobson summons Treant Valse for real this time, and he smashes one skeleton into the ground. Coil moves over to help kill the skeletons around Morenthal, killing two. The remaining others knock out Morenthal and bite into Coil, Valse, and Feyli. Bear Feyli misses her attacks as Gelnek uses Summon Celestial, which appears as a large spirit of Chief Stompy. The little skeletons manage to knock Feyli out of her wildshape. Trilby rushes over to Morenthal and gives Morenthal a Cure Wounds bonbon. Morenthal stands up and uses the Eye of the Nightmother to pinpoint where the Second is. The Second moves away from Valse, eating the hit, and lines up a shot to hit Stompy, Feyli, Gelnek, and Hobson with a Lightning Bolt for big damage. Hobson loses concentration on the summoned Valse, leaving him with only cantrips left. His Eldritch Blast manages to pierce the Second's attempt at Shield, killing him. As he falls, he did plead to The First to save him, but nothing happens.

All that remain are the skeletons summoned during the fight. They go after everyone, and one manages to kill Goo Coil. Gelnek uses a Channel Divinity charge, splitting between primarily Feyli and Stompy, and he uses another Mass Healing Word to the Party. The little skeletons attack several Party members, and Coil absorbs some damage heading towards Feyli. Trilby gives Coil an Morenthal temp HP from Scrubbins before backing away to cast Fireball on the group of skeletons surrounding them, and he kills two off of that. Morenthal kills another skeleton and prepares to fight another one. The Second's body begins to glow in a green light. His skull rises up out of his skin, and his arm raises too, and he's now some kind of undead. Dan J. also reveals that the Second was raised by one of Feyli's Deck pulls. The Second walks to the center of the room, eating an attack from Stompy. His jaw extends unnaturally wide, screaming and causing damage to everyone other than Hobson. Gelnek and Feyli are reduced to 0 HP and knocked unconscious due to the Banshee's scream. Hobson force feeds Gelnek a healing potion. Coil summons Goo Coil and goes up to the Second to start attacking. The Skeletons don't relent on their attacks. Gelnek holds Marakstar to his head in both hands and calls upon Divine Intervention to Ather the Breaker. The call goes unanswered, so Gelnek uses another Mass Healing Word on everyone. The small skeletons chip away at the Party. Trilby sends Scorching Rays at the Second, only hitting once. The Second takes a Legendary Action to force Morenthal to make a WIS save. A black rune appears above his head as he is cursed to have disadvantage on all attack rolls until the curse ends. He goes up and attacks the Second, flanking with Coil. The Second retaliates with blackened smoke, blinding Morenthal. He then sends tendrils of smoke to attack Trilby, Morenthal, and Coil. Trilby and Coil have their life energy sapped from them, but Trilby takes less due to his Necrotic resistant armor.

Hobson kills a little skeleton, trying to clear out the riffraff. The Second takes another LA to reduce everyone's max HP by 15 other than Hobson, who resisted it. Coil continues to attack the Second while the remaining skeletons attack the Party. Feyli goes down from those unrelenting attacks. Gelnek uses another Mass Healing Word and casts Death Ward on Feyli. The small skeletons continue to chip away at the little HP the Party has left. Trilby uses a Thunderwave on the skeleton next to him. Morenthal uses a Potion of Lesser Restoration to clear the Blindness and gets his attack on the Second. The Second recharges his Banshee scream, knocking Morenthal to the ground as everyone else saves. Hobson shoots a weak Eldritch Blast at the Second, who attempts and fails to curse Coil. Goo Coil is brought back again to kill a skeleton as Coil recklessly attacks the Second. Another round of skelly attacks lays into the Party. Feyli heals herself as Gelnek uses Spirit Guardians to help clear out things nearby. The Guardians destroy the skeletons harassing him and Feyli, and he runs up to the Second. This kills another two skeletons. The Second takes another LA to blind those around him, getting Scrubbins and Gelnek. Trilby gets Morenthal back on his feet, taking a skeleton swipe for his troubles, but Scrubbins gives much needed temp HP to everyone. Morenthal gets back up and stabs the Second. The Second saps the vitality of Coil and Morenthal, knocking Morenthal back down. Hobson uses more Eldritch Blasts, and The Second causes another wave of energy to reduce the Party's max HP again. Coil cleaves into the Second again and calls to Feyli to give him the key. The last major skeleton attacks Trilby as Feyli misses a Guiding Bolt. She walks over to Coil and unlocks Coil's jaw with the key. Gelnek's Spirit Guardians kill the major skeleton, and he uses his final Mass Healing Word.

The final little skeleton goes after Trilby and gets obliterated by Spirit Guardians immediately after. Trilby misses with a Ray of Frost as Morenthal continues to stab the Second. The Second causes more CON saves for Morenthal, Coil, and Gelnek, absorbing energy from Gelnek and Morenthal. Hobson misses more Eldritch Blasts as the Second takes another LA to reduce more Party members' max HP. Unhinged Coil goes at the Second, and Feyli creates a tiny Wall of Fire on his space alone, but the Second has a special avoidance and legendary resistance. Gelnek misses his own Guiding Bolt and heals Morenthal a little bit. Trilby hits with a Ray of Frost, and Morenthal continues to stab away, and he discovers that nonmagical weapons do nothing, regardless of silver. The Second attempts and fails to absorb health from the Party, and he avoids the Wall of Fire. Hobson misses twice, and Coil continues to slice into the Second. Feyli uses a very precise Call Lightning on the Second, who has to burn his final Legendary Resistance. Gelnek adds one to the "missed Guiding Bolt" tally. The Second takes an LA to try to reduce the Party's max HP, but they all resist it. Trilby uses the Wand of Smiles on the Second, which does nothing. Morenthal misses with his Purging Dagger, which means he does nothing. The Second continues to hang on, resisting the Spirit Guardians, and absorbing energy from Morenthal and Gelnek, sending them both down. Hobson finally hits a single Eldritch Blast as Coil gets a single hit with his sword. Feyli calls another lightning strike on the Second. Trilby gets Morenthal back up off the floor and heads over to Gelnek's body. The Second fails to absorb energy, and Hobson hits one blast. Coil hits once, and Feyli gets Gelnek up while also blasting the Second with a lightning bolt. Gelnek gets the final kill on the Second with the one and only Guiding Bolt to connect this entire fight.

The Second dissipates, and the sickly green glow fades away, and his necklace falls to the ground. Unfortunately, Coil is still unchained. End of Session.
