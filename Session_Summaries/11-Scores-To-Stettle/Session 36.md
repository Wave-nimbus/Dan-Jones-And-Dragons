## Session 36: What do You Fight For?

(*Note: Dan F. was absent and Nichole was late to this session*)
We open back in Lucian's estate as the Party was exploring the pocket dimension with many doors just as Lucian arrives. He walks up to Hobson who is holding one of the books and asks what the Party is doing inside. They responded that they were worried for his safety and were exploring to see if he was alright. Trilby mentions the vision he wanted to talk to Lucian about. Lucian replies that he's fine and was alerted to his house being broken into while he was out for groceries, and so he returned. Morenthal tries to leave by saying there's an important meeting. Lucian closes the door that leads back to his estate and asks for clarification on Trilby's vision. Trilby does so, while Morenthal desperately tries to figure out how to leave. Once finished, Lucian asks for the specific time of the Spire's energy spike, and Trilby relays it. He thinks while taking the book out of Hobson's hands. Lucian says that when Adokas gave Lucian the Heart of the Keep to return to the Vaults of Eversteel, the energy spike would've happened about the same time he left the pocket dimension. He speculates that the pocket dimensions of the Bag of Holding & this study area meant that the Heart wasn't affecting the Spire's barrier. When the Heart was brought back to the same plane, that caused the energy spike. Coil asks if Lucian has experienced this before, but Lucian hasn't. He plans on checking on the relics by taking out a large amber crystal on a chain. He removes it from the chain, and it expands into a china cabinet. Inside are the recovered Artifacts: The Defiler's Staff, $\color{tan}{\textbf{The Seed of Rebirth}}$, The Heart of the Keep, and Ring of Flame. Lucian doesn't think anything is wrong, and the Party agrees that everything seems fine. Lucian shrinks the crystal back to necklace size and decides to hide it better than just on his person. 

He then goes back to asking why the Party decided to invade Lucian's personal space in the name of finding Lucian. Trilby rambles about just wandering around through doors and getting into trouble while profusely apologizing for the intrusion. Lucian thanks them for worrying, opens the door, and ushers the Party to leave. Everyone rushes out the door. On the way out, Lucian asks if the Party found anything else. Morenthal has been dying to know what's behind the light door, since "Trilby" thought it might've been Lucian. Lucian says that it is a creature from the Shadowfel that is useful for his research. He keeps it behind a Wall of Force, though he could banish it back whenever he wants. The Party is escorted back out the front door as Lucian tells them to find a different way to contact him if they need him quickly, such as a Sending spell. He then closes the door in their face. The Party head on back to the Hall of the Flower Crowns, more than a little on edge given Lucian is scary. Morenthal notices a child-like figure watching them as they enter their neighborhood.
(*Nichole returns here.*)

The Party is feeling paranoid at this point, so Coil and Hobson give a comb over of the estate, Hobson using Detect Magic. Nothing is found. Morenthal says that the only weird thing is the kid, which worries the others a bit. Coil talks about Lucian's notes on the Flower Crowns and his concern that they mention the Planar Mirror specifically. Morenthal and Gelnek speculate that Lucian might be interested in getting the Mirror for himself. The Party realizes they don't know much about Lucian, so Trilby goes to their library to look him up. Lucian is apparently a previous member of royalty from the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Kingdom of Fywood}}$, an elven kingdom in Orlon. The kingdom is independent and on good relations with the Empire of Orlon. He seems like a good sort, so the Party decide to just keep an eye open rather than telling people about it. The Party relaxes in Northcliff as they make lunch, and they consider what they could do. Morenthal wants to find some kind of potted plant to take care of, and he buys one for 1 gp. He also asks Miori if there's a way to take care of it while the Party is away. Miori suggests using the Little Guy, the neighbors, but she eventually just carves a rune that will do it. While she's alone with Morenthal tending to the plant, she asks why the Party just brushed off Trilby's vision so easily. Morenthal says that Trilby says weird things all the time, but Miori asks what the line between saying weird things and trying to convey important information is. Morenthal says that life and death situations are different than everyday random occurences. Miroi asks if there's a fine line between the two. Morenthal sarcastically replies that Miori is a diviner of visions, but she replies it's more about trusting friends. But she can tell Morenthal is new to that as well. He doesn't have an answer to that.

Miori stands and gives some last minute advice about the plant before heading towards the door. Morenthal thanks her. She says that the Party has already accepted him, so growing closer shouldn't be a problem in her opinion. He thanks her again as she leaves. Coil goes over to Feyli and asks if she's okay given the Second is still alive. She feigns that she's fine, and Coil asks if the Second has abilities to avoid death. Given that he's a very strong necromancer, him avoiding death is unsurprising. She didn't believe the rumors that it was possible, else the cult would've brought back the people they said they would've. They're not sure what to do now, but they expect that the Second might be restarting his cult. The Party goes back to wondering what to do now. Trilby goes off to the Forge and Forestry to pick up his sword before heading off to Bifar's house. Bifar greets Trilby who relays the entire story about the Heart of the Keep. Nothing's really happened to Bifar, and he wants to become an adventurer after he finishes school. Trilby asks if Bifar wants a $\color{tan}{\textbf{Tuning Fork}}$ from the Bag of Many Things, but Bifar is hesitant about planar travel. Trilby didn't realize it was for planar travel. Trilby then gifts the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Pair of Shackles}}$ also from the bag, and Bifar takes it. Bifar has been working on something as well. There's a pair of leather gloves with large disk gems on them. He puts them on, creates some runic shapes and animates a wooden bird in his hands. Trilby is thoroughly impressed by what Bifar made. Trilby also suggests that Bifar finds friends that he can rely on when he begins adventuring. Bifar also emphasizes that Trilby's vision about him being jealous couldn't be further from the truth. Trilby also rolls the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Two D10s}}$ (mistaken as d6's) that was gifted a while ago.

Coil goes over to check on Blade Goldwill, who is currently resting and reading a book. They catch up and talk about various experiences. Coil apologizes for defeating Blade, but he's glad to be of his own mind again. He thinks that he would defeat Coil in a duel. Blade then asks Coil how the Flower Crowns treat him. Coil says they treat him well and fairly, not as property. Blade says that's good, and he likes the shopkeep that humanizes him as well. Coil extends the invitation for Blade to help the Party if they ever need it. Blade agrees, given that they're friends at this point. Coil gives Blade the address of their house for when the Party calls on him and/or they want to duel. They talk for a while afterward. Trilby meanwhile, researches how Tuning Forks for planar travel work. They function as foci for teleportation. Trilby decides to use Identify on the Metal Tuning Fork he's had for a while. This one is attuned to the Elemental Plane of Air. He might need to use Identify on other Bag of Many Things items. He also remembers to pull from the bag today. He pulls out $\color{gray}{\textit{22 gp}}$. Feyli decides to pull a card from her Deck of Cards. She pulls out *The Hanged Man* with a small hummingbird on a post in the background. As the card burns away, a spectral hummingbird lands on Feyli's shoulder, serving as a 1-time familiar. She is granted proficiency/expertise on Arcana while it is out, but when it is shunted to its safe space, the effect goes away.

Sometime later, Gelnek clears an area in the yard of their house and calls for the Party and Miori outside. Since Gelnek had his class swap, he claims to have had a crisis of faith regarding how to interpret Aether the Breaker's teachings through the Flower Crowns' lens. As a result, he wants to know what flame of passion and altruism burns in each Party Member. Gelnek selects Morenthal to go first, inviting him to come close. Gelnek asks outside of a better tomorrow, what is Morenthal fighting for. Morenthal guesses he fights for survival. Gelnek says that's good, but there has to be something worth surviving for. Morenthal says that he fights for his freedom. Now that he has said what he's fighting for, Gelnek calls on Morenthal to strike before Aether. Morenthal is baffled given Gelnek's speech, but he goes to stab Gelnek. Gelnek slaps his arm away and proceeds to smack talk Morenthal. It takes 3 swings before he connects. Trilby and Feyli immediately heal Gelnek, and Gelnek gives Morenthal a `B-`. Trilby is next. He used to fight for his family's money, but now he fights for the adventure, knowledge, and strength gained from the Party. Gelnek approves and calls for the hit, which Trilby throws a weak punch. Gelnek is disappointed in the punch, so he wants magic instead. Trilby doesn't hesitate this time, so he casts Fireball. Gelnek gets blasted and subsequently healed by Trilby and Feyli again. Trilby gets an `A`. Gelnek looks to Coil and Feyli, but Hobson (controlled by Dan J) chooses to go next.

Hobson's reason to fight is skimmed over, but the attacks are a pair of Eldritch Blasts, which both miss. Gelnek knows that these blasts can do a lot better, so he initially gives a `C-`. Hobson is disappointed in the result but chooses to see it as a sign to improve. Feyli nods at Coil to go next, and so he does. Gelnek mentally braces himself for the attack as he asks what Coil fights for. Coil responds that he doesn't know, but he fights to be useful and find purpose. Gelnek calls for the stronger attacks, as he is supposed to be an existential threat. Coil gets a very solid hit as Trilby and Feyli continue their healing jobs. Coil gets an `A+`. Gelnek attempts to shoot a Guiding Bolt at Coil, but he misses completely. Miori doesn't want to be last, so she goes next as Gelnek tops off on healing. She fights against the injustices of the world having previously fought to protect her town. Her attack requires a WIS save, which Gelnek passes easily. With the Polymorph failing, she instead uses Erupting Earth on him, landing a solid hit. Miori gives some healing as Gelnek gives an `A-` grade. Feyli is up last. She says that everything she's fought for has died, so she doesn't want to choose anything. Gelnek believes having a purpose to fight for is worth it in the eyes of Aether, so even if something is at risk of dying, it's worth it. Feyli responds that she doesn't want to put those she cares about at risk. Gelnek says it's about protection, not risk. Feyli says she's failed to protect everyone she's cared about before casting Wall of Fire and burning Gelnek. She asks what Gelnek fights for. Gelnek says that this current exercise isn't about what he fights for but about what Feyli fights for. Feyli responds by casting Insect Plague and asks again what Gelnek fights for.

Gelnek casts Death Ward on himself and walks toward Feyli as he gives his answer. He fights for the Party, that he might have something to protect, for his new family, for the legacy of the world to continue. Feyli angrily responds about what happens when none of it works and everyone else is dead. Gelnek says he'll continue the fight until he is no more, because he believes there is always a chance, no matter how slim, to protect things. Feyli relents but says that she never said she stopped believing. It just never worked. She heals him. He knows that Feyli's answer came from a place of passion and heart, so she gets an `A+`. She's very confused, but Gelnek explains that her magic comes from deep emotions, despair and love. Feyli doesn't know what to say, so Gelnek continues. He says that her past is littered with the bodies of both enemies and loved ones, so she is strong and chosen by Aether for surviving all of that. Feyli starts crying, and Gelnek and Coil go to comfort her. Gelnek says the lesson is concluded and goes up to the house to recover. Trilby flexes his muscles, feeling proud of himself. Coil didn't realize that the others could choose to hit each other and asks if they want to hit him. Morenthal and Feyli immediately object. Coil says that it could be fun, and he asked them to. Gelnek overhears this, and runs back. He proposed the idea of sparring matches between the Flower Crowns for training. Hobson speaks up that the Crossed Swords also has sparring matches available. But back to hitting Coil. Trilby says he could fire a shot from Scrubbins, which he does. Coil is hit and slides back 5 ft, and he appreciates the hit. He'll accept being hit again. Miori asks if this whole attacking each other thing is new or not. Mornthal says it's new and walks back inside.

Trilby chucks Scrubbins at Coil and detonates him, hitting Coil, Trilby, Hobson, and Miori. Only Coil fails, though. Miori and Coil are baffled by this development, but Trilby says he's fine to rebuild Scrubbins. This also reminds Trilby about fighting the Party in the Heart of the Keep and pointedly says nobody has opened up about it. Nobody speaks up, so he goes inside. Miori goes over to Coil and asks if he's alright, and Coil says he's fine and happy to fight. Miori says there seems to be something contradictory about fighting friends. The end of the day arrives, and everyone gets some well-deserved rest. Coil reminds the Party that the Second is alive again, so they may need to check in at Lafford. At some point, they should also go find $\color{tan}{\textbf{Karashala}}$ to get Gelnek's soul back. Feyli at least knows that Karashala is nowhere near Filgrove right now. Magic users can maybe try to track down the Second with location spells. Feyli decides to us Scrying on the Second. Feyli is very familiar with the Second, so she is able to scry on him. She performs the spell in the garden, near the pond, and her eyes go completely white as she connects. She gets a quick vision of the planet zooming out before zooming back in on Springoak.

She can see a cellar of some kind with a single candle. Someone is hunched over a book and scrawling notes from or into a spellbook. The Second is much more emaciated with facial features more resembling an undead than the living. He's constantly muttering to himself, and it lasts for the duration of the spell. As it ends, Feyli says that he seemed panicked, and the Second is acting strangely. This is probably the best time to confront him, but something is definitely wrong. The Party recalls that there was a rather unpleasant necromancy tome left behind in Lafford when the Midnight Lotus's base was set on fire. Feyli pieces together that said book was probably the Second's original spellbook, so he is now making a new one. She says that the Party should get going. They all agree to go murder the Second again. They head off to the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Viridescent Javelin}}$ where $\color{tan}{\textbf{Azru}}$ asks about their destination. Trilby says it's Springoak, and so the airship heads further north for multiple days. Miori is impressed by the airship and gets the tour from $\color{tan}{\textbf{Marm}}$. She's introduced to the crew and says she's available if ever needed. She also talks with $\color{tan}{\textbf{Karva}}$ multiple times during the voyage. Gelnek gets advice from Feyli about how to do healing. Trilby pulls once from his Bag of Many Things, taking out a red vial that changes into a yellow color when swirled. It is a $\color{lightgreen}{\textbf{Potion of Fire Breathing}}$. It lets the drinker breathe fire in a 30 ft cone three times, wearing off after an hour. 

Sometime during the voyage, Coil approaches Morenthal. Coil noticed that Morenthal wasn't a fan of people hitting Coil. Morenthal just doesn't see the point, since the point of combat is to inflict harm. Coil says that it can also be used for good, for defense. Morenthal doesn't see why or how fighting each other achieves anything. Coil says that it helps strengthen the Party and learn about how each other fight. Morenthal says that's a lie to justify the desire to hurt others, and he doesn't want to hurt the Party. Coil says that he might enjoy it, but he doesn't want Morenthal to join in if he doesn't want to. Morenthal says he doesn't want to hurt the Party if he can help it. Coil is built to take and inflict damage while Morenthal only is built to inflict it. Not fighting is how Morenthal is going to be expanding his horizons, and Morenthal isn't sure why Coil gets out of that expectation. Morenthal hates the fighting because his entire life revolved around infighting and trying to be the strongest. He doesn't want that anymore. Coil understands. Morenthal says that fighting is a tool to be sharpened, but he doesn't want to sharpen it on Coil. Coil thanks Morenthal for explaining just as Azru calls all hands on deck. On deck, there are multiple drakes carrying humanoids that are going to be attacking. Azru calls them the $\color{aqua}{\textbf{Steel Tempest}}$. Each blue drake has three humanoids on it and yellow paint to resemble pirate symbols.

*Combat Begins.* Hobson (controlled by JoCat) runs towards the top deck and casts Synaptic Static on one of the drakes and its passengers. Trilby uses Expeditious Retreat to run from the bottom deck to the top deck and summons Scrubbins in Force Ballista mode. Scrubbins shoots at one of the other drakes' passengers, shooting him off to fall to his death. The drake captains take crossbow shots, hitting Trilby, Hobson, and Ardwill. Ardwill misses a ballista shot, Karva misses with an Ice Knife, Miori fails on Earthbind, and $\color{tan}{\textbf{Geo}}$, $\color{tan}{\textbf{Priscilla}}$, and Coil run along the middle deck. Marm and $\color{tan}{\textbf{Vespi}}$ keep the engine running. Gelnek fails to cast Command: Flee on one of the captains. Morenthal runs from the lower to upper deck. Some pirates take crossbow shots at those on deck while others jump onto the deck to attack with scimitars. The drakes land on the ship and proceed to wreak havoc. Hobson uses Eldritch Blast to blast a drake while Scrubbins nearly blasts a pirate overboard. Trilby uses Thunderwave on one boarding party, sending the overboard pirate to his doom. The captains lay into Trilby, Ardwill, and Gelnek. The Javelin crew get on deck while Miori wildshapes into a giant tiger. Coil rages and lays into the captain attacking Trilby. Gelnek uses Channel Divinity: Preserve Life on Trilby before running up to cast Healing Word on Ardwill. The pirate goons knock Ardwill out again. Feyli's turn will be up next. End of Session.
