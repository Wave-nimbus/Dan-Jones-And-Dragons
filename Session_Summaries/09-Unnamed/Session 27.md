## Session 27: A Place To Call Their Own.

We open as the Flower Crowns begin their home shopping. After looking at a number of properties, they are approached by Delila Wintersbane and a different Succubus woman. Delila introduces herself and $\color{teal}{\textbf{Melitha}}$. She has heard that the Party is looking for some real estate, and she is acquainted with a major realtor of note: Melitha. Delila lets Melitha take over with the Party, who asks her about what they want in a home. As she asks, she is telepathically able to detect that Feyli thinks she's hot. Regardless, she knows about a place on the East side of Northcliff that would serve well. Delila also mentions that the fees Melitha needs are waived as another gift from the Council. After looking, the Party decides to listen to Melitha. We then cut back to Moeb's Pawn Shop where Coil, Morenthal, Moeb, and Estevan have successfully revived Blade Goldwill. He asks Coil where he is, and Coil responds that they're all in Northcliff. Blade realizes that he's been revived and asks if Mr. Wick is dead, which Coil confirms. Blade recalls that Coil told him that Mr. Wick was using something against Blade. Thus, he is willing to hear Coil out about what was happening. Coil tells him about the Shaper of Minds, and Blade mutters that it explains a lot. Blade is also surprised that Morenthal is not Coil's owner but rather a companion. He then asks if anything can be done to reverse the effects. Coil recalls that the Council has a way to reverse the effects. Blade, not able to trust his own memories, agrees to ask the Council for help. However, if Coil and Morenthal are messing with him, then he'll kill them outright. Alternatively, a powerful Cleric could reverse the effects with Greater Restoration. Since Blade hasn't been to Northcliff and to hold them accountable, he asks Coil & Morenthal to lead the way. Before they go, however, Coil takes out $\color{tan}{\textbf{Bartholomew}}$ and asks Estevan if something could be made so he can walk on his own. Estevan may need Moeb's help and some time, but he'll probably be able to make something. Bartholomew will hang out with the two of them. Coil, Morenthal, and Blade all head off to the Crystal Circle to find a Cleric.

The square is fairly busy with various prayerful and clergy people going into and out of many of the temples. However, some are full and some are empty.$\color{teal}{\textbf{Xybris}}$ the Laughing Mask's temple is vandalized and mostly empty, and nobody really cares about his temple. As the three of them are looking around, an elderly Gnome man walks up to them and asks if they need anything. Coil mentions that they need a cast of Greater Restoration, and the Gnome directs them towards the temple of Ophena. They head on in, and the temple looks exactly like a fantasy church would look. They are approached by $\color{tan}{\textbf{Becket Maxwell}}$ who asks if they need treatment. Coil repeats the need for Greater Restoration for Blade, and it will cost about 450gp. Blade, having worked for a noble, pays it easily. Becket leads them into his office and he asks them about the specifics of what to look for with the spell. Coil mentions that there was some memory tampering, and Becket completes the spell. Blade nods and remembers what happened with Mr. Wick. Coil then turns to Morenthal and asks if Morenthal wants the same treatment to confirm that the memory tampering is gone. Morenthal agrees to get the spell. Blade pays 200gp of the cost as thanks, Morenthal pays 100gp, and Coil pays the other 150. Becket resets the ritual circle for the spell and does the spell again. Nothing changed. Morenthal looks at Coil, the start of tears in his eyes, and asks to leave. Becket escorts them out, and the three of them leave. Coil says Blade is free now, but jobless. Blade says that his previous employer wasn't much better than Mr. Wick. For now, he'll look for some lodgings while he looks for work. The three of them head off to the Shattered Shield, and Blade gets a room. Coil puts his hand on Morenthal's shoulder and Morenthal apologizes for not trusting Coil. They're glad that the Shaper of Minds is no longer on the Material Plane, and Morenthal never wants to see it again.

Some time later as Feyli enters the Shattered Shield, she sees a Drow woman talking with Arjuun. As he sees Feyli, he points her out to the Drow woman, happy to help. Feyli leaves the inn The woman introduces herself as $\color{teal}{\textbf{Naomi}}$ who's been looking for the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Westmon}}$ mage. Arjuun had told her that Feyli had maybe seen the mage. Naomi wants Feyli to turn over any personal items that the mage has. Feyli doesn't have the book, Trilby does, so she says she doesn't have anything. She also asks what the mage did. Naomi explains that the mage was researching forbidden magic at Westmont and killed a few other people before going on the run. Naomi wants any leads on the mage, and Feyli says that they can talk to Trilby. However, Feyli needs to talk to him first since she calls Trilby skittish. Naomi will meet them in the Shattered Shield tonight. She leaves but Feyli overhears a tinny voice talking to Naomi. The voice knows Feyli is lying, but Naomi can't do anything about it for now. Feyli joins Naomi, and since it's almost 3 PM, Feyli says that Trilby is probably sleeping. Naomi's sword is in another chair. Naomi pulls out a wanted poster for Finch Ironbellow, and he's wanted for using blood magic illegally. He's ran through Duskern and avoided spreading Ashen Rot, and most people who've encountered him have faced his wrath. Feyli confirms that Finch knows that he's been doing this willingly. She also asks if there's any personal items that they should look for. Naomi mentions that only Finch's spellbook would be the thing he has left, since Finch would've dropped anything behind. Feyli also notices that Naomi glances at the sword every once in a while. Feyli takes out Pocket, who was napping, and introduces her to Naomi. Pocket is extremely willing to help and points to a random Dwarf as the suspect. Feyli gives the Dwarf a tankard before letting Pocket go back to sleep.

Trilby gets up because he's hungry. He comes down stairs and Feyli calls him Fava Bean and drags him upstairs to explain the situation. Feyli doesn't trust Naomi, so she says Trilby shouldn't give her Finch's spellbook. Feyli knocks on Hobson's door to explain the situation. Hobson gets up and opens the door to listen to Feyli. Feyli tells Hobson to be called Trilby, to act scared stiff, and say nothing. Trilby will go by Fava Bean. If Naomi asks, they found nothing of the Finch's possessions. The three of them head back downstairs. Feyli introduces Naomi to Hobson, who says that they didn't find anything. Feyli also says that the three of them didn't find anything. Naomi is sad but asks Hobson and Trilby that if they find anything, they should tell Naomi. Finch is dangerous, and Naomi wants to prevent anything bad from happening, including any deaths. Trilby says that the sword is cool, and the sword glows. Naomi says it's named $\color{teal}{\textbf{Pierce Ironheart}}$, and she can introduce them to him. She unsheathes it, which looks exactly like the Darkmoon Greatsword from Dark Souls. It comes to life and Trilby introduces himself as Fava Bean. As they make small talk, Pierce tells various stories from before he became a sword. Ironically, he used a mace when he was a person before being cursed. They try to get Pierce to eat Trilby's hashbrowns. Gelnek walks into this conversation at this point. He is immediately interested in Pierce the talking sword. Gelnek asks to hear the story of Pierce, who claims to have gone to the Hells with under the banner of *Feos*. There, he was cursed by a pit fiend named *Zathabule the Promised Nightmare*. When Zathabule traveled to the Material plane, he was left behind and was eventually found by Naomi. Whether this story is true or not is unknown, but Gelnek wants to make a song or ballad about Pierce. About this time, Coil and Morenthal walk in as well. 

Morenthal asks what's been going on. Feyli says they've been talking to Pierce the sword. Morenthal asks Feyli if she and Trilby found anything. Feyli grabs Hobson, and the two of them deny finding anything. She also asks Trilby (calling him Fava Bean) if he wants anything else. The three of them are trying very hard to keep up the charade. Morenthal plays along for a second. Coil, completely baffled, has no idea what's going on and says that Hobson isn't Trilby. Gelnek is also trying to figure out what's going on, since he wasn't in on the joke. Naomi stands up at this point and calls out the Party for lying. Feyli tries to cover it up one more time, but Naomi is done with this deception. She says that if the Party isn't saying anything about Finch, and Finch hurts anyone, their blood is on the Party's hands. Morenthal asks why Hobson and Trilby were lying too. Naomi is exasperated, so she sheathes Pierce and goes to her room. Morenthal goes over to the table and asks Feyli to explain herself. Feyli explains that Naomi is looking for Finch's spellbook, which Trilby has. Morenthal wonders why they weren't honest with Naomi. Feyli can't really explain, but she goes upstairs to sleep off the alcohol. Trilby says that if Morenthal trusts the bounty hunter, he can go get the spellbook. Morenthal points out that a blood mage without a spellbook probably is willing to use blood magic to get the spellbook back. Trilby weighs the options of handing over the book vs holding onto it, as do the rest of the Party. Since Coil doesn't have any blood, he should be the one to hold onto the book. The Party goes upstairs, Trilby grabs the spellbook, and Coil puts it in his chest cavity. Trilby then wants to go back to sleep. Later at night, Blade is talking with Arjuun at the bar and Naomi is sitting at a side table. Hobson goes to Arjuun to ask for the hashbrown recipe, which he gets. 

In the morning, the Flower Crowns are greeted by Kasma in the inn. She begins to walk them towards the Spire's Shadow district and retells how the Council created a small committee dedicated to finding the Artifacts. She wants them to meet $\color{teal}{\textbf{Lucian Geldran}}$, another person who was in charge of helping search for the Artifacts. He made the orbs to find the Artifacts or traces of their magic. She brings the Party over to his residence to explain the situation about the Crystal United Mercenaries. His house is a quaint condo near the Spire with massive windows and black brick. Kasma rings the doorbell and the Party meets Lucian: an elderly Elven man with a slight limp and a cane. His left arm is heavily bandaged, and while not often used, it still has its strength. He greets them and beckons them inside to a large sitting room. A ghostly butler delivers cups of tea to everyone. Lucian asks what he can do for them, but he assumes they want to know about the Mercenaries. Josh was initially turned away by Lucian before the Flower Crowns retrieved the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Staff of Defilement}}$. Lucian had a lunch meeting with Josh's father Thadius, and was reluctantly forced to give over the approximate locations of the Wand of Chaos and the *Gauntlet of the Eye*. The Gauntlet has yet to be retrieved, but through what was probably a ton of money, the Mercenaries found the Wand. Fortunately, this means that finding the Wand has been done, so now they have to take it back. Unfortunately, the Mercenaries are a formidable group, so taking it will be no small task. Lucian considers putting more effort into discovering the identities of the other members. He warns that the De Rosas have very deep pockets, so they should expect more attempts on the Party's life. For the crew of the Viridescent Javelin, the Council's coin should ensure their allegiance, even if it wasn't needed. The crewmember who bailed, Grayton, has had a warrant put on him. Hobson asks if there is anything they should worry about regarding the Wand. Lucian says that the Wand is just as a danger to the user as it is anyone else. Trilby asks after the Gauntlet, but Lucian only relays what is already known: the Gauntlet is somewhere in the Desert of Thorns. Lucian says it is possible to get a meeting with Thadius, but the Party doesn't really want that, which is the correct answer. If the Party has no other questions, Kasma wants a moment with Lucian to talk in private. A small servant's bell is placed nearby if the Party needs anything.

Trilby rings the bell and introduces himself to a ghostly butler that shows up. The butler does not respond, but Trilby recognizes this as an Unseen Servant spell. Trilby says the tea is delicious before dismissing the butler. He wants one for the Flower Crowns' house. Coil asks what they should do now, but without any leads on Artifacts or the Mercenaries, they can't do a whole lot. Hobson asks if the Party has gotten anything owned by the Mercenaries, he could attempt to scry on them. Trying to rob them seems like a bad idea, but if they ever do any heist plans, they should do it in secret. Pocket pipes up from within the bag and says that they should steal from Lucian. The Party shoots down the idea, and Hobson points out that the magic artifact mage probably knows how to keep things from being stolen. Conversation can still be heard, so the Party considers eavesdropping. Pocket walks over and Feyli wildshapes into a ladybug to listen in. About 30 seconds in, however, they realize this conversation is literally just talking about random stuff. The Party just sits there and waits for Kasma and Lucian to be done talking. Hobson had been looking around and sees the 7 colors of Void Crystals -- blue, purple, red, green, yellow, orange, and white -- in a glass case over the fireplace mantle. Feyli is still a ladybug at this point, and Lucian notices that Feyli isn't here. Feyli exits as a ladybug, ends her wildshape, and knocks on the door. Lucian lets her in before saying that he'll pass along any information to them. They all leave, and Kasma says that while it wasn't super helpful, at least they know how the Mercenaries got the lead on the Wand. She also says that it is very difficult to say no to Thadius. She'll also try to upgrade security on the Viridescent Javelin to prevent teleportation.

As they head back to the Shattered Shield Inn, they see Melitha waiting for them. She has news that the Flower Crowns' estate is ready to showcase. They all head off to see it. Melitha leads them out to the Eastern district near the Croyfair river and some of the last forests on Northcliff. Some of the Party want to experience home again and others for the first time. Various flowers adorn the outside of the upstairs windows. A covered patio with a swinging bench is in the front. The house is currently called the $\color{coral}{\textbf{Hall of the Flower Crowns}}$, though it could change later. Each member has their own room. The sitting room has a magical fireplace with comfy chairs. The kitchen table seats eight, and the kitchen itself has a roaring fire with a grill. A full pantry and a tea & coffee maker sit next to a staircase to a small cellar. There is a library with common books of both fiction and non-fiction. A large table serves as a war table, and a map of E'lythia is spread out on top of it. There is an interior garden with a tall tree filling much of the greenhouse-like roof. A small pond, tall enough to stand in, has a few fish inside it. Various smaller flowerbeds and greenery exist. Almost everything in the house is controlled by magic, simulating modern conveniences. Upstairs, a storage room has equipment for training, which can be used in the yard outside. Each Party member also has their own room, specified to each member's wants and desires. <br>
Hobson's is a basic but cozy bedroom. There's a reading nook, a shelf of Valse's desires, cushions, and blankets. <br>
Gelnek has a large rug with Ustenki patterns on it. Ethically sourced beast pelts and bones are used for furnishings. It looks like a Monster Hunter room, filled with patchwork things. There are also Ustenki instruments like drums, danger bongos, and horns. <br>
Morental's room is extremely stripped down. He has a new fluffy bed and a window with roof access. A closet with equipment and a prayer candle to the Nightmother. A small potted plant sits on a shelf for the Little Guy. The window also leads to a balcony that looks over the interior garden. <br>
Coil's room is surprisingly nice. A large window is on the far side, and Voedian architecture is apparent. A telescope points out the window. A large space for him to rest is available. A small space for polishing and sharpening weapons & himself is also in the room. Voedian architecture is heavily reliant on triangles. <br>
Feyli's room is fairly elegant and simple. There's an iron canopy with vines and fabric for decoration. There's a painting of the river where she and Aryc would talk. She also has a telescope, and the furniture is mostly a dark cherry. There is also furnishings for Pocket, Soley the Rat, and the lizard pet. There also appears to be a plush of a generic Warforged. <br>
Trilby's room is extremely similar to his room in Filgrove, but with a workshop in mind. Most of the room is actually a workshop, filled with shelves and benches. His bed is lofted above a desk, and there's a perch for Scrubbins. A childhood rug with cobblestone roads and carts is on the ground.

After the Party has explored the house, they talk with Melitha and express their amazement at how well the rooms fit their desires. Thanks to some creation and transmutation magics, Melitha and her staff were able to whip it up in a night. Morenthal is a little suspicious since he doesn't understand how Melitha knew everything. Coil asks, and she lets them know that she read basic desires. After giving them all a rundown of the estate, she leaves a business card behind, the Party thanks them, and she leaves. As per $\color{tan}{\textbf{Ustenki}}$ tradition, after a rousing successful achievement, the Party must feast! Gelnek heads down to the cellar to search for food to make a feast. Since it was on his mind, there's a pig and chicken, and with the grill, he's going to make kabobs. Hobson is put in charge of cutting meats, Coil will move kabobs onto the grill, and Trilby will ensure the heat of the fire is perfect. Gelnek will supervise as he's a master chef. Morenthal is on taste test duty, though Gelnek will join in too. Soon enough, the meal is completed, and the Flower Crowns eat their first meal in their new home. Some time after the meal, Hobson is in the library. He realizes that he's reading a book on the Fey. Valse wants to take this time to explain the whole situation with the portal at Champion's Hall. He starts with the faction categories of the Feywild: The $\color{cyan}{\textbf{Seelie Court}}$, the $\color{cyan}{\textbf{Unseelie Court}}$, and the $\color{cyan}{\textbf{Wildfey}}$. Valse was part of the Seelie Court, presumably a high up one, and he made a contract to travel to the Material Plane. In short, Valse was tasked with getting a statue to serve as a beacon to and from the Feywild. If it was created and then destroyed, then Valse is held responsible. However, since Valse is within the gauntlet, how this contract works is fuzzy. Contracts with the Seelie are only good for the Court since they get what they want either way. Broken contracts allow them to cause punishment to happen. With this in mind, Hobson asks Valse what to do about it. Avoiding them forever is the best, but probably not an option. If Valse returns to an actual form, it might be worth trying to wiggle in the gray space of the contract since he wasn't fully himself. Hobson resolves to help Valse figure out how to fix this when it happens. Until then, Hobson will have to try to research the fey further. At the very least, Hobson and Valse haven't been dragged back to the fey, so that's a good sign.

As the Party prepares to go to bed, Coil pulls out the vial of dark ooze, feels a pull of familiarity, and opens it up. He pours it onto his hand. There's an instinctual warmth as it seeps into the armor. As it fills him completely, Coil's vision goes white. He gets a vision. He stands in golden light, surrounded by clouds of many colors. He is on a platform, leaning on a railing made of a golden metal. In his hand, he's holding an ornate glass of liquid. He can't focus on anything too far away. A humanoid figure approaches him, wearing a shining robe. They're talking and gesturing, but Coil can't really hear it well initially, but he focuses. He hears a little as the figure says that "the answer was not with our Nine but with $\color{teal}{\textbf{Obrius}}$". The figure grabs Coil's arm and turns him back to the party. The vision cuts back through the blinding sunlight. A new vision forms, and Coil feels empty inside. He is inside a darkened stone room. He sees a vat of the liquid with pipes going from it to the wall, one of which is connected to Coil's back. Nearby, there is a table with an arcane light. Coil feels for his muzzle, but there doesn't seem to be one. He attempts to rip the pipe out, discovering that it twists out. Coil attempts to look inside a hatch of himself and sees that he is filled with the black ichor. He goes over to the desk, which has a letter, a book, and a familiar key. The book is titled "Maintenance and Regulation after the Siphon". As he reads that title, he wakes back up in his room. Thinking back on it, he realizes that the dark room reminds him of a room in the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Underdark}}$, where he first encountered the black ichor. For once, Coil is going to find a journal to take notes of the vision.

Around 8 PM, there's a knock at the front door, and Hobson answers. Uncle Pancreas is at the door. He heard that Trilby bought a house from his coworkers, and he wanted to see if it was true. Trilby is unfortunately sleeping at this point. Pancreas has been listening to gossip and wants to be an informant for the Flower Crowns, at least for commoner rumors. Hobson insights that Pancreas genuinely wants to help out, so Hobson takes the notes that Pancreas had. Hopefully, he can help Trilby and the rest of the Party with finding rumors and gossip. Hobson also offers to give a cut of any rewards, and while Pancreas can't accept directly, they'll say if any scoops were useful. They thank each other and Pancreas goes off on his own. The Party sleeps peacefully before Gelnek shouts that the first night of the Obliteration Amphitheater Tourney is tonight. It'll be a 3-round tourney, with two rounds that evening and the final on the next day. Trilby pulls a $\color{lightgreen}{\textbf{Pair of Shackles}}$ from his Bag of Many Things. It has no key, but they are otherwise ordinary. Nobody has any major plans for the day or afternoon, so everyone prepares for the tourney. Gelnek trains, Trilby makes signs & posters, and Hobson makes hashbrowns. Coil does a quick spar with Gelnek, but Coil is still distracted from his visions. Gelenk takes this as a sign from $\color{tan}{\textbf{Ather the Breaker}}$ that the matches are in the bag. End of Session.
