## Session 28: Tourney of Obliteration

*Note: Nichole was unable to attend this session. Feyli is considered to be following the Party for this session but will not say anything.*

The Party wakes up in their new estate, and Hobson is given the information given by Uncle Pancreas from last episode. Today is the day of the Obliteration Amphitheatre's tourney, which starts at sundown. Hobson wakes up to make a breakfast fit for a warrior for Gelnek. Gelnek, while grateful, is nervous about fighting in front of such a large crowd. Typically, Ustenki people bring a bone from a slain monster, but since Gelnek has no such bones, he'll have to bypass that tradition. Gelnek will also have to remember how to fight solo while the rest of the Flower Crowns watch in the audience. He then asks Hobson how he deals with being nervous constantly. Hobson replies that he tends to rely on instincts after panicking, though the Party has helped him panic less. Gelnek's instincts are probably better anyway. He then asks Coil, who also expresses that he has moments of self-doubt, surprising Gelnek. When Gelnek asks how Coil deals with said feelings, Coil replies that he doesn't. Hobson mentions that Gelnek uses music to inspire the Party, so he could maybe use that to help himself. Trilby wakes up and is asked about fear and doubt. Trilby replies that he feels it at all times unless he doesn't realize there is a fight. Therefore, forgetting about the fight can maybe help Gelnek's nerves. As an omen, Trilby pulls from his Bag of Many Things. He retrieves an $\color{lightgreen}{\textbf{Empty Beaker}}$. Trilby interprets that since it's empty, it represents Gelnek not having any fear or anxiety. Gelnek says he still has it, so Trilby offers to bottle up the fears inside the jar. Gelnek mimes moving anxiety into the jar. Trilby asks if the Ustenki has a rally cry, and Gelnek says that the song "Hooked on a Feeling" is the traditional Goblin chant. As the Party continues their day, Hobson shares the note that Uncle Pancreas shared with him. Morenthal declares that there's a lot of weird things going on in the world based on the information. The conversation wanders around until the Party gets on the topic of hobbies. Gelnek & Trilby want to get Morenthal into woodworking, so at some point, they'll bring Morenthall to $\color{coral}{\textbf{Hearth Depot}}$ to find something for him to do.

Coil mentions how interesting their personalized rooms are, especially since nobody told Melitha anything about their desires. The others agree, though Morenthal says that his room is very bare bones. Gelnek laments that the Party is uncultured and wants too take everyone to an Ustenki art gallery, whatever that is. Trilby takes a look in Coil's room and sees what was described in Session 27. Trilby is impressed about how much stuff is inside given Coil doesn't really think he wants any major amenities. Coil mentions his weird dream from last night but doesn't elaborate. They discuss Coil's bed and how he never really needs sleep or rest. Nevertheless, he does sit on occasion. The Party continues to discuss each other's rooms, and Trilby looks at Hobson's room, specifically the Valse shrine. The Party continues to look at each other's rooms. Morenthal's is next, and they are surprised at how simple the room is. Gelnek and Trilby think there should be more in the room, something that reminds him of good memories. As Morenthal mulls this over, he looks over to the Little Guy's terrarium and might have gained just a bit of understanding. Next on the list is Gelnek, who happily shows off the wild room he has so he can explain every single object in it. Importantly, it feels like the home of the Flower Crowns mixed with his home with the Ustenki. After that final statement, the Party realizes it is about time to go to the Obliteration Amphitheatre for Gelnek's fight. Trilby takes a moment to emphasize that the glass jar is at the house rather than with Gelnek.

The Flower Crowns approach the Amphitheatre, which is a large rectangular building. The Half-Orc at the booth waves Gelnek through but everyone else pays for tickets using 5 sp each. Gelnek goes to the ground floor to meet the other gladiators, the Party is directed upstairs to the guest viewing areas. Gelnek gives a final reminder of the famous chant as the rest of the Party head off to their seats. The viewing area has multiple rows of bleachers, some box seats, and a large glass dome for more official people to sit in. There is currently a band performing in the arena, which itself is carved into 5ft square tiles. The arena is packed. Gelnek, meanwhile, is in the locker room where the combatants are preparing. A Half-Elf staff member named $\color{teal}{\textbf{Client}}$ waves him over and confirms all his information. The tourney is a 64 bracket-style event. First up is an obstacle course with 16 participants with 2 people advancing. Next is a 4-way free for all, and then the final is also a 4-way free for all. To ensure everyone is at their best, all abilities are refreshed between rounds, and if anyone goes unconscious, they'll be rushed out of the arena and tended to. Gelnek goes off to prepare. Up in the stands, Trilby sees a lone figure in one of the box seats, a Dragonborn with an elongated neck. Zorinn Enkitellor isn't wearing any obvious armor, but he is observing. The Gnome band leaves at this point as a Halfling ringmaster amplifies his voice to announce the system & notable people. The current Champion has won 3 times, and his name is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Scios Myrewyrm}}$, who clearly has a big fandom behind him. With the announcements complete, the participants march out to the arena as the obstacle course magically arranges itself. Among the prizes, there's the title as Northcliff's Strongest, 5000 gp as a cash prize, and a free enchantment from the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Forge and Forestry}}$. The Halfling calls for the contestants to get ready, and Gelnek prepares with Heroism and Longstrider spells. As a skill check gauntlet, Gelnek will need to succeed on a bunch of skill checks to win the day.

First obstacle is a large pit with tall platforms enabling someone to cross. Gelnek immediately leaps from platform to platform, easily crossing the pits. Second is a tall wall of vines and ropes for Gelnek to cross. Gelnek decides to Cirque du Soleil his way up the rope like an acrobatic performer, which he manages easily. From the top is a slope leading to a 10 ft long pit of thorns. Directly afterward is a 30 ft long pool of water. Gelnek slides down the slope, seeing the point to jump off it to go over the spikes. He lands it in the water and uses Thunderwave to torpedo himself through the water. Obstacle five is another 15ft pit with hanging ropes, and Gelnek delicately grabs ropes one after another. As he lands, darkness envelopes him and he hears the tiles shift in front of him. Startled, Gelnek swings his hammer around and discovers an invisible maze. Gelnek uses Guidance to assist his Investigation check of hugging a wall through the maze. It takes a bit, but he gets through it. After the invisible maze is a thick hedge maze about 20 ft high. Gelnek uses his internal compass to easily breeze through this particular maze. The final dash lies in front of Gelnek, who is the first to exit the maze. Nothing except cut grass and the buzzers. Gelnek takes a moment to see the Flower Crowns' signs and waves towards them. He then takes a simple sprint towards the buzzers. The second from Gelnek's heat is a female Half-Elf nicknamed as The Mace of the Forge wearing a heavy tabard and hefty armor. An intermission happens as the Amphitheatre resets. Gelnek greets the Half-Elf, who introduces herself as $\color{teal}{\textbf{Daphne}}$, and they both agree that each of them did a good job. In the event they are in the same free for all, they could try to make a temporary alliance. But they should check if that's allowed first. As the two of them leave the arena, some Ophena clerics come over to fully refresh them. However, the next hour or so will be the other three heats, so they have time to get merch or food. Trilby buys a foam sword & a novelty popcorn bucket, and Hobson buys an extremely overpriced ale.

In the other heats, some notable contestants include $\color{teal}{\textbf{The Beast from Beyond}}$, a male Elf that is extremely buff, a Leonin, $\color{teal}{\textbf{The Corporal of Pain}}$, a Crown's Guard Member, and the Boreal Blade, Scios Myrewyrm himself. He looks like an average Human with blue-white scales, a horn, and a dragon-like tail. He wins extremely easily. The Semi-finals are up next, and since Gelnek did the best, he gets to choose his starting corner, and he takes Northeast, as that is where he first met the Flower Crowns. The arena shifts accordingly, and the quadrants are set up. The tiles start normal, but they have arcane runes on them. In this battle, Daphne, the Beast, and the Corporal will be fighting with Gelnek. Daphne has a large mace, the Beast has a greataxe, and the Corporal has a heavy crossbow & a longsword. It is a free for all, but the arena will shift every few seconds. *Combat Begins.* The Corporal rushes towards Gelnek and shoots with his crossbow. Daphne runs to the center and casts Hold Person on the Corporal, giving Gelnek a wink. Gelnek, taking a hint, uses Marac-Star on him with a 4th level smite added on top. Before the Corporal can hit the ground, he is teleported out for medical attention. The Beast Misty Steps to get to Daphne and recklessly attack her. Daphne summons a Spiritual Weapon to attack the Beast. Gelnek returns Daphne's favor and casts Hold Person on the Beast, and he also returns the wink. As the Beast fails to break out of Hold Person, the entire arena becomes covered in ice, and the temperature drops dramatically. Daphne hits him as Gelnek comes barreling in to destroy him with a 2nd level smite, sending the Beast to the medical bay. The ice disappears and the temperature normalizes as the ground becomes quicksand. Gelnek gets sucked in and becomes restrained. Daphne calls for the best warrior to win, and Gelnek says that he intends to. She proceeds to miss her attacks and is hit by Gelnek's Thunderwave in return. Gelnek runs up to Daphne as the arena shifts into a lush forest, which has no environmental effects. Daphne gets some hits onto Gelnek as he two-hands his mace. He casts Shatter to the tune of the "I---" call in "Hooked on a Feeling". As she falls and is teleported, Gelnek continues to play the song in victory. A much shorter intermission occurs before the other fight, which is also fairly one-sided as Scios wins his own semifinal.

After the bout, Scios walks over to Gelnek to introduce himself. Scios was wondering whether Gelnek wants to do a 1v1 or if he wants to do a 3v3 if they each invite a pair. Gelnek asks if that's allowed, and Scios says he can make it so it is allowed. Gelnek agrees and before the final, he goes off to choose his pair. Client tells him to be back in 10 minutes. Gelnek tells the Party about the 3v3 and asks who wants in on the fight. After some discussion, Gelnek selects Trilby and Morenthal. Neither one was expecting it, but they're both willing. The arena has changed to a much simpler square with only a few pieces of debris scattered around. Scios's companions include a squat Aaracokra with a large firearm and a large humanoid with a snake head who wields a scimitar and a bow. The announcer introduces everyone and starts the fight. *Combat Begins.* $\color{teal}{\textbf{Olick Taloncrest}}$ flies up and lands on a taller piece of debris to take some shots at Gelnek which both miss. Gelnek buffs Trilby with Guidance and gives Morenthal his bardic inspiration before charging into battle. Trilby gets Scrubbins onto the field, who shoots Orlick and pulls him off the debris. $\color{teal}{\textbf{Natole}}$ runs up to attack Gelnek with his scimitar as Scios flanks Gelnek. Morenthal goes straight to attack Scios and deals some hefty damage. Orlick heals Scios with a Healing Word and shoots Trilby twice. Gelnek is getting angry, so he attempts to cast Hold Person on all three using a 4th level spell, and only Scios saves. Trilby has Scrubbins shoot Natole before casting Fireball to automatically hit the paralyzed Natole and Orlick. Scios turns towards Morenthal & Trilby and blows a white flame towards them, and the two of them dodge it, but Trilby still takes a bunch of damage. Morenthal retaliates with a classic sneak attack just as Gelnek annihilates Natole and Orlick breaks out of Hold Person. He also heals Trilby, just as Scrubbins and Trilby combo Orlick into dust. Scios, as the last one standing, hits three times onto Morenthal. Morenthal casually slices Scios to ribbons, which results in Gelnek, Drum of the Flower Crowns winning the tournament.

After the fight, the crowd slowly begins to simmer down. Client brings the tournament prizes to Gelnek: 5k gp and the voucher at the Forge and Forestry. Various people come up to talk and take magical selfies with the Flower Crowns & Gelnek. Trilby tries to find Zorin but he left immediately after Gelnek won. As the arena closes, the Party is kicked out of the Amphitheatre. Gelnek splits the 5k among everyone that's not himself, but he keeps the voucher. He's feeling a little overwhelmed by the fame and attention. Hobson proposes just heading home to relax by themselves. Hobson asks if they way the Party fought is how they all look fighting together. Gelnek says that they aren't always that good but this time they were. Gelnek also asks about what race Scios is, given the Dragonborn-like qualities but the distinctly Human body. Thinking on it, Scios is probably something like a half-Dragonborn, born from the humanoid form of a powerful dragon. The rest of the night passes uneventfully, and the Party gets well-deserved rest. Trilby wakes up first to ask Coil about any new dreams, but he hasn't had anything happen since the previous vision. Trilby is a little sad he didn't get to explode Scrubbins during the final match, since it would've made for good spectacle. Coil wonders if that Scrubbins is sentient, and Trilby asks. Scrubbins, predictably, gives a blank stare back. Trilby wants hashbrowns, but since nobody has made any, he instead waits at the table. Hobson sees this and decides to start making hashbrowns for breakfast in the morning. A little later, Gelnek goes to talk with Morenthal about getting him a hobby. Glenek thinks Morenthal would be a great thespian since he is good at physical jobs and performances. His speech and talking skills could improve a lot over time. Alternatively, Gelnek proposes that Morenthal could be a stuntman for any plays that need them. After all, having a hobby is good for a person, though Morenthal is still flabbergasted about being a thespian. However, he has trouble visualizing himself in such a job, though he did have to disguise himself as an actor once. Gelnek cuts him off and claims that he knew Morenthal would be a great thespian and runs off to eat breakfast. Later that day, Gelnek turns in his order at the Forge and Forestry, though JoCat doesn't know what he wants yet. Coil is curious about how the walker for Bartholomew is going, but this early, Moeb probably barely has a prototype right now.

Meanwhile, Hobson is reading the book on Fey that Valse pointed out and also trying to figure out how E'lythia's own law system works. Their conclusions still hasn't changed: getting Valse a real body again will help correct the deal that existed. Hobson wants to talk to some people with magic knowledge to get Valse out of the bracer. Morenthal & Trilby also go shopping. Morenthal looks for some plants for the Little Guy at Hearth Depot and Trilby writes a letter to check in on his family. At some point, Coil notices a small Half-Elf girl crying and looking around in the street. Coil slowly wanders over, and as she notices him, she walks over and asks for help. She says that some man strange broke into her home and hurt her mother before she ran to look for help. Coil asks if she can lead him to her house, and she does. They walk north to a decent cottage. She points to the front and says the man broke in the front and she ran out the back. Coil asks if she knows how to get to the Crown's Guard, and she nods. He tells her to go to them as he inspects the house. He sees that none of the shutters/blinds are open, the girl has jewelry on, and she takes a while to head towards the Guards. Coil is suspicious, but he enters anyway. Inside, there are no lights on. A trail of blood leads into a bedroom off of a hallway. He hears something dripping from that room. He also notices the pitter patter of feet behind him as the girl attempts to stab him with a pair of knives. A lanky golden but bloody hand grips the doorway of the bedroom. 

*Combat Begins* as she misses her stabs. Coil grabs the girl by her throat, lifts her off the ground, and asks what she's doing. Her expression has gone from sad and scared to determined as if she was Morenthal. Coil yanks one dagger out from the girl's hand, and it looks like it has green ichor on it. While not very strong, she is clearly toned and trained for something. The gold figure steps into the light, and Coil recognizes this as Finch Ironbellow the renegade blood mage. From a slit in his arm, he attacks Coil, claiming that "he has it". The girl manages to stab Coil with her dagger, which is heavily poisoned. Hobson doesn't seem to notice anything. Black ichor seeps out and a clone of Coil appears behind Finch, as he summons an Echo of himself. He manages to disarm the girl's other dagger and his Echo bounces off of Finch's Shield spell. Finch stabs into himself and the blood that spews creates a summoning circle that makes a summoned fiend. The fiend immediately walks up and begins to pummel Coil. The fiend lets out a guttural grunt at Coil as Feyli (controlled by Dan) walks in and asks WTF? End of Session.
