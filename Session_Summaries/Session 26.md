## Session 26: Constructs Have Emotions

During the recap, it is confirmed that the Magic Key is indeed the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Shaper of Minds}}$. We open as the Viridescent Javelin departs Brightcoin late at night with the Flower Crowns & Six on board. Trilby does a quick check to see if any arcane circles were made, but there were none. Gelnek is the first to change back into his combat gear, taking off his artificial fur coat. Trilby starts to work on Scrubbins, using Marm & Vespi's workshop. The mood on the ship is fairly dour, still reeling from Grayton's betrayal. Six inspects the ship, and Coil brings the body of Blade below deck. Pocket talks to Feyli about their backstory: she was caught by hunters in the Feywild before being sold as a pet. She's enjoying the airship, though. Hobson makes coffee and goes up to ask Six if she eats or drinks. She replies that she does not need a power source. However, she does accept a mug of coffee from Hobson and drinks it, still scalding hot. Morenthal proceeds directly into the cargo bay to brood alone. That doesn't stop Hobson from offering him a coffee, which Morenthal declines. Gelnek insights that Six is incredibly Lawful, so she will not be a threat to anyone unless provoked. Everyone sleeps off the major fight they just had, getting their long rest. The mood is back to neutral after everyone on board gets their sleep. Flying over the desert is uneventful. Trilby fixes up Scrubbins overnight and gets a new idea and goes to find a Party member to show it off. He finds Gelnek and says his idea: He can make Scrubbins self-destruct. Gelnek is skeptical on how that is useful, but Trilby points out that exploding things do damage. Gelnek wonders if he could learn how to make himself explode, and Trilby gives him a Void Crystal to eat. Gelnek licks it first and asks Trilby how it all works again. This explanation takes the rest of the day and Gelnek will not remember it. During the explanation, Six comes over to watch Trilby intently and lifts his chin up, moving it left and right. She mentions that he's "defective," meaning he has been modified in some way. It is faint, but it is there. Gelnek and Trilby are confused about this, since neither think Trilby has encountered mind-altering effects. Trilby asks if it has Hag vibes, and Gelnek explains that they've had contact with Karashala. Six offers her hand to Gelnek so he can see, and with a nod from Trilby, he takes it. There is a very slight obscuring effect over Trilby's face, though it appears more as a blurring effect. Six offers to fix it, and Trilby says okay. Six goes to grab Trilby's face, but Gelnek pauses her to confirm that it is safe. She says it is safe, so she proceeds to grab Trilby's face. 

He suddenly recalls that Trilby has seen Quinn before. Once when he was going to meet Byfar, he was paralyzed by Hold Person and Quinn appeared. He took a lock of Trilby's hair, patted him on the head, and left. Trilby realizes that certain spells are strengthened by having possessions and items, so the hair might be for that. Gelnek and Trilby discuss hair for a while after that, and Six leaves them alone. Gelnek asks Trilby if he could infuse some kind of explosion magic to a weapon. Trilby says that it might destroy the weapon, but he'll see what he can do. Gelnek gives him a maraca hammer. Morenthal wakes up from his rest in the cargo hold, and he continues to brood. Coil walks up to Feyli, holding the Music Box. Feyli tells Pocket to pause so she can talk to Coil alone. Feyli doesn't recognize the Music Box at first. However, as she looks at it, she begins to remember it. Coil recalls that he took the Box since he thought it was harming Feyli in some way. He also mentions meeting with Horace in Northcliff. Feyli remembers Horace now, but still hasn't fully wrapped her head around the memory loss issue. Coil apologizes for hiding the Music Box and his worries from Feyli. She trust Coil and tells him not to worry about it. She has also been talking to Pocket for hours on end, so she's pretty calm. As she thinks harder, though, she begins to realize just how strange the memory loss is. Coil then asks why she took the Heart. She recalls that the night she left was the night she realized the Midnight Lotus was just leading them on, all for the First. She overheard people talking behind her back about how fooled everyone in the cult was. She ultimately thought that taking the Heart might stop them from hurting other people. Coil thinks she made a good call because it led to the destruction of the Midnight Lotus. She resolves to stop any other groups like it that she encounters. She goes back to letting Pocket talk her ear off. That done, Coil goes off to look for Morenthal and finds him in the cargo hold. Morenthal is playing with his $\color{tan}{\textbf{Little Guy}}$ and a feather.

Coil apologizes for forcing his way into Morenthal's mind using the Shaper of Minds. Morenthal asks Coil how he knows that undoing the damage is what Coil did. For all he knows, Coil could have altered Morenthal's memories like Mr. Wick did. Coil says that it is a good question, but if he had, he probably would've tried to make his memories happier. They both agree that the Shaper of Minds shouldn't be in mortal hands, and they hope the Council agrees to let Six take it back. Coil hopes to rebuild Morenthal's trust in the Party as time marches onward. Morenthal gives a soft "Thank you," as  Coil leaves. The day passes into evening, and Azru announces that everyone is meeting on the top deck for drinks. Attendance is mandatory. The sky is very clear, and there are auroras in sight, providing dim light to the deck. Ardwill and Azru have set up a small party. Morenthal is the last one to arrive, but he does show up after Azru prods him again. Azru gives a speech and declares that the mood on the ship is too depressing, so this party is to liven things up. Most of the crew loosen up and Gelnek gets into it. He asks if anyone knows how to play any instruments. Carva brings out a pipe flute, Azru begins to sing shanties since he and Ardwill were both sailors. Six continues to observe. Feyli and Pocket (who is still carrying the mythril chain) enjoy the party. Feyli finally looks at the Arcane Lock that has kept her chained. Hobson attempts to use Dispel Magic on it, which works perfectly. Feyli now has 25 ft of $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Mythril Chain}}$. Pocket immediately gets cartoonishly drunk, complete with bubbles coming out of her. Everyone except Morenthal and Six get into the merriment of the party. Morenthal broods on one side of the deck, and Six just doesn't know what to do other than sit there. Gelnek modifies the shanties to try and get Hobson to dance with Six. It takes a little explanation for Six to understand what dancing is. She eventually allows him to dance with her, and he's fairly comfortable despite the obvious size difference. She's awkward about it, but she does not step away from it. She finally reveals a little emotion: embarrassment, and she sits back down to observe the party.

The drinking party winds down, Hobson helps clean up, and the night passes peacefully. Hangovers greet some people on the ship as the morning comes. Winter has set in with snow flurries. Trilby pulls from his Bag of Many Things, pulling an $\color{lightgreen}{\textbf{Magic Opal}}$ the size of a golf ball. A faint thumping inside reveals there is some sort of magic trapped inside, but it is hard to tell. Trilby pops it in his mouth and regrets that decision. He puts it back in his bag. Trilby goes over to find Gelnek, who is playing Baltoro in the middle of the ship. Trilby hands over the maraca hammer, which has now been infused to be a radiant weapon. Gelnek is very excited about this weapon, and he hugs Trilby. Gelnek then declares that Trilby should name the weapon. Trilby will need to get back to him on that one though. On the third day of travel, the weather becomes a little turbulent. Azru and Karva are arguing about the storm. Going through the storm is a bad idea given that they're in Cloud Giant territory. Going around it will add a day of travel and may result in wyvern attacks. Waiting it out could add even more time, but it is the safest option. Azru asks the Flower Crowns for input. Most of them agree that avoiding the storm is for the best. Six wants to get to the Council ASAP but understands it's not her call. Pocket wants to go through the storm. Coil knows that Blade could be revived with the same technique regardless of the 1-day difference. After deliberation, the decision is made to go around the storm. Carva is happy with that decision and Azru detours through the higher mountains. Trilby finds Gelnek again to give the name to the maraca hammer: The $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Marac-star}}$ ("Mah-rock-star"), which they agree is an awesome name for it. The next day is mostly uneventful until about midday. Mountain peaks are clearly visible compared to the rest of the landscape and the storm passes harmlessly. Gelnek and Coil keep watch, and they notice some large shape rise out of one mountain peak. It jumps off and unfurls its wings, which is absolutely enormous. As it gets within 1000 ft, Azru curses and announces for all hands on deck. And for everyone to tie themselves to the ship. Everyone does so, though Trilby's knot could be better.

*Combat Begins.* Morenthal stabs it immediately with his Purging Dagger, and Hobson doesn't pierce his hide. He tries to back off but gets clawed badly. Feyli summons an insect plague on the roc and enters her chalice form. Coil runs up to attack the roc as it flies overhead to attack Morenthal & Coil. Coil gets grappled by its massive talons. The roc flies straight up, ignoring the swipes by the rest of the Party. It is unable to snap the rope attached to Coil, but only barely. It hovers 20 ft above the deck. Gelnek uses his Paladin multiclass to fire a Guiding Bolt at it and gives bardic inspiration to Hobson. Trilby summons Scrubbins and gives most of the Party temp HP. Ardwill, Six, and Morenthal all shoot it with ballistate. Hobson hits it with Synaptic Static, causing the roc to screech in pain. Feyli hits it with Guiding Bolt, giving an assist to Coil, who slashes four times to kill the roc. It falls dead on the deck, and Azru tells Marm to increase engine power to compensate for the weight. Ardwill comes over and tells the Party to carve the roc since it cooks really well. The roc gets defeathered and prepared for food. By the next day, the Viridescent Javelin arrives back in Northcliff. With the Shaper of Minds a priority, the Party and Six go to find the Council. Adokas or Casma are able to gather the Council quickly. Six prepares to present her case to the Council. Gelnek asks that Six mentions what happened to the civilians in Brightcoin, and she agrees. As she goes up to the dias, Turnos makes a snide comment that this should be good. Six covers the whole situation involving the Shaper of Minds. She says that this meeting is more of a formality as she will not forsake her orders to $\color{teal}{\textbf{Primus}}$ to return the Key. She also mentions the civilians in Brightcoin. Murmurs ripple through the Council, and Ciona requests a brief recess to deliberate. Kasma then asks the Flower Crowns what they think of the situation. Gelnek says that the Party mostly agrees with Six that the artifact should be taken with her. If possible, the effects should be reversed. 

Adokas announces that the Council is at a tie vote of 3 in favor of keeping it on the Material plane and 3 in favor of returning it to Mechanus. Thus, the tiebreaker should fall to the Party. The key's effects could probably be reversed by Greater Restoration. Ciona adds that finding the civilians affected may be hard, but it should be reversible. The Party briefly discusses what to do, but they're all decided to let the Shaper of Minds return to Mechanus with Six. Adokas formally says Six is allowed to return the key. Six immediately turns to the Party and thanks them for the time on the Material plane. Gelnek responds that he's thankful she showed grace to their ignorance about the situation. Six creates a portal to Mechanus using her arms. Through the portal, the city can be seen as it rotates on a massive gear rotating at a clockwork pace. The denizens are all mechanical or constructs. She walks through the portal, waves goodbye, and the portal closes. Ciona tells the Party that while the artifact is not in the Council's possession, it is secured, so payment will arrive at the Shattered Shield. She asks if there's anything else the Party wants to tell the Council. Trilby says that Josh of the Crystal United Mercenaries have the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Wand of Chaos}}$. Adokas briefs the Council on who they are, and he asks that the Party confirms they have the artifact. They do, and they also mention that the Mercenaries killed civilians. Adokas resolves to inform the Crown's Guard and make wanted posters. Casma asks what Adokas knows about the group, and he mentions that he only knows Josh and $\color{tan}{\textbf{Ida}}$. However, the Council would know of Josh's father, $\color{teal}{\textbf{Thaddius De Rosa}}$, a major noble living in Blackfane. He asks if the Party knows more about the Mercenaries, and Trilby recounts what they know. They know Quinn's name, but they can only describe the other members. At the mention of the Dragonborn, Turnos scoffs at the possibility that $\color{teal}{\textbf{Zorinn Enkitellor}}$ could be working with the Mercenaries. He is a champion of the gladiators in Dragonhold. Ida and Josh can have bounties placed on their heads, and that should keep the group moving. 

Gelnek asks if the Council has any idea why the Mercenaries would do this. Adokas speculates that Josh is spoiled and wants everything, including being a hero group. So, he is throwing a tantrum, which unfortunately involves lives. The Council will try to blacklist the group from any funding they could get from anyone. The other descriptions for the members of the Crystal United Mercenaries get some responses. Brenon recalls that the Bugbear is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Shade Destall}}$, a powerful mercenary from the Endavi. Despite the posters, the group does have considerable influence due to Josh's father. This won't be a final solution, but it should help keep them away. They don't have any other important information to convey, so the Flower Crowns are dismissed, and the Council is adjourned. The Party head over to the Shattered Shield and gain their artifact payment and also Preston's payment. They split the cash, meaning each member gets 3250 gp. They then immediately head off to the one and only Chameleonaire in the Trade District. He greets them warmly, though he has to do age verification for any sales. His magic items haven't changed, but the rings are always for sale. Trilby goes first, receiving the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Ring of Heavy}}$, which can become 500 lbs once per day. His second ring is the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Ring of Invisibility}}$, which turns invisible when worn. Gelnek gets his ring game on, receiving the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Pocket Ring}}$, which can store a fist-sized object in a pocket dimension. Hobson takes a spin, and he gets the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Ring of Signing}}$, which gives a +8 to forging documents. Morenthal is up next and receives the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Ring of One Eye}}$, and while attuned to it, by closing one eye, he can see out of the ring, Mr Potatohead style. As Feyli goes up to do ring gacha, the Chamelonaire angrily tells her 400 gp per roll. Feyli refuses to pay that amount and goes to a Crown's Guard to explain the situation. The guard walks over and asks the Chamelonaire if there's a problem. he hastily covers and denies it. The price goes back to the default 200 gp per roll, which Feyli accepts. The first ring is the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Ring of Flint}}$, which can ignite a nonmagical weapon for a single attack. Feyli is done, but she wants to smack the Chameleonaire without actually hitting him. She uses Druidcraft to make it rain (literally) in his stall.

The Party contemplates what they can do with all the money they now have. One proposal is to actually get a real place to live instead of using the inn constantly. Trilby proposes calling it $\color{coral}{\textbf{The Garden}}$, since they're the Flower Crowns. A house with room for the 6 of them goes for about 20k gp, while higher-end houses go for up too 50k gp. Gelnek collects the money together: Gelnek, Hobson & Coil for 4k, Trilby & Feyli for 3k each, Morenthal for 2K. The house will be fully furnished and a handful of specialized rooms. They end up choosing a spot in the Eastern District, and it has a small forested area plus being near some water. The house design will come later. This process will take a while, so the money is set aside just in case they need it later. Once he's free, Coil drags Blade Goldwill's body over to Moeb's Pawn Shop to see what can be done to revive him. Morenthal tags along, and the Little Guy helps carry the body. Moeb, Estevan, and Cog are all in the store. As they enter, Estevan clears a table to set Blade down on. The story of Blade gets relayed as Moeb also comes over to inspect him. Moeb asks if Coil wants Blade revived, and Coil says yes. Moeb thinks he could probably fix him in two ways. The Arcane Core could be stripped for parts, or the parts would need to be gotten elsewhere. Using the core for revival will cost 500 gp, but getting the parts and reviving will cost 5,500 gp. Coil doesn't have strong feelings about the Arcane Core, so he tells Moeb and Estevan to use the Core. As Moeb prepares to revive Blade, Coil & Morenthal are allowed to make an offering to Blade to improve the revival process. Using the Core has already given a big boost. Coil doesn't have anything meaningful to offer and neither does Morenthal, so the revival proceeds. With quite a bit of work, Moeb and Estevan are able to raise Blade back to life. He turns to Coil and asks what Coil did to him. End of Session.
