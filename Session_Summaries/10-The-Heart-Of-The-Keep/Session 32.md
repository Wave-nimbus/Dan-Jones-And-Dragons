## Session 32: Damage to Mind and Body

We open as Miory wakes the Party up before they can take their Short Rest in the safe room. The previously purple fire is now burning a bright orange. She asks what the Flower Crowns are doing here. Gelnek and Coil explain that they're adventurers tasked with exploring the Keep and that they're looking for something. Miory says she was part of the first party who explored the Keep, ordered by Raya and pressured by the Merchant Collective. By her time, it has been about a week since she entered. She has managed to survive by eating Goodberries, which she conjures and hands to the Party if they want it. She explains that while the room is safe, the fires are not. They have spirits that feed on arcane energy from spells and magic items. If the fire glows red, the spirits won't do anything, but if they glow yellow, they will be placated long enough to safely rest. Purple is the standard color where it feeds off of people's life force. Coil asks if anyone else came with her, but she was alone. Since the odds are better for her to stick with the Party, she joins. Mechanically, spell slots provide a number of hours equal to the spell slot level. Hobson can throw in his two level 5s and retrieve them with only a Short Rest. The Party discusses what to do for resting as Miory has provided about 2 hours of safety. Hobson tosses in one of his spell slots to provide more safety. They begin to go over the various magic items from last session, and the Ironbark Armor is officially named the $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Venombark Armor}}$. While Trilby has access to Identify, he doesn't have it prepared right now. Feyli puts on the Venombark Armor. It has the same AC as Half-Plate armor, and Feyli realizes that it appears to be attuned towards the natural $\color{brown}{\textbf{Ironbark Forest}}$ in the south swamps. When she takes Poison damage, she can use a Reaction to reduce that damage by 1d8 + CON. The Short Rest commences with Morenthal staring with distrust at Miory the whole time. Miory notices that Feyli is a Druid, so Miory asks if Feyli is part of a circle. Feyli thinks about it, but she doesn't remember anything before the Midnight Lotus, so she doesn't know where she got her powers. Miory notices the reaction of confusion and despair and decides to step over to Morenthal to talk to him. They discuss how Miory got inside and the fact that the doors close so they can't get back out. Morenthal makes it clear that he doesn't trust her and proceeds to be stonewalled about what his story is. Feyli asks Coil if he knows about where Feyli learned magic, but he doesn't know either. The Party prepares to continue to explore the Keep with Miory in tow.

They open the door north of the safe room to see what's inside. A fairly large room greets them with four iron platforms hanging from the ceiling. A grate in the center gives off an acidic smell. They head over to the red sand room with lit brasures and spiked floors. The ceiling continues into nothingness above. The sound of a cheering crowd is audible. Coil spawns Goo Coil into the room to see what happens. The crowd sound roars to life as cheers and hollers fill the room. A pair of short white platforms emerge from the sands. Goo Coil notices that there is a large glyph carved into each pedestal, though its function is unknown. Doorways to the west and east have iron doors. Trilby tells Goo Coil to step onto the platform, but Goo Coil asks what the Party will do for him, since he got eviscerated by spikes earlier. Morenthal decides to just step into the room himself, to the roaring cheers of the crowd who calls him by name. He checks the sigil and knows that it'll do something, though what is uncertain. Goo Coil thinks the glyph looks arcane, so Feyli should come take a look, and she does (to the cheering crowd). Feyli notices that it appears to be a customized symbol that evokes magic of holding something or someone in place. She doesn't think the Party should touch the pedestals, instead walking over to the west door and finding the same room with the grate. Trilby checks the grate room and all he can surmise is that various chemicals and acid has passed through the grate many times. Morenthal checks the north door out of the red sand room, which appears to be yet another safe room. Hobson walks into the red sands room but has no memory of doing so, as the crowd alternates between chanting for Valse and Hobson. Coil opens a door to the east of the sands room which leads to a large central chamber that the Party hasn't investigated yet but they have seen before. The ceiling of this room visibly goes on for hundreds of feet rather than disappearing into darkness, and a massive spire towers into that ceiling. The walls are covered in stained glass, and there is an altar with a humanoid shaped indent in it. Goo Coil gestures for Hobson to approach before he bends down and touches a sigil with his hand. The sigil reacts but doesn't seem to activate completely. Morenthal suggests putting the other hand on the other pedestal, which is difficult, but Coil is large. Feyli leaves the room and Hobson steps back. Miory is asked what her thoughts are on it. She thinks that the crowd and atmosphere resembles an arena of some kind. Hobson fails a WIS save as Valse takes over and stands onto one of the pedestals. The crowd goes insane, as the glyph activates and traps Hobson in a small cylinder that he cannot leave. Coil steps on the other pedestal as Morenthal enters the east doorway. Goo Coil and Miory have to make DEX saves, but both of them fail to avoid being sucked into the sand.

The crowd shifts to chanting "Blood must spill!" as Morenthal, Trilby, and Gelnek try DEX save sto see if they want to stay inside the arena area. Gelnek gets sucked inside the sand, but Morenthal is able to stay sidled up against the doorway to witness what happens. The glyph holding Hobson and Coil disappears, causing the red sand to turn into red stone after the pedestals sink back into the ground. Coil points out that he doesn't have blood, and Hobson doesn't like how this is looking. Morenthal continues to be against the wall because he doesn't trust the ground. Hobson mutters whether he actually needs to fight Coil or if cutting himself would do the trick. Morenthal says that it's worth the shot before Coil cuts Hobson down to size. Hobson cuts the back of his arm with his sword, and as his blood drips onto the floor, it begins to flow through a massive glyph on the ground. But not enough blood has been spilled to fill the area. Seconds later, Morenthal is pushed off the wall as new spikes form on the walls and doorways, and the current spikes begin to close in. Coil, Morenthal, and Hobson discuss their options, and Morenthal inspects the large glyph. He surmises that it is completing some kind of ritual as the walls close further. Morenthal tries to dump a potion on the ground, but it splashes normally. Based on the amount of blood Hobson has spilled, he would need to sacrifice all his HP to fill the glyph. Those outside can't do anything to help. In desperation, Feyli pulls from her $\color{tan}{\textbf{Deck of Cards}}$, pulling out *The Hermit*. It permanently increases her lowest ability score (STR) by 1. Inside, Hobson asks if Morenthal knows how to bleed someone out nonlethally, which kinda goes against how assassins work. Hobson cuts himself more, filling the blood pool a little bit more. Coil points out that Hobson and Morenthal are the only ones with blood. The crowd continues to chant that one must die, and Morenthal is concerned that it will only accept Hobson's blood. Coil slashes at Morenthal with a claw. Morenthal's blood has a different gradient than Hobson's, and it seems to fill the sigil less. Morenthal's blood did not fill the sigil in addition to Hobson's. Morenthal tries and fails to stab Coil, and the spikes close in so that there's only a 5 ft wide space to walk through. Morenthal tries to convince Coil to kill/KO Hobson. However, Valse takes over and says that since Morenthal has started, they should finish him of instead. He then shoots a pair of Eldritch Blasts at Morenthal. Hobson regains control, slashes himself again to slowly begin filling the pool, and Coil tries fruitlessly to push back the wall.

Morenthal tells Coil to try to smash the stones below them, and as Coil bends down, Morenthal hops over to stab Hobson for massive assassin damage. Hobson is still up and says that he thought Morenthal did this for a living. Morenthal is slightly offended by that, but still hesitates about killing Hobson. Coil smashes the original stone pedestal, as the walls begin to crush them fully now. Hobson says out loud that he'd rather die than everyone dies. At Morenthal's hesitation, Hobson says screw it and stabs himself. His body slumps to the ground unconscious as his lifeblood fills the sigil, and a pulse of magic causes the spike walls to retract. The crowd noise fades to silence, as Goo Coil, Miory, and Gelnek reappear from sand. In the center between the two original pedestals is a rotting humanoid hand, and the stone doors open, revealing the scene of Hobson down. Gelnek uses Lay on Hands on Hobson for a full 15. Miory heals for even more after Morenthal slaps Hobson for what he said earlier. Trilby asks if everyone is okay, to which Hobson says that he is now. Morenthal picks up the rotting hand, which he notes is very similar to the rotting head from earlier. Hobson chastises Valse for being so impulsive given that Hobson has blood currently while Valse doesn't. The Party heads over to the central chamber with the humanoid indent and column. Coil takes the rotting hand and places it into the indent, However, the hand animated and crawled into place. Hobson takes out the head from his Bag of Holding and places it in the slot. Feyli notices that Miory is staring at the stained glass on the walls. There are a total of 8 stained glass windows, with each Party member plus Pocket and Miory represented. Each mural describes an extremely depressing fate for each Party member. Morenthal is dead from his life of assassination; Feyli is the head of a Black Hand cult; Trilby's family dies before he does; Coil is a monstrous raging beast; Hobson is lost in the Ethereal Sea with no control over anything; Gelnek is alone and forgotten within a void. Pocket is bound in chains, unable to move. Miory is sealed in a crystal, surrounded by geodes like her staff. Morenthal judges that Miory's reaction is similar to their own, fearful to accept the fate foretold in the murals. Morenthal wants to try and smash his mural by shooting it with an arrow. He takes out his own face, opening the window into a black void beyond, but the rest of the window remains intact.

Morenthal goes around to the east door from this central room, opening into the large library. Thinking about it, the Party backtracks to the acid room with Trilby leading the way. They are pretty sure that the hanging platforms will be needed, and Miory says she can get herself and two others up there by turning into a large bird. With how the sandy room went, she wants to transform immediately, which she does. As a Giant Owl, she picks up Trilby and Gelnek and brings the three of them up to one of the floating platforms. They don't see anything else from their higher vantage point. Feyli turns into a large bird on her own to stand on another hanging platform. Trilby rides with Miory to a third platform, gets off, and sends her to the fourth platform. Miory picks up Hobson as well to take her place on the fourth platform. Coil walks into the room finally, but nothing immediately happens.  He looks into the grate and sees a vat of green bubbling acid about 10 ft below, and he sees some kind of object inside. Trilby jumps off and is caught by Coil. Trilby takes a peek and doesn't see anything more specific. He tries to use Mage Hand to grab the object. Morenthal chooses to back out of the room as the doors close. Trilby retrieves a decaying forearm and hand from the acid pit. A pair of dark shapes descend from the ceiling, landing on the grate. One appears fiendish with wings and curled horns. The other appears to be a pale spiked fiend of some kind. *Combat Begins*. The horned figure stabs Coil three times for big damage. Morenthal hears the sounds of combat and attempts to pull the door open enough to crawl through to join the fight. Trilby uses Vortex Warp to move the horned fiend into the acid bath, and Gelnek fires a Guiding Bolt at the pale fiend. He gives a Bardic Inspiration to Coil too. Feyli spawns a Wall of Fire on top of the fiends, though both are immune. Coil lays into the pale fiend as Miory drops her wildshape as she casts Earthbind on the same fiend. The fiend retaliates against Coil with claws and a tail swipe. Hobson hits the trapped fiend with Eldritch Blast, and the acid rises to be flush with the grate. 

The horned fiend hits Trilby twice with fiery bolts, though they are still stuck drowning in acid. He begins to destroy the grate slowly. Morenthal gets a powerful stab onto the pale fiend. Trilby goes over to Miory & Hobson's platform and requests to get up there. Miory isn't a bird anymore, so it'll have to wait. Trilby and Gelnek both shoot the pale fiend, with Gelnek granting Morenthal a Bardic Inspiration. Feyli follows suit to finish off the bony devil as it turns to ash. Coil picks up Morenthal and moves towards the fourth hanging platform, preparing to jump. Miory reaches down to try and grab Trilby while Hobson holds onto her. Hobson does what little he can to help hold onto Miory to let Trilby climb. The horned fiend breaks through the grate, flies into the air, and throws a bolt of fire at Trilby. Morenthal stands onto Coil's shoulders and shoots the fiend before climbing onto the platform. Trilby fails to climb Miory, and in a panic, uses the Trilbee to heal himself. Gelnek misfires a Guiding Bolt, inspiring Feyli to miss as well. Coil manages to clamber up the platform. Miory fails to bind the fiend to the earth, and she also fails to try and pull Trilby up. Hobson summons Valse as a treant and yells at Trilby to climb the tree if he can. Treant Valse manages to lift Trilby out of the acid to be level with the platform. The horned fiend is still very mad at Trilby, using all three attacks despite the second one knocking Trilby down, giving him two death fails. Morenthal shoots at the fiend, trying to chip away at him. Trilby rolls a death save successfully, and Gelnek brings him back up with a powerful Healing Word. Feyli also follows suit, using Mass Cure Wounds on most of the Party. Coil shoots the fiend with his crossbow. Miory holds her action to help Trilby get up, and Hobson uses Eldritch Blast again right before pulling Trilby onto the platform with Valse's help. The fiend angrily flies towards Gelnek and shoots fire at Feyli, a small piece is absorbed by Coil's Shared Pain bracelet. Their third strike goes at Gelnek, which hits. Morenthal asks to get thrown by Coil at the fiend, so he holds his action to do that. Trilby heals himself, as Gelnek finally is within melee range for bonking, and he heals Feyli. Feyli misses her guiding bolt shot as Coil manages to throw Morenthal across the room onto the back of the fiend. He gets a critical hit and the two of them fall into the acid. Fortunately the acid recedes as combat ends and the doors open.

Trilby heads over to the central room to place the arm everyone reflects on the battle. Treant Valse shrinks down to a normal-ish size with a dryad texture about him. Hobson introduces Valse the person to everyone in case he hasn't already. The arm animates to crawl into place within the humanoid indent, filling the right arm and hand. As the Party discusses what to do now, the sound of footsteps becomes louder and faster from the south. Miory looks over to the Party and asks if they're running or fighting. The Party retreats to the safe room north of the sand room to rest. Gelnek uses his Paladin ability Divine Sense to detect celestials, fiends, and undead. the patrol they ran from pinged as undead, and something fiendish pings above him. Miory also puts in a spell slot into the flames, buying time for a Short Rest if desired. Hobson uses his Warlock recharging to give everyone a Long Rest. After the rest, everyone takes a WIS Save, with only Hobson saving. The remaining Party members gte a personalized vision, though Hobson can listen in. 
Trilby goes first. A memory is powerfully recalled. Trilby finds himself in the $\color{coral}{\textbf{Clemingsworth Estate}}$ as $\color{tan}{\textbf{Bifar}}$ places a cup of tea in front of him, a sight familiar from Northcliff. Bifar asks what the last adventure was, and the two of the discuss what's going on and when he'll be back. Eventually, Bifar asks if Trilby can keep a promise to which Trilby agrees. Bifar chokes up before asking that Trilby not come back to talk with Bifar. He can't afford to be listening to Trilby's fairytale adventures because he has work and school to do. Trilby is distraught about this, but he says that this won't change their friendship. A scowl crosses Bifar's face as a different voice, more sultry and feminine, says that Trilby isn't fun to play with. The vision ends. 
Morenthal is next. His memory is from early on in his time at the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Bloodletters}}$, as $\color{tan}{\textbf{Daemon}}$ brings Morenthal back after a rough outing. This is notable because this memory is when Morenthal met Daemon's family. A pair of young boys run up and greet their father before turning their attention to Morenthal. Daemon looks at Morenthal and says he's just a broken tool that fails to do its job. He was just leaving in fact. Morenthal walks out the door without another word. The vision ends.
Feyli is third, and her memory is back in the lair of the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Midnight Lotus}}$. $\color{tan}{\textbf{Aryk}}$ knocks on her door and asks if they can talk. Feyli stands up and hugs Aryk and asks what is going on. Feyli recalls specifically this memory is when Aryk voiced his doubts about the organization. He says that he needs to get something off his chest. He's not sure when he stopped thinking about bringing his brother $\color{tan}{\textbf{Milo}}$ back. But his time in the Lotus is convincing him that maybe he deserved what happened. Maybe Aryk better off without Milo. The fact that he and Feyli are in the presence of $\color{tan}{\textbf{The First}}$ means they're worthy while Milo isn't. Feyli is confused by this, and Aryk continues that he wants to stand at The First's side with Feyli at his side. This is why he is here. Feyli agrees and takes Aryk's hand. The vision ends. 
Gelnek is next up. The vision opens as Gelnek is thrown to the ground, defeated and bruised as he fails his pilgrimage. From the crowd, $\color{tan}{\textbf{Chief Stompy}}$ calls for the end of the fight. He walks over and scowls at Gelnek, saying the pilgrimage has been failed and that it would be a shame to banish one of their own if Gelnek wasn't so weak. He offers nothing to the Ustenki. Gelnek is bewildered by Stompy's harsh words. The Chief has always been encouraging, but not now. Gelnek hangs his head in shame and accepts the judgement. The vision ends. 
Finally is Coil. His memory is of the first few moments entering $\color{tan}{\textbf{Brightcoin}}$ at a crossroads. This is the moment he ended service for $\color{tan}{\textbf{Preston}}$ because of Feyli at his side. A thud is heard as Coil's Obsidian Key is thrown to the ground. Feyli says she held up her end of the bargain and doesn't want Coil to accompany her. Coil is free to go. Coil asks why Feyli took the key, and Feyli responds that it was just for skill practice. Coil asks if she doesn't need a powerful warforged, and she affirms that she doesn't need a mindless drone following her orders. Coil picks up the key. He still thanks her for his freedom. They say their goodbyes and go their separate ways. Coil closes his claw around the key, feeling lost. The vision ends.

Much of the Party awakens in an unfamiliar arena. Hobson, Trilby, and Miory wake up to a curvascious fiend floating near the Party. Coil, Gelnek, Morenthal, and Feyli's heads remain unconscious as a pink sheen covers their eyes. The fiend mentions that she almost took control of everyone so easily, so now it is time to finish business. *Combat Begins.* The controlled Party members (those still charmed) cannot speak and must fight the non-controlled Party members. Morenthal opens by landing a critical hit on Trilby. Coil rages and goes after Hobson. Miory is confused by the infighting, but Trilby calls that they're probably possessed. Miory can try to fix one of them, and without a clear direction from Hobson and Trilby, she cures Feyli of the charm effect. Feyli realizes that the fiend was whispering nothings into her ear, as she wakes up. Trilby heals himself for a large amount of HP. The fiend creates an additional 7 copies of herself spread through the arena which all begin to glow. Feyli fires a Guiding Bolt at the original clone's position, and she Wildshapes to become Archer form. Gelnek runs up to critically hit Feyli as well and gives Coil Bardic Inspiration. Hobson uses Thunder Step to get out of dodge, hurting Valse, Coil, and Morenthal in the process. The damage does allow Coil to snap out of the charm effect. Valse deals some extra damage to Morenthal, and Morenthal snaps out of the charm too. He runs up to stab one of the clones, destroying a clone. Coil sees Gelnek attacking Feyli, walks over, and grapples Gelnek. Miory heals Trilby again because she doesn't have many attacks. Trilby summons Scrubbins in Protector Mode. The clones attack by creating lines connecting their positions, causing several DEX saves required from the Party, but Trilby takes the brunt of it. The line hits Gelnek too, and that shocks him out of the charm effect. One clone was alone, and she shoots at Miory. The other clones break formation and begin to approach the Party. Feyli fires away at a clone while Coil lets Gelnek be free and critically smite another clone out of existence before hitting a second one. He also gives Bardic Inspiration to Trilby. Hobson discerns that using a Dispel Magic on a clone could destroy them, but he instead uses a Synaptic Static cast to destroy 2 for the price of 1. Valse fails for tree smashing on a different clone. Morenthal kills another clone as Coil spawns Goo Coil, rages, and attacks some clones in conjunction with Goo Coil. These swipes bring the clone count to 2, meaning one of them is the original. Miory asks if anyone needs healing, but nobody does, so she casts Sheleligh and bonks a clone on the head. Scrubbins grants temp HP to Coil, Morenthal, Feyli, and Trilby. Trilby is mad, firing off and hitting three times with Scorching Ray. The remaining clones shoot fire at Valse and Hobson, only hitting the latter. Feyli hits presumably the real one twice. Gelnek uses Dispel Magic on one of the clones, which fails because it was the real one all along. He then shouts at Hobson to go kill by granting Bardic Inspiration. Hobson uses said Inspiration to make one of his Blasts hit the real fiend while Valse fails miserably to hit the last clone. Fortunately, the Valse's roots cause damage to kill the clone. The last and real fiend remains for Morenthal to go in and grab the kill. With a wailing sound, the fiend dies and the Party is left back in the safe room. End of Session.
