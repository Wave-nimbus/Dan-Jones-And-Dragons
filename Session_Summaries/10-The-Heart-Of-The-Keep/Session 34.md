## Session 34: Getting to the Heart of the Matter

*Content Warning: Body Horror*
We open during the Long Rest after Morenthal stormed out and is hiding from the Party. Everyone else remains in the safe room, sleeping soundly. Coil, keeping watch, sees Miori prepare diamond dust and other spellcasting components for the day. She starts talking to Coil, asking if its common for Morenthal to be a jerk. Feyli, who is also awake, says that it does happen often. She asks if they think Morenthal saw something in the demon vision. They aren't sure, but they each saw their own thing that they don't want to talk about. Since she isn't part of the Flower Crowns, she doesn't have the same dynamic as they do with Morenthal. However, she is worried for what might be seen in the center of the Heart of the Keep. Coil stands up to go try to find Morenthal, who is still hiding in the library. Morenthal chooses not to hide as Coil approaches. Coil mentions that the ring is still available, but Morenthal brushes it off. Morenthal laments that the Heart of the Keep has not been fun, and he's been questioning everything again, especially what he's even doing these days. Coil reassures him, saying that he's earned the Party's trust. Morenthal is angry that everything that goes wrong always seems to be his fault, and Coil only says that Morenthal's been a jerk recntly. Morenthal hates this place and says that the Party shouldn't be here. Coil agrees, but who else would do it otherwise? Morenthal reiterates his desire to get out somehow, since everyone knows what will hapen when the final part is placed. Coil reminds him that the only way out is probably putting that last body part in. Morenthal feels like he's becoming more unwelcome in places, which Coil tells him to stop talking about that. Coil knows what it's like to only exist as a weapon and ask if he has a place. Yet, the Party is the closest he's ever felt to having a place. Morenthal doesn't answer, but they hear the clattering of bones as the two of them head back to the safe room. The Party completes their Long Rest.

Trilby finally opens his Bag of Many Things, pulling out a $\color{lightgreen}{\textbf{Square Topaz}}$, which has a static electricity crackle tied to it. With only 1 body part left to slot in, and every part of this floor explored, there's not much else to go. Miori walks over to Feyli and asks if she or anyone else has diamonds for spellcasting components, especially Revivify. Dan J. asks the Party who has Revivify diamonds and who has the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Orb of Recall}}$. A d20 is rolled to see if the Party bought Revivify diamonds, and Trilby & Feyli give up 300 gp each for diamonds. Coil holds the Orb of Recall. Miori used her last diamond to make diamond dust for Greater Restoration. The remaining magic items are doled out to the Party now that they are all here: Trilby takes the Bowl & Spoon of Soup, Feyli takes the Cultist Skull, and Hobson takes the Purple Void Crystal Necklace. With preparations complete, the Party heads off to the corpse to place the final body part. Miori casts Stoneskin on herself for safety. Coil lets the leg stitch itself to the body. The corpse's eyes roll into its eyes, stares at the Party, and takes a gasping breath. It stands up, and with a pained look, angles its arms towards its chest. He rips open his own rib cage with his bare hands, blood curdling screams echoing off the walls. As the chest cavity widens, so too does a doorway on the central pillar made up of that creature's flesh. With nowhere else to go but onwards, the Party enters the small space. A mechanical contraption closes the door behind them, as they ride an elevator up and through the strange magical darkness that not even Hobson's Devil Sight can pierce. A rumbling voice becomes audible, recalling the tale of a leader who followed their lord through bloodshed and destruction, hoping to find a place to call home. His lord eventually answered with a place that turned out to be a curse, and so the being fights the urge to cull his own kin and loved ones. The lord only desires for destruction and death that spreads to every corner of the globe. The being desires the release of death, the end to this place's ability to torment others. They wish the Party luck in the coming battle, yet they cannot let it be easy for the Party. A couple more minutes pass before the elevator finally stops, and a small red mote of light appears. It grows and pushes the darkness away, revealing a new room and a new figure.

The room is fairly large, ornate, and pretentious, with faceless portraits, black columns, and red carpets adorning the room. Three large windows oversee a swirling mass of purple clouds with the bay of Varu'Il visible through a gap in the clouds. A humanoid, perhaps a Lizardfolk, wearing a black suit with red trim and a frilled collar turns towards the Party, glass of red wine in hand. They raise one hand while the other drops the glass of wine. They snap, and the red mote of light detonates. Everyone takes a DEX save as *Combat Begins* and music begins to play. The Delayed Blast Fireball deals 50 Fire on a fail and 25 on a save. The Lizardfolk pulls out a massive greatsword, lights it on fire, and approaches the Party, hitting Coil with the sword and knocking Trilby prone with his tail. Gelnek goes around the side of the arena to be behind the Lizardfolk to attempt a Compelled Duel on them. The Lizardfolk uses Counterspell to stop the spell, and Gelnek gives Trilby a Bardic Inspiration. Morenthal attacks the Lizardfolk, who happens to be an undead creature, so his Purging Dagger deals extra radiant damage, which appears to make a more permanent wound. He also leaves the dagger inside the enemy, which will deal additional radiant damage each turn and create sunlight. Hobson summons Valse as a werewolf who proceeds to claw into the Lizardfolk. The Lizardfolk uses a legendary action to get away from being surrounded by Party members. Coil enters his rage and gives a look towards Feyli to unlock his muzzle, which she does. Coil rushes the Lizardfolk to start cleaving into them. Feyli activates her chalice form and hits the Lizardfolk with a Guiding Bolt. The Lizardfolk uses another legendary action to send a Fireball towards everyone. Trilby uses his spell scroll of Counterspell to dissipate it, gaining a glare. Miori heals Feyli and also drinks a healing potion for herself before spreading out. Trilby gets Scrubbins out in protector mode for most of the Party and then heals himself. The Lizardfolk yanks out the Purging Dagger before summoning two fire Mephits from obelisks in the room. Gelnek does a Dark Souls point down gesture before Commanding the Lizardfolk to grovel, but the Lizardfolk isn't affected. Gelnek then gives the weakest possible Healing Word to Coil. The Lizardfolk takes another Legendary Action to cast Mirror Image on himself. Morenthal runs to pick up the Purging Dagger and stabs one of the clones from the spell, destroying another with his off hand dagger. The Mephits go after Coil & Gelnek. Hobson manages to clear the final Mirror Image clone while werewolf Valse slashes into the Lizardfolk. The Lizardfolk bites Morenthal, dealing massive amounts of piercing and poison damage. 

Coil summons Goo Coil to assist in the destruction. Being unchained gives a second Action Surge as well, which he uses to great effect. Feyli heals Morenthal at range for a lot, even though she isn't happy about it. Miori heals Feyli again while downing her last healing potion for herself. Trilby orders Scrubbins up towards the melee combatants, while he misses a silver crossbow bolt. The Lizardfolk's arm gets covered in black ichor as a black platform forms under Hobson, dealing heavy damage and also reducing his max HP. Gelnek runs up to enter melee with the Lizardfolk and smites them for near-minimum damage. The Lizardfolk uses several legendary actions to teleport away and create a massive circle encompassing everyone other than himself, reducing saturation in the area to zero, dealing a bunch of necrotic damage. Hobson falls to the ground, causing Valse to also dissipate. A fire Mephit explodes and harms Coil. Gelnek attempts and fails to blind the Lizardfolk with Marac-Star, but the Lizardfolk is unaffected. Morenthal drinks his superior healing potion before charging the Lizardfolk. Coil continues to attack the Lizardfolk, causing him to cast shield to block one attack, and the Bardic Inspiration lets Coil pierce through. Feyli uses Healing Word on Hobson, bringing him back from unconsciousness. Miori wants to save her heals for bringing people from zero, so she casts Enlarge on Coil with a smack before running away. Coil did not appreciate said smack, so he hits at Miori as she retreats, knocking her unconscious and immediately ending the Enlarge spell. Trilby gets Scrubbins to help most of the Party and gets Miori back up. The Lizardfolk uses the same black hand attack on Morenthal, dealing massive necrotic damage and also reducing his max HP. Gelnek runs over and casts Heroism on Morenthal, Coil, and Trilby and gives Morenthal some Bardic Inspiration. The Lizardfolk's legendary action goes after Morenthal with his sword, knocking him unconscious. Hobson resummons Valse out to keep on attacking the Lizardfolk, and Coil follows suit, along with summoning Goo Coil again. Feyli heals Morenthal off of the floor, which is immediately reversed by the Lizardfolk's legendary action. However, uncanny dodge plus Coil's Shared Pain item keeps Morenthal awake. Miori heals Coil a little bit before taking a swing at the Lizardfolk. Trilby moves Scrubbins again before trying to throw holy water at the Lizardfolk but misses. The Lizardfolk uses another legendary action to sweep their tail around, knocking Miory down again. On the Lizardfolk's turn, he grabs Morenthal by the scruff of his neck, and angrily says that he might dodge the attack, but will others in the Party? A green glow appears at their fingertip, as he scans the room to see who Morenthal wants to die the least. In this moment, Morenthal wants Coil to not die the least, but the Lizardfolk fails to read that. Leaving it up to the dice, Trilby is targeted by a 6th level Disintegrate spell, and makes a DEX save. Trilby manages to dodge it, taking no damage. 

Gelnek, in a desperate movement, smacks Marac-star into the Lizardfolk's face and also adds a smite on top. He also brings Miori back from the ground, which is reversed by another legendary action tail sweep. Morenthal gets back up and stabs into the Lizardfolk, screaming in emotions. In mild confusion, the Lizardfolk detonates a Fireball on his position, giving Miori two death fails, knocking down Gelnek, and nearly killing himself. This ends the fight, as the Lizardfolk asks that they cut "it" out, quickly. The Party rushes to heal their downed members, managing to do so. Coil continues to bite into the Lizardfolk, who screams in agony. Something otherworldly is keeping them alive. Enraged and unhinged, Coil dismembers the Lizardfolk before the jaw snaps closed and Coil almost shuts down. The Lizardfolk is somehow still breathing, and his one remaining eye gives a pleading look towards Morenthal. He takes his daggers, plunges into the Lizardfolk's chest, and rips open the chest cavity, shattering the ribcage in the process. A mangled, oversized, nonsensical heart pounds within. Morenthal takes the Lizardfolk's cape as a barrier to grab the Heart of the Keep. The same sensation from the ring is felt, with agonized screams emanating from the Heart. He takes it out of the body, wraps the Heart in the cloak as Hobson opens the Bag of Holding to deposit it. Morenthal puts it inside while he looks at the Lizardfolk. The ceiling shakes and begins to collapse. The rift in the clouds overlooking Varu'Il grows larger and larger, until the Party finds themselves falling 600 ft in the air above Varu'Il. Miori turns into a Giant Eagle, and Feyli considers following suit. However, they cannot carry everyone. The Immoveable Rod would kick the can down the road, but the Orb of Recall, if everyone can reach it, is their best bet for safety. Just before they all hit the water, the Orb activates, and the Party plus Miori find themselves back in a sleek black metal room. Cylindrical canisters and Blue Void Crystal are visible along the walls. Though they haven't been there before, they all realize that they're in the basement of the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Spire}}$ in Northcliff.

A Gnome woman with an eyepatch is the first to rush over to the Party as $\color{tan}{\textbf{Kasma}}$ mentions that nobody said they were returning anytime soon. Seeing that the Party is quite injured, she also calls for some healers. Kasma makes sure that the Flower Crowns all got back safely and are accounted for. Miori undoes her wild shape and asks where they are now. Gelnek tells her that they're in a safe place as Trilby says they're in Northcliff now. Miori says that she needs to get back to Varu'Il, as the conditions for her gaining freedom involve going into the Heart of the Keep. The fact that she hasn't yet gone back to the Merchant Collective yet will be held above her, so she's a little desperate to get back. Kasma says that something can maybe be arranged on short notice with $\color{tan}{\textbf{Gregor}}$, and Miori is thankful for that. Finally, some clerics of Ophena arrive along with Adokas to talk with the Party and heal them up & remove the max HP reduction. Adokas asks if they have the artifact, and Hobson holds up the Bag of Holding for Adokas. He takes it and says that he'll give it back once the Heart of the Keep is returned to $\color{tan}{\textbf{The Vaults of Eversteel}}$. The Party can schedule a debriefing later, and payment will be made then. Miori is offered the same debrief and payment, which she accepts, but she's still stressed out. Kasma is happy and all, but she does want the Party to go home and rest. Back in the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Hall of the Flower Crowns}}$ Hall of the Flower Crowns, the Party is finally allowed to relax. Hobson finds some sheets and pillows for Miori to crash on a couch. They talk about the Merchant Collective and whether the Flower Crowns should accompany Miori. She asks if there is anything at all that the Merchants could maybe hold over the Party's head. Just meeting with them is one thing that could be held against them. Coil thinks that maybe only one or two need to be there for the meeting. Meanwhile, he wants to know what happened during the fight. Miori mentions getting attacked by Coil, to which he's horrified by. However, Coil doesn't really remember anything after the Long Rest. The Party fills him in. They resolve to worry about everything later, so they all go to take a Long Rest. As Coil walks into his room, he sees a medium humanoid figure standing in the corner. Their body is translucent except for the skull of his head. $\color{tan}{\textbf{Bartholomew}}$ has a Plasmoid body, which allows him to do crazy things with his body. He can also go up stairs, finally, so he feels great. He asks where he should hang out, since he can also compress pretty well. Coil is surprised since Bartholomew doesn't have to stay if he doesn't want to. However, Bartholomew is welcome with the Flower Crowns' house anytime. Bartholomew appreciates that and goes off to scare the others.

Sometime later, Morenthal goes to Coil to talk. Morenthal asks how Coil is doing overall, to which Coil says the last battle was really rough. Morenthal asks if Coil remembers a ring from the Heart of the Keep, which Coil only vaguely remembers. Morenthal asks if Coil remembers talking in the library, but unfortunately, Coil doesn't. Maybe the memories will come back and maybe they won't. Coil is curious what was talked about, but Morenthal brushes it off, saying it was just about how weird the Heart of the Keep was. Coil surmises that Morenthal feels sad about what memories were lost. Morenthal then asks what Coil feels like when he loses control. Coil recalls the stained glass window of him being alone and in a fury, so he's always worried about that outcome. Morenthal reassures Coil that he'll always be himself. At the very least, Coil will always find his way back to the Party. Coil then asks if Morenthal is okay, and Morenthal replies with a hug. Morenthal then leaves Coil's room. The Party is now Level 10. End of Session.
