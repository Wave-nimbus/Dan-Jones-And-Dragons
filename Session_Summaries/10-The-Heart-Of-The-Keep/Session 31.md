## Session 31: Storming the Gates

*Combat Begins* immediately, as the Flower Crowns have to fight a Zoetrian shaman and a green dragon. The shaman creates a Swarm of Insects on the Party, hurting some of them in the process. Hobson gets out of bee country and hits both the shaman and the dragon with a Synaptic Static, which causes the swarm to subside. Morenthal does something foolish or brave by critically stabbing the undead dragon with his sneak attack. Feyli misses with a Guiding Bolt and enters chalice form, Trilby misses with a Ray of Frost, and Scrubbins hits the shaman with a force ballista. Coil rages up towards the dragon and begins to carve into it, dealing less damage than expected. The dragon is not happy about that, and spews necrotic smoke that hits everyone except Hobson and Feyli. Scrubbins is destroyed by that breath attack. The dragon attempts to move and go invisible, but it's spotted by the Party and isn't able to hide. It walks over to Feyli as Gelnek does a war dance and casts Heroism on Coil & Morenthal, and he gives Feyli a bardic inspiration. The shaman causes the ground around the three of them to shake violently, creating difficult terrain and dealing a little damage. Hobson misses the shaman before walking up to the dragon. Morenthal walks up to and stabs the dragon again, and Feyli heals herself and Trilby. Trilby spawns Scrubbins again in protector form and fails to make the shaman smile. Coil runs up to slash the shaman down to size. The dragon hits Feyli & Hobson with its claws and hits Morenthal with its tail. It also failed to turn invisible again. Gelnek walks over and fails to rock the shaman with Marac-Star and heals Morenthal a little. The shaman polymorphs Coil into a slug, and Hobson whiffs his sword strike. Morenthal stabs the dragon two more times while Feyli continues to be on heal duty for Morenthal and Hobson. Scrubbins gives Trilby some temp HP while Trilby shoots a Ray of Frost through the shaman, killing her. Coil walks towards the dragon and applies his Shocking Oil -- way back from $\color{tan}{\textbf{Filgrove}}$ -- to his sword. The Dragon is furious at Morenthal and decides to focus all three of its attacks to knock him out. It then manages to turn invisible and run past Hobson. Gelnek gets Morenthal back in the fight with a Healing Word and attempts to find the dragon. Hobson uses Eldritch Blast to search the south part of the room. Morenthal stands up, and uses the Eye of the Nightmother to locate the dragon for the Party. He drinks a healing potion, and Feyli continues to heal the Party. Scrubbins gives Gelnek & Coil temp HP, and Trilby uses his Spell Refueling Ring to recover a 3rd level spell lost and heroically runs away. Coil courageously slashes into the dragon, who retaliates against Coil & Gelnek with its claws. It also bites Coil hard. Gelnek hits the dragon with Marac-Star and smites it too, and then he gives Coil bardic inspiration. Hobson hits it once with a Eldritch Blast and gives Morenthal a healing potion. Morenthal ignores the healing potion and runs up for the killing blow on the dragon. As the dragon dies, the shadows recede, and the room returns to normal lighting.

At the far south end of the room, the armored wagon with the horse golem can be seen next to a bunch of shelves. The Party takes a second to heal each other before going over to inspect the wagon. The shelves are full of various gadgets and gizmos, and the horse golem is powered by a large purple void crystal. The wagon resembles a classic locomotive. Hobson is able to detect about 5 magical items that are immediately noticable: A $\color{gray}{\textit{Lightsaber Hilt}}$, A $\color{gray}{\textit{Runic Belt}}$, a $\color{gray}{\textit{Silver Bird Pendant}}$, a $\color{gray}{\textit{Spiked Golden Bracelet}}$, and a $\color{gray}{\textit{Starry Scythe}}$. With the Zoetrians bearing down on them, the Party loads up the magic items onto the armored wagon. Coil has the command word to control the horse golem and decides to ride it. The rest of the Party climb in the back as they head towards the main elevator. They quickly comb over the basement levels for notes and information. On B2, Trilby finds notes about the dragon in the bottom of the lab and a $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Vial of Black Ichor}}$. On B1, Trilby, Hobson, & Feyli find a trio of potions: two normal Healing Potions and one Superior Healing Potion. Meanwhile, Gelnek finds two vials of burgandy neuron connections, and Feyli determines them to be two $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Vials of Dragonsbane}}$, which would be a useful poison against draconic enemies. They also divide the healing potions, giving Morenthal the superior while Hobson takes the normals. Everyone except Coil piles into the wagon in preparation for the breakaway. Coil continues to ride the horse golem, enjoying the ability to ride something for once. The armored wagon easily bursts through the lab's entrance and rides past the Zoetrian forces outside, and about 15 minutes later, they arrive back at the Maelestrom. Raya, her militia, and The Runebreakers make their way back to the tavern to rest from their own fights. With the plan to attack the tower at midnight, everyone should have time to rest up before the invasion. Raya asks about the lab, and the Party relays that all the researchers are dead and mentions the experiments. Everyone but Hobson notices that Raya knew about the experiments and isn't surprised. She doesn't know the exact nature of the experiments, but the Merchant Collective was funding them, so she couldn't do anything about it. The Party also mentions the dragon, which she is surprised by. The Zoetrians previously worshiped a green dragon named Rimereth who disappeared decades ago. She'll have words with the Merchant Collective once the Keep is gone. Since it is currently about noon, the Party has time to rest up and do any preparations before midnight.

Hobson begins to use scrolls of Identify on the five magical items from the lab. The Starry Scythe is determined to be a $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Moonscythe (+1)}}$, a martial weapon that is surprisingly not made of metal. Feyli takes it. Beyond its +1 nature, it also can be used as a spellcasting focus, and it gives an additional d4 when healing. The Lightsaber Hilt looking thing is a classic $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Immovable Rod}}$, which Hobson holds onto. Out of spell scrolls, he goes to find a magic shop that can do Identify for 100 gp per item. The Runic Belt is determined to be a $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Belt of Hill Giant Strength}}$, raising the wearer's STR score to 21. The Silver Bird Pendant is a $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Necklace of Birdsong}}$, allowing the wearer to perfectly mimic bird calls. The Spiked Golden Bracelet is a $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Bracelet of Shared Pain}}$, which lets the wearer use a Reaction when a creature takes damage within 30 ft to reduce the damage by 1d12, but the wearer takes that damage as psychic damage. The Party discusses who should get the remaining three items. Gelnek receives the Belt to boost his Marac-Star attacks. Hobson will hang onto the Necklace for now, but anyone can use it. Trilby wants to tinker with the Bracelet to maybe put it on Scrubbins, but Coil will hold onto it since he has the most HP. Gelnek orders a great feast to celebrate the killing of the dragon and to rest up for the fight ahead. During this resting time, the Runebreakers are also spread around the Maelstrom, relaxing and enjoying the free time. Eventually, Rodrick and the Water Genasi walk up to the Flower Crowns and join their feast. The Genasi is introduced by Rodrick as $\color{teal}{\textbf{Nori Watergrave}}$, but Nori doesn't say anything. Rodrick discusses the story of the Runebreakers in Varu'Il, and the attack on the Keep has stalled out due to the sheer quantity of demon monsters that pour out of the Keep every time the bridge is crossed. He's very happy that the Flower Crowns are here just to give more fighters.

Night approaches, and Raya, her soldiers, and the Runebreakers are preparing for the raid. Raya gives the Party some instructions and advice, saying that they should all stay inside the armored wagon, lest they be attacked. Once they get to the doors, they will probably close, so the Party should be prepared to jump out quickly. With all preparations complete, they ride out to where the bridge should materialize. Further, the horse golem may need to be kept on track through concentration saves. Coil has the best bonus to that, so he'll command the horse. Attacks can be shot out of the wagon, but stronger attacks should be saved for the tower. Midnight is struck, the bridge materializes, and the doors to the keep open. Silence for a second before Coil orders the horse forward. Swarms of imps and devils flood out of the castle as the city's defenses light them up. A handful of them attack the wagon, but the Party fends them off without issue. The wagon runs over a decently sized demon half-way across the bridge, and as they approach the other side, the doors begin to close. The wagon is fast but not fast enough to avoid being wedged between the doors, immediately stopping the Party in their tracks, which hurts. The horse plows on ahead into a large statue in the opening room. However, the doors are still closing on the wagon, which begins to crumple under the pressure. The Party leaps out as the wagon is split in two, and the entrance hall looms ahead as does the silence of the Heart of the Keep. The entrance hall is in pristine condition with black knight armor are spread along either side of the walls, curtains hang from enormous windows, and a set of double doors wait at the far end of the room. Thunder and lightning can be seen and heard through the windows. They can hear music playing inside. The horse golem got destroyed, but its arcane core is salvageable, so Coil picks it up.

The tiles on the floor crack as zombies climb out from their graves into the room. A pair of dire wolves also crash through the windows to approach the Party in this symphony of the night. *Combat Begins.* Gelnek makes quick work of a zombie and gives Coil a bardic inspiration. Coil follows suit and runs up to a dire wolf. The wolves surround Coil and use their pack tactics to rip into Coil. Morenthal backs up Coil and fights the dire wolves. Hobson shoots a zombie, and Feyli kills a dire wolf. Trilby blasts the other dire wolf with a Ray of Frost. The zombies don't get much done, but Gelnek makes another one bite the dust. Coil and the other dire wolf trade attacks. Morenthal and Hobson take down another zombie each. Feyli takes out the other dire wolf, while the Party mops up the last zombie no problem, with "Trilby" getting the final blow. The doors on the other side of the room open as the final enemy falls. The hallway beyond is filled with paintings and statues of distorted faces and people. There is black marble on the ground, and footsteps in the distance imply that the Party isn't alone. An opening directly ahead looks grand. To the east is an open hallway into a a library or study. The hallway has a mixture of normal sized doors and larger double doors, and the mostly have handles and/or locks. Trilby opens a door to the south, revealing a crypt-like room with 6 sarcophagi and roots hanging from the ceiling. To the south of this room is a door and 5 keyholes are in a pentagram shape next to it. Morenthal uses the Eye of the Nightmother in the crypt, and there is some kind of magical concealment going on, but the sarcophagi are blocking specific vision. Morenthal doesn't like the room, so they should be careful. It feels completely different to the rest of the Keep. Hobson notices enchantment over the coffins. Coil goes to open the door, but it has nothing to hold onto, and it's completely solid. The 5 keyholes are not pickable, so something needs to be found. The Party takes a closer look at the sarcophagi, each of which have a rune/symbol carved into them. Symbol 1 is of $\color{tan}{\textbf{Ather the Breaker}}$, Symbol 4 is of $\color{tan}{\textbf{The Nightmother}}$, and Symbol 5 is the Black Hand. The Party tries to figure out the other three, and Trilby recognizes Symbol 6 as the same from the blood mage's spellbook.

(*Feyli had to leave session early at this point.*) Based on the 4 identified, the symbols are a mix of the Divine Nine and the Fallen Gods with no discernable pattern. They can't figure out the rest of that room, so they head back north to the library room, which has books and scrolls strewn about all over the shelves and floor. Most notably is a large tome with a sapphire on its cover and a lock. It is sitting on a 6-sided pedestal with each side having a rectangular slot in it. Trilby investigates the room and notes that there are only 6 blue books in the room. He points that out to the Party, and Morenthal takes one immediately. The book has gold lettering saying "Ritual Magic and Flora" on it. As it's pulled, a stony hand appears from where the book was pulled before stopping. Morenthal looks away for a second and when he looks back, there is a second hand, and the first hand got closer. The Party realizes what kind of situation it is, so they bunch up to keep an eye on the hands. Morenthal notices that the book would fit into a slot, and he opens it. The book is filled with gibberish, and once he closes it, only the word "and" is left behind, with "Ritual Magic" and "Flora" staying on the ground in whole words.. They all realize that the books will spell a sentence. Morenthal pulls the next book: "Finding Combat in Exotic Alchemy." He shakes the book, leaving "Finding in" behind, leaving "Combat", "Exotic", and "Alchemy" on the ground. The stone hands will approach as long as nobody is watching. Book 3 says "Sword and Deft Hands," which splits into "Sword" & "Deft Hands" onto the ground, with "and" remaining on the book. The other three books are collected, and the Party begins to solve the puzzle. There are also pictures on each book cover, implying that the words need to be rearranged to fit the pictures. Hobson and Feyli were on hand watching duty, but Feyli's eyes start to water, so a face becomes visible out of one of the holes. Other Party members allow more creatures through as the book puzzles continue to be solved. A few seconds later, the books have words and Morenthal begins to slot them into place, narrowly avoiding the strange statue as he and Trilby try to keep things covered. He's a bit slow, so the creatures continue to advance. One of them touches Gelnek, petrifying him instantly. Morenthal hurries to get the final few books in, and when the final one is place, all the creatures disintegrate into stone, and Gelnek unfreezes. Morenthal goes over and opens the large book. It has a large space inside of it, and there's a rotting head in it, severed cleanly at the neck. Nothing else is written in the book, and Morenthal attempts to get the sapphire out of the book's cover.

Gelnek picks up the severed head to hang onto it in case it becomes useful later. The library is available for things to research, especially for the symbols. Hobson determines the symbols in the sarcophagus room are in order: Ather the Breaker, The $\color{teal}{\textbf{Seeping Rot}}$, $\color{teal}{\textbf{Blinding Light}}$, The Nightmother, The Black Hand, and the $\color{tan}{\textbf{Scarlet Harvest}}$. They try to find differences in anything. Ather's domain is war, combat and honor; The Seeping Rot's domain is decay, disease, and rot; The Blinding Light's domain is light, perfection, and absolutism; The Nightmother's domain is the moon, death, and anti-undeath; The Black hand's domain is of death and necromancy; The Scarlet Harvest's domain is brutality, bloodlust, and war. So Blinding Light appears to be the only one that isn't about death. Gelnek claims dibs on opening the Ather coffin, which has a single scarab beetle that flies into a keyhole. He then opens the Scarlet Harvest sarcophagus for another scarab beetle. In preparation for something going wrong, Hobson uses his Immovable Rod to keep one of the doors open. Morenthal opens the Nightmother's for a third scarab. Hobson opens the Black Hand for a fourth scarab. Coil opens the Seeping Rot for the fifth and final scarab. The stone to the south descends into the floor, leading to a short underground tunnel. A small boat, large enough to fit the Party, floats on a small river. The Party backtracks to the east hallway and finds a locked door. Morenthal unlocks the door, opening it to reveal an impaled body in a cage. It is filled with spikes, and the body has its head. At the end of the hallway is a painting of a human woman with a blue earring and a cloth wrapped around her head. Notably, her face is clear and not distorted. The room is revealed to have a simple room. It feels safe in here. There's a simple carpet and a purple flame in the center of the room. The paintings on the walls all have normal faces, and there are doors to the north and south. No distant footprints are audible in here. The south door leads back to the crypt, and the north door leads into the library. The doors can be locked from inside this room, making this room quite safe. The Party heads back to the main hall, but Morenthal passes another locked door. Unlocking and opening it reveals a half dozen dead bodies, none of which are missing heads.

Heading off to the West side of the castle, Morenthal unlocks another set of bodies in the closet. Trilby notices that the bodies appear to be from different places, as some of them are tieflings. Coil finds the first major room to the Northwest. The temperature is quite warm, the floor is a reddish sand, brasures are lit in the corners of the room while the walls are lined with spikes. Crowd noise is audible from the room. The South has a set of double doors, and teh west end of the hallway has two more doors. Trilby tries the south room, which opens into a large room divided by iron gates, which create a winding path. At the end of each gate is a set of spiked walls, retracting into and out of the floor with a certain rhythm. There is a stonework fiend at the end of each segment with eyes that switch between white and gray, and bronze platforms are in the center of each segment. All the way on the other end of the room is a small end table with stuff and a rug. Coil manifests Goo Coil onto one of the platforms and nothing happens. Goo Coil steps onto the checkered tile floor as the fiend's eyes mismatch the ground. The statue spews fire and destroys Goo Coil. Morenthal steps up to the plate to deal with 7 DEX saves in a row. Trilby uses Enhance Ability to give Morenthal advantage on the saves. Morenthal gets 5 saves in a row before he stumbles and triggers all the traps. There were pressure plates that caused the copper dias to electrify nearby tiles. He makes it to the desk at the end of the gauntlet, and there only appears to be a single spell scroll there. It appears that the wall behind the desk is discolored, and Morenthal finds a lever in the desk that opens the false wall. Beyond is a treasure room with jewels, gold, chests, a suit of armor, and magic items. A corridor leads north, and a painting illusion opens into a safe room. He also opens the door back to the west hallway. The Party congregates in the west safe room.

The Party collectively go to the treasure room, where Hobson determines that the suit of armor and one of the chests are magical. Trilby figures out that the suit of armor is known as $\color{forestgreen}{\textbf{Ironbark Armor}}$, a reinforced wood made by druids, serving as heavy armor that isn't made of metal. The armor also appears to have runes carved in it. The remaining treasure appears to be normal. Dan J. has Feyli lay claim to the armor. Morenthal then goes to open the chest, which explodes into Fireball, burning the whole Party. Except Morenthal. The chest has a few magical items inside. A $\color{gray}{\textit{Spoon and Bowl}}$, a $\color{gray}{\textit{Red Shortsword}}$, a $\color{gray}{\textit{Lotus Engraved Skull}}$, a $\color{gray}{\textit{Golden Amulet}}$, and a $\color{gray}{\textit{Purple Void crystal Necklace}}$. Hobson scoops the rest of the treasure into his Bag of Holding, totalling about 1750 gp, and the Party heads back to the West safe room for a Short Rest. As they all relax, they begin to feel really relaxed. Hobson and Trilby fall asleep and begin to have pleasant dreams. Hobson dreams of his family's inn as the lunch rush is about to begin. Trilby is back at home eating dinner with his family as his father walks in without his limp. Thus the captain of the Crown's Guard is reinstated. The rest of the Party doesn't fall asleep, but they are sufficiently relaxed. Eventually, though, the room suddently changes color from purple to red, and everyone wakes up. There is now a figure in the room with them. She is a female Human with short strawberry blonde hair and a staff with a geode attatched to the top of it. She says that the Party should now be safe, and tells them to wake up Trilby and Hobson immediately. The two of them feel even more tired than before they rested. The woman assumes that if the Party is here then they should stick together. Her name is $\color{teal}{\textbf{Miory}}$ and they have a lot to talk about. End of Session.
