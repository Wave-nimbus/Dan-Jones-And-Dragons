## Session 45: The Throne of Want

We start with the Party heading back towards the only room with a locked door. They choose to start with combat and use their gold key on the door in the top row, second column. Inside is a massive library with bookshelves rising into the ceiling 60 ft above, but they go higher into a void. On the floor is a series of oversized books with runes of nonsense. In the center is a large draconic skull, and Hobson is detecting magic from the books. Conjuration and Enchantment ping from the skull and books, respectively. Hobson doesn't trust anything, so he goes right up and pokes the skull with Mage Hand. *Combat Begins.* The dragon's eyes glow with purple fire and begins to rise into the sky. Vertibre made of purple fire extends to over 100 ft, with its head coming to a rest 40 ft in the air. Coil starts of with a crossbow bolt, and at the start of the dragon's turn, the Party is forced to take a WIS save, becoming Frightened if they fail. The dragon head moves around, comes closer to the ground, and uses its breath attack of magic and books on the entire Party again. Gelnek casts Bless on the entire Party, which will help a lot until he loses concentration. He then uses his Goblin feature to hide. A frightened Morenthal shoots his bow before running around a bookshelf to hide. Hobson uses Synaptic Static on the dragon, but it still saves. Unfortunately, the dragon also appears to be immune to Psychic damage. Feyli casts Wall of Fire, which barely clips the dragon, and also shoots her archer form, but it misses. She also breaks out of her frightened status. Trilby summons Scrubbins in Protector mode to keep the Party alive. Coil runs up to a second floor to shoot more crossbow bolts and rage. The dragon flies over the Wall of Fire to attack Trilby and Feyli. Gelnek summons his Spiritual Weapon right next to the dragon, taking the form of a fly swatter. He also prepares the Dodge Action. Morenthal steps from his hiding spot to shoot the dragon head, before he runs to hide further. Hobson shoots once with Eldritch Blast, and the dragon teleports to the book in front of Coil. Hobson calls out that they might need to destroy the books.

Feyli creates an Insect Plague next to the dragon and misses an archer shot. Scrubbins gives more temp HP while Trilby hits a book. He hits, but the book seems completely fine and also retaliates with Psychic damage. Coil summons Goo Coil to get flanking on the dragon. He gets several hits between him and his clone. The dragon forces bookshelves to drop books on the Party for a lot of damage. Gelnek, knowing that everyone needs healing, casts Mass Healing Word. Morenthal, shoots a book from a distance with his bow, but it also retaliates. He hides but remains scared of the dragon. Hobson hits his Eldritch Blasts this time and tries to hide again. *???* Feyli shoots a Guiding Bolt, and the dragon moves to a different book once again. Trilby stands up, moves to Gelnek, and brings Scrubbins over for protection. Coil runs closer and teleports Goo Coil over to hit the dragon head. Unfortunately, the dragon then flies over the bookshelves and uses its breath weapon again on almost the entire Party, but everyone except Scrubbins saves. Gelnek misses with his spiritual weapon and blasts it with a Guiding Bolt. Morenthal steps forward to shoot an arrow, which manages to pierce the skull and kill the dragon, ending the fight. A large black onyx crystal rolls out of the dragon skull's mouth, which is picked up by Feyli. There isn't a key, but the letter in the room is an 'O'. There's a door to the South, which gets to be opened by the Feyli key.

Inside is a basic bedroom, similar to a nice inn room. To most of the Party, this seems normal. Feyli recognizes that this is the room where she first commited murder. The person was considered a threat by the Midnight Lotus. A flash of the memory passes her vision, as a nobleman lies face up in a pool of blood. The memory passes, and a pamphlet for the inn sits on a table. A blackberry pie is next to it. The pamphlet mentions *???* that the pie is for her, as someone else ate the rest. Feyli, a little distraught, picks up the pie and eats it. The drawer to the nightstand has a 4-digit combination lock. She looks around the room, noticing the other furnishings, but nothing stands out as a numbers. She hands the pamphlet to Coil and sits on the floor. Coil comforts her and gives the note to the rest of the Party so they can help. They realize that the note has the solution: 4218. Inside is an iron key, which can fit the door to the East. Trilby also finds the letter 'R' in the window's curtains. Feyli goes up to the bed, takes a flower out of her hair, places it on the bed, and mouths a "sorry." They use the iron key on the door.

Inside is a campsite along a trail. It feels similar to something they made along a road near Filgrove in their earliest adventuring days. Filgrove can be seen in the distance, and a peaceful night sky is above. The door to the North has five large empty slots in it, each of which is about fist-sized. After a minute or two, Miori, Oro, and Otto get teleported into the room, but they're all asleep. Everyone silently agrees to sleep the night away and take a Long Rest. As Trilby wakes up, a 'D', which gives the clue for the stone order for the final door: Azure, Olive, Mauve, Ebony, Ivory from top to bottom. They have 6 stones, but they put in the Blue, Green, Purple, Black, and White gems they got from bosses along the way. They hold onto the Red gem. Miori wakes up and joins the Party for what feels like the final confrontation. The stone door parts ways, revealing a blinding lgiht they can step into. Inside is a large throne room with water trickling down from the ceiling. Large fires sit on the edges of the room, and a huge pile of gemstones rest near the Throne. The Throne is black and made of metal & stone with red tendrils pulsing. They restrain the Kua Toa brother named Ox, who appears to be unconscious. After the Party enters, Ox's head looks up, as if puppeted. He reprimands the Party for letting the brothers back in and causing a rukus in the mansion. He says they can leave and he'll grant mercy. The Party says the brothers want him to be safe and for him to leave. Ox says that he'll leave when he wants to. He stands up, hundreds of veins connect him to the chair. In a flash, the Throne of Want speaks into the Party's mind. It says that some people who sit on the throne have simple wishes, but others are consumed by greed. Greed that it feeds upon. Ox is a blessing to the Throne, as he continually wants more and more. With such power, the Throne will show the Party what this power has done. The Throne itself stands up as it animates, connected by more red and black tendrils. 

*Combat Begins.* Morenthal rushes the Throne and would have gotten a brilliant stab, except for the Shield cast byt the Throne. As a Legendary Action, Ox says he wants the entire Party to "drop dead." Morenthal wasn't hit by the effect, and Gelnek saves with the help of Trilby. Everyone else fails the CON save, taking massive Necrotic damage. The Throne then smacks Morenthal several times. Trilby summons Scrubbins in Protector mode again and moves up, and Coil rages and rushes for attacks but misses. Feyli also approaches, enters Archer form, and gives some healing to Morenthal. Miori also gets closer, gives healing to Morenthal, and Wildshapes into a panther. The Throne takes another LA to swipe at Morenthal. Hobson gets closer and readies a Command spell for if Ox tries to make another wish. Gelnek once again casts Bless on the entire Party (minus Miori) while getting closer to the Throne. Morenthal gets a pair of stabs into the Throne. It then disengages & moves to the center of the room as an LA. On its turn, it forces almost the entire Party to make STR saves. On a fail, they fall prone and take a bunch of Thunder damage, taking half on save. Scrubbins slowly approaches the Party while Trilby blasts the Throne with a Thunderwave. Coil catches up to the Throne and continues to cleave into it. Feyli stands up, disengages, backs away from the Throne, and heals herself. Miori drops her Wildshape to heal Feyli. Hobson summons werewolf Valse behind the Throne to attack it. By now, the Throne looks pretty damaged, and it attacks Valse with an LA. Gelnek runs forward and uses a Channel Divinity to heal much of the Party, and he additionally does a Mass Healing Word to heal folks. Morenthal gets one big stab into the Throne, revealing most of its organs and fleshy bits. It throws Ox away, and it reorganizes the veins to reveal a central eye. Ox is unconscious and bleeding out.

The Throne runs over to where it threw Ox, provoking several opportunity attacks, and then blasts Morenthal, Valse, Coil, and Gelnek with an eye blast. Valse and Coil fail, and they all take a lot of fire. Trilby blasts another Thunderwave at the Throne, and uses the Trilbee on Ox. Coil summons Goo Coil once again to lay into the Throne, including with his Action Surge. As an LA, the Throne shoots a ray of light at Morenthal for a big hit. Feyli switches to Chalice form and misses a Guiding Bolt. The Throne does the same ray at Gelnek for more big damage, which Coil reduces slightly. Miori runs up to heal Gelnek with Healing Word before attacking with her crystal hand spell. Ox stands up to run away and cower. Hobson misses twice and gets blasted by the Throne in retaliation as an LA. Gelnek decides it is now time to use Divine Intervention. It fails. He then creates a Spiritual Weapon that resembles Marakstar. Morenthal gets a pair of stabs, and the Throne attacks the people surrounding him. Trilby carefully hits the Throne with a Shatter. It reforms itself to have much higher ranged on its melee attacks and gets some revenge on Trilby as an LA. Coil uses Second Wind and keeps slicing the throne to bits. It takes another LA to fire its eye ray at Gelnek, partially shared by Coil again. Feyli uses Mass Cure Wounds and moves closer to give more healing to Gelnek via Chalice. As a LA, the Throne shoots at Feyli in anger. Miori skirts around the Throen to give Coil a Cure Wounds. Hobson brings another Valse onto the field, as Gelnek gets back into the damaging mood, hitting with his Spiritual Weapon. The Throne uses an LA to get space before blasting almost everyone with another fiery eye laser. Scrubbins gives temp HP to most people in the eye blast, as Trilby hits the Throne with a Fireball. The Throne finally gets to show off its Legendary Resistances, though.

Coil brings Goo Coil yet again and together they keep tearing apart the Throne. It retaliates with another tenticle smash on Coil as an LA. Feyli runs up to give Cure Wounds and Chalice form on Coil. The Throne continues to be very mad at healers, and it eye blasts Feyli, finally knocking somebody down. Miori fixes that immediately by healing Feyli, syaing "stay with me." Hobson keeps Eldritch Blasting and gets Valse to attack the Throne. Yet another eyebeam retaliates to Hobson as an LA. Gelnek gets in close to cast Mass Cure Wounds on almost everyone. As Gelnek tries to run back to safety, he gets hit by an opportunity attack by the Throne. The Spiritual Weapon gets a bonk in. Morenthal gets some stabs in, just as the Throne blasts the people nearby plus Hobson with another eye beam blast. Miori and Hobson go down due to the blast which also removes Valse from the battlefield. Trilby runs up to give Miori a Cure Wounds bonbon and Scrubbins gives a bunch of temp HP to everyone. Trilby takes an eye blast for his trouble. Coil finally gets the kill with a final desperate stab of his greatsword. As it dies, the water and fires shake and become more intense. As the sword is removed, a small white cube of light falls from the remains. Trilby quickly gets Hobson back on his feet too. They walk over, and Hobson opens the Bag of Holding to pick up the cube. His instinctual Detect Magic is an absolutely pure cube of Conjuration Magic. He doesn't think it's part of an Artifact from the Vaults. Valse gasps in Hobson's mind, saying the cube is a concentrated cube of desire. Touching it won't do anything, but consuming it could give someone whatever they desire. Hobson remains very careful, even though he can tell Valse is serious about the activation. Once the cube is in the Bag, Hobson relays the information to the Party. *???* The Party starts to think about how greed can be used on the cube to twist everything.

They then remember the giant treasure pile behind the Throne. Hobson notices several magic items: 2 Potions, a Longsword, a small wand, a single Arrow, a small purple stone, a cape, a circlet, and a scroll with the the symbol of the Wellmore keep on it. Coil walks over to Ox, who is sniviling and clearly malnourished. He asks why the Party helped, since he did so many bad things. He knew what he was doing, but he still did it. He also was the cause for the max HP reduction, so that effect is removed. Coil gives Ox a Potion of Greater Healing. Around this time, the light of the door into the throne room dissipates, and Oro & Otto rush in to reunite with Ox. They are appreciative that Ox is still alive. Hobson brings the magic items to the Party's attention, and Trilby sits down to cast Identify on everything. One potion is a Potion of Supreme Healing (10d4+10), and the other is a Potion of Flying. Gelnek attempts to say they shouldn't take the gold, but the Party shuts that idea down. They use Hobson's Bag of Holding to carry all the gold. The wand is a **Wands of Secrets**, which is capable of locating secret doors. Feyli takes out Pocket to choose a thing, and she chooses a golden spork. The sword is a **Good Aligned +2 Longsword**, which requires a Good aligned creature to attune to it. The arrow is an **Arrow of Humanoid Slaying**, which deals massive additional damage on humanoid creatures. The purple stone is a **Zimera Stone of Fortitude**, granting a +2 to CON saves while in use. The cape is the **Cape of the Mountback**, which allows a cast of Dimension Door once per day. The circlet is a **Circlet of Blasting**, which allows the wearer to cast Scorching Ray once per day. The piece of paper is easily opened. It appears to be the deed to the Keep, which is currently signed by Otto, Oro, and Ox. This means that they could kill the brothers and take the keep. The loot and treasure is valued at 13,461 gp which is 1923 gp per person, including Miori. The Arrow is given to Morenthal, and the Party discuss who the Cape could go to. They also aren't entirely sure who qualifies as "Good," but they think Coil, Gelnek, Hobson, and Trilby are "Good." End of Session. The Party levels up twice to Level 12!
